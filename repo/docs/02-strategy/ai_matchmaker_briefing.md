## Who to introduce me to (the bullseye)

The sweet spot is high on BOTH axes:
- **Trust (sameness):** resonates with uniqueness / zone-of-genius / conscious-
  business ideas; ideally Russian-speaking (a strong trust lever, not required).
- **The gap (complementarity):** a grounded operator or founder, with means and a

pausing, "figuring out what's next"; interests in entrepreneurship, product, zone of
genius, conscious business. **Do not try to assess readiness or inner state, I screen

## Also valuable to meet (different angle per stakeholder)

## How to frame ANY intro (the rules)

1. **Recognize, don't pitch.** Name accurately where the person stands; let them feel
   seen. Never manufacture desire.
2. **Don't lead with the paid offer.** Let the conversation earn the next step. (The
   instinct to avoid leading with the paid studio is correct.)
3. **Name concrete mutual value**, both sides.
4. **Match the angle to the stakeholder** (client vs host vs investor vs peer), above.
5. **Sovereign tone:** no hype, no neediness, no "guru." Plain words.

## What NOT to do

- Don't call me a coach, an accelerator, or a fund.
- Don't imply someone is already a fit for the paid studio; let it earn.
- Don't lead with money, mine or theirs.
- Don't match on inner traits you can't observe ("ready," "wobbly," "ripe"); surface
  on observable signals, I do the fit-screen.
