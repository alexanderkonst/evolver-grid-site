## Deliberately not reinstated

- Vocabulary as proof of an inner faculty, or missing vocabulary as proof of incapacity. Search evidence cannot establish these claims.
- Hard requirements for a visible ending, self-employment or apparent means. Current desire and budget remain matters to establish, not infer from status.
- Automatic practitioner/non-buyer routing, “Finished Cathedral” exclusion, or rejection of all assessment businesses. These conflict with the current founder-serving audience and overlapping relationship routes.
- Mandatory quiz-first introductions, invented event availability, and earlier revenue/timing promises or commercial ladders. The current approved invitation and explicit agreement govern.
- Twenty names as a target, or more volume as a substitute for evidence. Boardy's six-to-eight default remains.
- A long biography or every historical rationale inside the sendable brief. Keep provenance here; use the brief to guide behavior.

## Structure and verification

Retained the current sequence: description and mechanism → audience → observable openings → resonance → relationship → commercial hypothesis → search and candidate card → invitation → learning. The recognition table sits beside openings; practical search details sit beside search; timing and feedback sit beside learning. This avoids appending a competing historical brief.

The sendable remains standalone. The sync script projects it into the commercial tool's stored brief; it does **not** migrate legacy scoring, exclusions, routing or message templates. Those behaviors remain a separate implementation issue.
