# Holomap Builder

A holomap is a taxonomy (x-axis) crossed with evolutionary stages (y-axis). It's a perception instrument — seeing the map activates what it describes. This skill is the method for building one from scratch, for any phenomenon that grows: a skill, a relationship, a technology, a business, a practice.

## When to use this skill

- Sasha asks to map how something develops over time or over mastery
- A phenomenon needs a diagnostic (where is this person/thing/team right now, and what's next)
- Building a new entry for `docs/holomaps/` or a sibling instrument to the Morphogenetic Holomap

## The five phases

### Phase 0 — ESC

Name the phenomenon's Essence, Significance, Consequence:

- **Essence** — what it IS at bottom. One sentence. Do not proceed until you have it.
- **Significance** — why it matters. What changes once this is understood.
- **Consequence** — what follows practically, including the nth-degree ripple into lived 3D experience.

If you can't compress Essence to one sentence, the phenomenon isn't understood yet. Go back and cut, don't pad.

### Phase 1 — The seven-stage octave

Build 7 stages, Do to Ti (the base octave, before crystallization). For each stage:

1. **One center** — the single thing organizing experience at that stage. Not a list of traits. One thing.
2. **Phenomenological voice** — write it as the person's own inner narration. "This is what I do." "I don't know, something's just off." Not third-person theory ("subjects at this stage exhibit..."). If a stage description can't be said aloud by someone living it, rewrite it.
3. **The phase shift** — between every pair of stages, name explicitly what flips, why it's a discontinuity (not a gradient), and what it costs to cross. A phase shift is not "a bit more of the same." It's water becoming steam.
4. **The two shocks** — Gurdjieff's Law of Seven, the reason octaves stall without outside help:
   - **Mi-Fa shock** (usually stage 3→4): an outside force is required to cross it. Most people stall here for years. This is where 3-4 sits on the money holomap (Accumulative → Strategic) and where the collaboration spectrum names Exchanges → Collabs.
   - **Si-Do shock** (stage 6→7): crystallization. The seeing becomes form.

### Phase 2 — The 3×3 taxonomy

Open the phenomenon into 3 key aspects — a trinity, MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive). Then open each of the 3 into 3 sub-aspects. Nine total.

**MECE test:** do any two sub-aspects overlap? Cut or merge. Is there something essential about the phenomenon that falls outside all nine? Add a cell, don't force it into the nearest one.

Precedent in the corpus: the Money Holomap's 8-column taxonomy (Creation, Flow, Storage, Exchange, Price/Value, Debt/Trust, Power, Meaning) and the CollabTech Holomap's 8 layers (Identity & Access → Interfaces & Bridges) show the shape — name the real joints of the thing, not a generic checklist.

### Phase 3 — The grid

Run all 7 stages across each of the 9 aspects: 63 cells. Each cell is a short concrete phrase, not a paragraph. Each one has to be true to that aspect at that stage — not a template filled in on autopilot.

Precedent: the Money Holomap's cell entries ("The hoarded Dollar does not work... The Dollar you spend is the only one you really have" — Flow × Stage 5) show the density: a real quote or a real diagnostic sentence, not filler.

### Phase 4 — The register shift (stages 8-10)

Stage 8 is not "more of stage 7." It's a change of register: the person stops experiencing the phenomenon as something happening TO them and starts relating to it AS a medium — something they work through, not against.

Write stage 8 carefully. It reveals what 9 and 10 are (they're usually: 8 = medium, 9 = the medium teaching others / propagating, 10 = the medium indistinguishable from the person — full symbiosis). Stop at 10.

Result: a **10×10 holomap** — 9 aspects + the phenomenon itself, across 10 stages, 70 (or 100, if you cross-tabulate the "itself" row too) meaningful cells.

## Quality gates — check before calling it done

- [ ] **MECE check** — the 3 aspects and their 9 sub-aspects don't overlap; nothing essential about the phenomenon falls outside them.
- [ ] **Voice check** — every stage description could be said out loud by a real person living in that stage.
- [ ] **Discontinuity check** — each phase shift is a genuine flip (a threshold), not a slider you could dial up gradually.
- [ ] **Retrodiction check** — run 5-7 real known cases through the map. If the map can't place or explain them, the map is wrong. Don't bend the cases to fit; fix the map.
- [ ] **Timestamp** — date every version at creation, per the project's Timestamp Law (dataset began Day 130, July 20, 2026). A holomap without a date is an unfalsifiable claim.

## Optional deepening — the 27-perspective pass

Any holomap can take a further Si-Do shock by running the 27-perspective instrument across it: 12 perspectives from inside the phenomenon (4 quadrants × 3 depths), 12 from outside it (the same 12 at the next octave up), the 13th holding all 12 at once (the center), and the 27th crystallizing the whole thing into one action. See `.agent/skills/holonic-seeing/SKILL.md` for the full mechanics. Use this when a 10×10 map is built and true but still feels like theory rather than an instrument you'd act from.
