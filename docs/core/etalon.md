## The invariant (Sasha, 2026-11-27 13:18 MST, verbatim)
> «Инвариант всей системы: возвращение к эталону самости чего бы то ни было.»
> «Самоотношение - это то, как человек (как частный случай систем) двигается к эталону. Своему эталону, который по образу и подобию всеобщего эталона Вселенной.»

Plain English: every part of the system exists to help something return to the etalon of its own selfhood.

## Three words
- **Etalon**: the true measure of a thing's own selfhood. Not a norm imposed from outside; each system has its own.
- **Deviation**: how far the thing has drifted from its etalon.
- **Return**: self-relation in motion; the act of comparing with one's etalon and moving back toward it. A shift (сдвиг) is one step of return.

## Traceability chain (from metrology)
Every measurement compares a thing with an etalon; etalons nest, and every working ruler traces to the primary.
Universal etalon (E-top) → the person's etalon (E0) → Sasha's canon (E1) → specs and templates (E2) → tests (E3) → the live screen (E4).

1. An etalon cannot be checked against itself: agents never give final sign-off on each other; the independent check at the top is Sasha and live first-timers.

## E0 measure: return, measured (Sasha, 2026-11-27)
Found in practice, not designed: Sasha's client programs already measure return to the person's own etalon.

1. **Own goals, own words.** At the first meeting the person names what they want; each goal is kept in their exact words.
2. **Own scale.** The person rates each goal 0-10 themselves. The etalon is theirs; we never rate for them.
3. **Three points.** Start (first meeting), middle, end. A baseline rated in hindsight at the end is weaker; take it live at the start.
4. **Result in one number.** Average across goals, before and after, plus the change in %. First case (private, one client, 2 months): average 2.5 → 5.3, +113%; biggest move in uniqueness (~0-1 → ~6). Limits: one person, self-report, baseline rated in hindsight.

**The scale is asymptotic (Sasha, 2026-11-27 17:59).** The etalon is approached, never reached. At 9 the scale opens: 9 of 10 becomes 90 of 100, then 990 of 1000, then 9990 of 10000. Each opening zooms into the remaining gap, so the last point is again a whole road. Reading: above 9 the honest number is how many "nines" are held (like 90%, 99%, 99.9% reliability), i.e. the distance to the etalon on a log scale. Consequences: (a) % change is fair inside one decade (0-9); across an opening, compare the gap left, not the score; (b) "10" never appears, so no one is ever done, only more precise; (c) growth near the top looks small in points and is huge in precision.

**Two metrology technologies (Sasha, 2026-11-27 18:12).** (1) *Shift measurement*: every shift product (a program, a course, a session) is measured before, middle and after on the person's own goals and scale. (2) *The unfolding asymptotic scale*: an analog approach to the etalon that unfolds digitally, decade by decade (9 → 90/100 → 990/1000 → ...). The first says how far a person moved; the second keeps the ruler honest however close they get. Sasha's programs ("Transition", then "Vector") are where (1) is applied.
