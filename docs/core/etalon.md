## The invariant (Sasha, 2026-11-27 13:18 MST, verbatim)
> «Инвариант всей системы: возвращение к эталону самости чего бы то ни было.»
> «Самоотношение - это то, как человек (как частный случай систем) двигается к эталону. Своему эталону, который по образу и подобию всеобщего эталона Вселенной.»

Plain English: every part of the system exists to help something return to the etalon of its own selfhood.

## Three words
- **Etalon**: the true measure of a thing's own selfhood. Not a norm imposed from outside; each system has its own.
- **Deviation**: how far the thing has drifted from its etalon.
- **Return**: self-relation in motion; the act of comparing with one's etalon and moving back toward it. A shift (сдвиг) is one step of return.

## Traceability chain (from metrology)
Every measurement compares a thing with an etalon; etalons nest, and every working ruler traces to the primary.
Universal etalon (E-top) → the person's etalon (E0) → Sasha's canon (E1) → specs and templates (E2) → tests (E3) → the live screen (E4).
Lessons we adopt (source: `docs/02-strategy/research/qa/exemplars/metrology.md`, template `docs/templates/calibration.md`):
1. An etalon cannot be checked against itself: agents never give final sign-off on each other; the independent check at the top is Sasha and live first-timers.
2. Check the checkers: the instrument itself can be off; a disagreeing check is never dismissed.
3. A failed etalon recalls everything it passed.
4. Etalons change rarely, on evidence, in versions, only through Sasha.
5. The kilogram stopped being a bar in a vault and was tied to a constant of nature (2019). Same move here: the etalon of selfhood is made in the image of the universal etalon.
