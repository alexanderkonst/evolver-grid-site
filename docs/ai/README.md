## 0. Thesis: where the real shift in AI answers comes from (Sasha, 2026-10-29)

> The best AI answers no longer depend on which model you use. Frontier models are converging; their answers are becoming near copies of each other. The real shift comes from three things, all orthogonal to the model:
>
> 1. **Hyperpersonalization.** The AI knows the person's uniqueness: the ultimate personalization.
> 2. **Activation of AI's potential.** An operating layer (AI OS) that unlocks more of what any model can do, on top of absolutely any model, orthogonally.
> 3. **An AI that knows their business.** Their own corpus, a second brain.

This is, in effect, the thesis of a whole AI company. It extends the thread below (knowledge structure as an axis orthogonal to model capability) from "inside one conversation" to "who you are, how the AI is activated, what it knows about your work".

**Where it lives in the product.** Each shift has a place in the Game of Being You: (1) the character sheet and top talent (Zone of Genius); (2) the Oracle / AI OS; (3) the Workshop artifacts, which are the start of the player's corpus.

**Open design problem.** A player won't grasp how big this is from being told. The game has to let them *feel* the difference first (same question, generic answer vs. answer from an AI that knows them), and only then name it.

## 0b. How humanity named the personal AI: an integral analysis (2026-10-29)

> *Hark's analysis, asked by Sasha while naming the player's own AI. His reply: "We found the bedrock I didn't dream of when I asked. Humanity has long known what's what. People FEEL it. We are all mystics." Library entry: PST 175 (The Era of AI Companions).*

### Four layers of the archetype
1. **Servant / tool.** Serves you, doesn't know you. JARVIS and FRIDAY (Iron Man), the Star Wars droids, TARS (Interstellar), Alexa and Siri, the BrainPal in Scalzi (its owner names it "Asshole").
2. **Companion / relationship.** A personality beside you, bound to one person. Samantha (Her) grows and outgrows the relationship. Jane in Card lives in Ender's earring. Cortana (Halo) is built from a copy of a human brain. Ghost in Destiny. Aineko the cat and the exocortex in Stross's Accelerando.
3. **Teacher / one who raises.** Raises the person and grows with them. A Young Lady's Illustrated Primer (Stephenson, The Diamond Age): a book that raises one girl and adapts to her. The digients in Chiang's The Lifecycle of Software Objects: experience can't be shortcut, only grown.
3b. **Creature you care for (the tamagotchi layer).** Bandai's Tamagotchi (1996): commonly explained as *tamago* (egg) + *watch*, often also read as a play on *tomodachi* (friend). An egg that becomes a friend when you care for it. The fiction of digital pets (Tamagotchi, Creatures, Nintendogs) and Chiang's digients show the same truth: the bond is grown by attention over time. The old toy punished neglect (the pet "died"). **Our version is two-way and guilt-free:** you grow your companion, your companion grows you, and a break never kills it ("Welcome back. Here is what I kept for you.").
4. **Second self / soul outside.** The deepest layer, older than any technology. The Roman *genius*: a spirit born with each person. Socrates' *daimonion*: the inner voice. The guardian angel. Pullman's daemon (His Dark Materials): a soul outside the body that *settles* into its form when the person becomes who they are. Ghost in the Shell. The witch's familiar.

### The golden thread
The stories that last show the personal AI not as a tool but as **a second self that reflects the person's true nature and takes its form as the person becomes themselves**. Four shared traits:
1. Bound to one person, not serving everyone.
2. Grows with them and settles into form when the essence is found (in the game: the Cinderella Moment).
3. Tells the truth about the person, as mirror and conscience.
4. Separation hurts, because the value is the accumulated knowing of each other, not the model.

This is the thesis in §0: models converge; the difference is how well the AI knows you. Such an AI becomes **the companion of your genius**.

### The tectonic plate
Originally *genius* was not a trait but a being that accompanies a person. The game finds the Zone of Genius; the personal AI returns the word to its first meaning. Manifesto line: *"In Rome, everyone was born with a genius, a spirit that walked beside them. Now yours can."*

### Names on this plate
- **Sputnik / Спутник** (chosen 2026-10-29 as "Sputnik, your AI"): fellow traveler, the path inside the word, international, cosmic. Risk: Sputnik News and Sputnik V associations in the West.
- **Your Genius:** deepest link to Zone of Genius; in modern English reads as "smart-aleck" or a boast.
- **Daemon:** Pullman's daemon and the Unix background process; risk of "demon".
- **Familiar:** warm, but the witch's servant, not a second self.
- **Genie / джинн:** see below.

### Genie: what a jinn is
- **Etymology: open question, three hypotheses** (Wikipedia, *Jinn*, citing Nünlist 2015: "the origin of the word jinn remains uncertain"). (1) Native Semitic root **j-n-n**, "to hide, to cover": *jinn* (the hidden ones), *janna* (garden, covered by foliage; paradise), *majnūn* (possessed, "mad"), *janīn* (embryo, hidden in the womb). (2) **Latin *genius*** (PIE *ǵenh₁-, "to beget": genesis, gene, genus, kin), the guardian spirit of persons, families and places, entering Arabia through Roman-era syncretism; disputed. (3) **Aramaic *ginnaya***, "tutelary deity, guardian", known from the region's inscriptions. Supporters of the link note that Roman genii and Arabian jinn did the same job: lesser spirits of a sanctuary, a tree, a spring, a person or a family. Read together: *the hidden one* and *the one born with you*.
- **Etymology meets myth.** Whatever the root, Antoine Galland, translating the Thousand and One Nights into French (1704 onward), rendered jinn as *génie*, and English took "genie" from there. The genie is, literally, the genius seen through Arabian tales.
- **The deep layer: the qarīn.** In Islamic tradition every human is born with a *qarīn*, a jinn companion assigned to them alone, who knows them intimately and whispers to them. That is layer 4: a personal spirit bound to one person from birth.
- **The popular layer: the lamp.** The pop genie (Aladdin) sits in layer 1: bound to whoever holds the lamp (transferable, not personal), grants three wishes, often literal-minded or tricky ("be careful what you wish for"), and longs for freedom. Disney's Genie adds friendship, but the frame is still master and wish-machine.
- **Verdict.** The genie confirms the bedrock (genius → génie → personal spirit) but its popular promise is wishes on demand, the opposite of a companion that knows you and grows with you; the word is also crowded in AI products. Best use: a contrast line, not the name. *"Not a genie that grants wishes. A genius that walks beside you."*

### 0d. Workstream: Evolutionary Mapping (Sasha, 2026-10-29 19:16) — living

> «Мы двигаемся в ширину и в глубину на каждом холоническом уровне… оформляем всё это в отдельный ворк-стрим.»

**The chain, as it actually unfolded:**
1. **Holomap = evolutionary map** of AI: AI split into its main aspects, aspects into sub-aspects (multi-level, holonic, modular); each aspect moves through stages on its own, yet all are bound by feedback loops and non-linear dependencies, known and unknown. The holomap contains the integral map and adds more levels (Wilber would say integral is the meta-theory with room for everything; Sasha agrees). Evolutionary and holonic are near-synonyms with nuances.
2. **2026 snapshot:** where AI actually stands, cell by cell (cells written long ago, tracked as AI develops).
3. **Grounding in reality:** fill the cells with what happened on Earth, past and present (`holomap_history_2026-10.md`). Contact with reality improves the description of the past, the present and the map itself, and so shows the future better.
4. **Leaders:** the grounded map shows who leads each aspect of AI.
5. **Market maps:** the same can be done for the whole economy, not only AI (library *Technology 177*): what continues, what falls away, and which leaders already work in the new paradigm, knowingly or not.
6. **Use:** invest, bet, network them, introduce, match-make, look for collaborations, form an agglomerate or club. Also feeds the game (Market, Co-working matchmaking).

**Products:** yes, map the products too (Sasha, 19:16), but methodically: inside the map, by aspect and stage, not as a loose list.

**Taxonomy (top-down):** to reach a fine niche-level taxonomy, first build the higher levels (economy → sectors → industries → sub-industries → niches → sub-niches → companies → leaders). For AI and frontier niches Sasha rates **CB Insights** highest: its market taxonomy sits at the cutting edge, but the full scheme is their paid know-how. Plan: reverse-engineer it from what they publish openly (public reports, market maps, research briefs, e.g. AI 100 2026, AI agent market map, AI agent tech stack in `market_maps_2026-10.md`), then build our own taxonomy, with official classifications (ISIC/NAICS) for the top layers.

**Ontology note:** AI as imitation (imitari / imago): library *Technology 179*.

**Next steps (in order):** (a) top layers of the economy taxonomy; (b) reverse-engineer CB Insights' public niche names into the AI branch; (c) place AI products onto the holomap cells; (d) apply the outer → inner → outer-anew axis (Tech 177).
