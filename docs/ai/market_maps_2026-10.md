## 4. Emerging vs crowded (as the maps show them)

**Crowded or consolidating**
- **Coding agents.** "The most crowded market on the map" (CBI-A). Largest app spend, $4B (Menlo-E). Margins are under pressure from reasoning-model costs (MAD; CBI-A).
- **Horizontal agents/copilots.** Horizontal outnumbers vertical about 2:1 and there are 1,000+ agentic offerings (CBI-A). Copilots are 86% of horizontal spend (Menlo-E). CBI-S calls agent dev platforms "crowded."
- **General assistants.** "The leaderboard is becoming familiar": only 11 new entrants in a16z-7. Users run three assistants on average (Menlo-C).
- **Customer support agents.** CB calls it a "land grab" (CB 2026 predictions); Sierra and Decagon are both on Forbes AI 50.
- **Creative/media generation.** Many players on MAD; labs are bundling (Nano Banana, Veo, Images 2.0) but specialised models still win spend (a16z-7).
- **Modern data stack.** Categories such as CDP and reverse ETL have "largely gone away"; dbt merged with Fivetran (MAD).
- **Consumer companions.** Under regulatory and legal pressure: Character.AI is in bankruptcy and NSFW apps were dropped from a16z rankings (would have been >20% of web traffic). App-store removals of Nomi and Chai in Oct 2026 *(tracker source, unverified)*.
- **Niche consumer AI apps** (meal planning, fitness). BVP doubts winners will emerge there.
- BVP says promising areas draw 2–3x as many rivals as in past cycles.

**Emerging (new boxes or called out as white space in 2025–26)**
- **Agent identity / KYA / agent security.** New AI 100 cohort (CBI-100); Insight's Agent IAM map; fastest-growing cyber segment (CBI-S).
- **Agent observability & evals.** Called an "M&A battleground" (CB 2026 predictions); private evals (BVP).
- **Memory & context.** BVP "dark matter" and "new moats"; MAD added Mem0 and Zep; CBI-S added a memory market.
- **Agent payments / agentic commerce.** CBI-S (backed by Visa, AmEx, Coinbase). Menlo predicts $100B of agentic sales within 12 months. Instinct has about $1B of transaction volume.
- **Personal agents in messaging.** Instinct, Muse, Dots, Poke, Town (a16z-7). 41% of US AI users have tried an agent (Menlo-C).
- **Open-source personal agent harnesses.** OpenClaw, Hermes Agent.
- **Local / on-device AI.** New MAD category; Menlo-E predicts "models move to the edge."
- **Physical AI / world models / robot fleets.** New AI 100 category (CBI-100); Air Street says robotics is near its "GPT-2 moment"; BVP covers world models.
- **RL environments and post-training data.** BVP "Second Act."
- **MCP infrastructure.** Gateways, auth, registries (Scalekit; a16z-MCP). About 124K unique servers (MCPacific), but usage is extremely concentrated: the top 0.1% of packages get 84% of installs *(mcphq.ai, secondary)*.
- **Voice.** Agents and voice as a primary interface (CBI-S; Menlo-C: 46% of AI users speak to AI); dictation (Wispr Flow).
- **AI hardware/wearables.** Plaud is the only scaled success; big tech is absorbing the rest (Meta–Limitless, Amazon–Bee). Wearables reach 15% of AI users weekly (Menlo-C).
- **Agent marketplaces, monetization, cost management.** CBI-S white space.
- **Consumer white space** a16z says is untouched: shopping, entertainment, social, dating, marketplaces, multiplayer. BVP predicts an AI-native social giant (not yet seen).
- **Generative video.** BVP: "2026 = year of video."
