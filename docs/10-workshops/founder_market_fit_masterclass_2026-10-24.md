# **Founder Market Fit Discovery: Masterclass \+ Live Coaching**

*Gamified Professional Success through Self-Discovery, Rapid Sovereign Entrepreneurship, and Conscious Business Scaling*

**Presented by:** Aleksandr Konstantinov  
**Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Duration:** 90 min online \| via Zoom

# **Abstract**

This masterclass outlines a transformative framework for sovereign entrepreneurs and founders to achieve authentic Founder-Market Fit (FMF). By synthesizing personal self-discovery with structured business architecture, the session explores how inner personal growth serves as the primary leverage point for commercial scaling. Moving away from standard hustle, grind, and scattered multi-talent traps, the material establishes "The Unique Business OS: 7 Artifacts" and the "Living Venture Flywheel." Through gamified self-discovery, conscious entrepreneurship, precise articulation of genius, and strategic AI integration, founders learn to transform their core essence, shadow, and worldviews into scalable, high-impact offerings.

# **What You'll Learn**

* How to escape common founder traps: hustle, multi-talent diffusion, spiritual fog, and adopting alien CEO roles.  
* The Mirror Principle: how your business serves as a direct crystallization of your inner state, talents, and shadow.  
* The 7 Artifacts of the Unique Business OS: Uniqueness, Shadow, Myth, Tribe, Pain, Promise, and Value Ladder.  
* How to operationalize your "Zone of Genius": structuring your core capabilities into Product, Marketing, and Distribution.  
* The Living Venture Flywheel and Conscious Entrepreneurship cycle: transitioning dynamically between BE and DO states (See \-\> Focus \-\> Act).  
* Integrating AI into your business workflow: equipping AI agents with full personal and business context maps.

# **What You Have Learned**

* True Founder-Market Fit: achieved when product transformation mirrors the founder's core self-transformation.  
* Resolving Dispersion: articulating your single underlying essence resolves multi-talent dispersion and builds clear market recognition.  
* Shadow Polarity: every talent possesses a shadow polarity; understanding this pairing reveals your unique myth and faculty on Earth.  
* Business Architecture as a Prism: marketing, distribution, and product function as a prism that freezes and crystallizes directed energy into cash flow.  
* Sustainable Momentum: relies on a continuous feedback loop between self-knowledge, strategic focus, irresistible action, and cash flow feedback.
