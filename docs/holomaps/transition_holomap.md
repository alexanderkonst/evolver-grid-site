## ESC

**Essence:** fit is never a property of a person or a context but of the relation between them, so a transition is the gap between two fits. The form that carried someone has ended and the next has not taken shape.

**Significance:** it is the only window in which the invariant becomes visible, because the form that was hiding it has dissolved.

**Consequence:** transitions are the highest-leverage intervention points in a human life. Help offered inside one compounds for decades. Identical help offered outside one is filed as "interesting."

## The seven stages

| Stage | Center | How they narrate it (first person) |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Settled | routine | "This is what I do." No narration at all; it is just Tuesday. |
| 2. The Itch | dissonance | "I'm fine. ...I don't know, something's just off." Tabs left open, side interests multiplying. |
| 3. Tremors | symptoms | "I just need to push through, or fix this one thing." Patching the old form from inside: new niche, new funnel, harder work. |
| 4. Rupture | the break | "It's over." Shock, relief, shame and freedom in the same hour. |
| 5. Liminality | the void | "I don't know who I am professionally." Consuming maps, tests, teachers, templates. |
| 6. Reorientation | recognition | "I think I see it." Energy in bursts; the first true sentence, tested on people. |
| 7. Crossing | commitment | "This is what I do now." Said to a stranger without flinching. |

---

## The six shifts

- **1→2** — the first feeling the current form has no slot for.
- **2→3** — the symptom becomes physical and starts costing sleep, energy, health.
- **3→4 — the Mi-Fa shock.** Something that was carrying you stops carrying you and the way back closes. Typically requires a force from outside the octave, which is why most people stall at Tremors for years.
- **4→5** — the last plan dies and the reflex to rebuild the same form exhausts itself. **This is surrender.**
- **5→6** — recognition rather than decision: something already true gets named. It feels like remembering, not choosing.
- **6→7 — the Si-Do shock.** The first real exchange; someone pays or says yes and the direction takes a body.

---

## The shock taxonomy (the flavors of 3→4)

Invariant plus six flavors:

- **External-forced** — fired, exit, shutdown, co-founder leaves, market dies, visa or geography.
- **Economic** — what reliably made money stops making money. The entrepreneur's most common flavor.
- **Somatic** — the body refuses: burnout, panic attacks, illness.
- **Relational** — a marriage, partnership or intimate field ends or changes its terms.
- **Internal/growth** — a practice, journey, medicine, teacher or book dissolves the identity from inside. Chosen, and typically slow: months or years, with no event to report.
- **Perceptual** — seeing something that cannot be unseen, after which the old form is intolerable although nothing external changed.

**Consequence:** the shock is not always an event and not always fast. Internal-growth ruptures are gradual dissolutions with no news to tell, which is why people inside them feel insane and do not recognize themselves in loud "I got fired" language.

## The 3×3 taxonomy: Identity · Economy · Fit

- **Identity** opens into Self-concept, Energy, Legitimacy.
- **Economy** opens into How money arrives, The clock, What I sell.
- **Fit** opens into Belonging, Recognition, Closest people.

**Note:** naming the third aspect "Fit" rather than "Relation" reveals that fit is the relation itself, which is what makes the map's core definition possible.

## Stages 8-10 and their shifts

**7→8** — the second crossing is recognized as the same shape as the first; pattern is visible only from the second instance.

**8. Fluency** — "I know which phase I'm in." Navigated rather than suffered; can be mid-transition and fully functional because the map is held.

**8→9** — the cost of waiting exceeds the cost of leaving.

**9. Volition** — transitions initiated before the shock arrives; a working form composted deliberately.

**9→10** — one's own crossings and other people's become one field.

**10. Hosting** — holding transition-space is the vocation; the crossing IS the work.

**Note:** stage 10 is universal as a capacity and specific as a vocation.

---

## Structural facts

- The stages are one-way. Nobody returns to Settled inside the old form, which is why an assessment based on this map reads as a verdict rather than a quiz.
- A transition is the mechanism by which the uniqueness line advances: uniqueness is the ratchet, a completed transition is the click.
- The practitioner's client zone is stages 5-7 (the crossing); stages 8-9 are the advisory and node zone; stage 10 is peers.
