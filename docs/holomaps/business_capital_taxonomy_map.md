# Business Capital — Integral Taxonomy Map

> *A MECE map of every way an external source of money enters a business. Two
> levels. Ordered top-to-bottom from most sovereignty-preserving to least, so the
> map doubles as a sovereignty gradient. Created Day 154 (August 13, 2026);
> sibling to the other taxonomy maps in `docs/holomaps/`.*

## Essence

There are only a handful of ways money reaches a business. Once you see all of
them on one page, "how do I grow?" stops being a fog and becomes a choice with a
known cost, and the cost that matters most here is **sovereignty**: how much of
the being you give away, and for how long.

## The map (most sovereign → least)

**1. Bootstrapping** — no outside capital; the business funds itself.
- Personal savings
- Customer revenue (retained earnings)

**2. Non-dilutive** — money that is neither owned back nor owed back.
- Grants
- Subsidies / tax credits
- Prizes / competitions
- Rewards crowdfunding / pre-sales

**3. Debt** — capital rented, then returned; no ownership given.
- Revenue-based financing (repaid from revenue, capped, then gone)
- Venture debt
- Bank loan
- Line of credit

**4. Hybrid** — sits between debt and equity.
- Revenue-share / royalty agreement
- Convertible note
- SAFE

**5. Equity** — a permanent piece of the being sold.
- Angel
- Venture capital
- Private equity
- Equity crowdfunding

## The sovereignty gradient

- **Bootstrapping** keeps you whole; the constraint is speed.
- **Non-dilutive** is free money but scarce and slow.
- **Debt** rents capital and returns you whole; **revenue-based financing** is the
  most sovereign form, it breathes with the business, is capped, and ends.
- **Hybrid** blurs the line, sovereign until it converts.
- **Equity** is the least sovereign: a permanent claim, sold to someone whose
  incentive is exit. A marriage you can't divorce, to a partner who wants to sell
  the house.

Rule of thumb for the Sovereign Founder Collective: **bootstrap to product-market
fit; after that, if a real machine is needed, prefer revenue-based financing or a
clean loan; reserve equity for the conscious venture-scale opt-in.** See
`docs/02-strategy/sovereign_founder_studio.md`.
