**The law in one sentence.** The words of Apple Seed point at the signal; the feeling they evoke is the contact with it. Caught, carried and re-met, that feeling becomes the person's own etalon, truer than any phrase.

### 🧠 Significance (AI draft)
- **From phrase to felt etalon.** Apple Seed v1 maximized the signal-to-noise ratio of the words. v2 keeps the signal itself. As the kilogram moved from a bar in a vault to a constant of nature, the person's measure moves from a sentence they can forget or re-word to a feeling they can evoke again and check against.
- **The only direct measure of the return.** A rating 1–10 is an indirect instrument. The felt moment is the contact (E0 in `docs/templates/calibration.md`).
- **The 3→4 crossing, lived.** In the uniqueness holomap the reveal is Initiation → Recognition («landed in my body»). The feeling is that Mi-Fa shock in the body; that is why it divides life into before and after.
- **The sword of truth.** Once caught, the feeling sorts: what does not resonate with it is not you. This is how articulation cuts mental garbage and becomes a tool for more contact (Technology 115).

### The five movements
1. **Evoke with a true mirror that has a cost.** The phrase together with the shadow. A phrase alone, flattering, sits in the Barnum zone (a generic sketch was rated 4.26/5 by the people it was given to; Forer 1949). The shadow is the cost that makes recognition real.
2. **Pause.** Silence right after the shadow lands. Nothing to do, nothing to rate. The moment belongs to the person.
3. **Catch: body first, then the person's own word.** Where did it land? Then, if it comes, one word or image of their own. Never a word given to them: naming dampens a feeling (affect labeling, pleasant ones too) and a supplied name replaces theirs.
4. **Keep and close calmly.** The person's word (or the place in the body) is kept as their tuning fork. The moment ends softly: the end of a moment shapes its memory (peak-end rule).
5. **Re-meet and use.** Later the person meets the feeling again, fresh, not replayed («is it here now?»), and checks a choice against it («does this feel like that?»). Recall reopens a memory; it deepens only when something new meets it.

### After the moment: the integration arc and the devaluation dip (Sasha, 2026-11-27 15:51 MST)
Sasha's account of how it works in people, close to his words:
1. The phrase evokes a feeling.
2. The words feel very precise; many sense, half-consciously, that fitting all of it into one phrase is huge.
3. After a few days the knowledge integrates to some degree, and the phrase no longer thrills as it did the first time.
4. **The mind devalues it at once.** These are malicious programs society installed about whether one can, in today's world, be oneself and be successful and not rejected. *(Key insight.)*
5. At this point the person can learn that formulations are endless, but some are more precise and some less.
6. Taking this knowledge in is itself a challenge.
7. All of this happens on the way to pulling Excalibur.
8. An animation of pulling it can come when it happens (much further along the path).

*Reading (Evolver):* the fading in step 3 is integration, not failure; step 4 is the shadow's counter-move, and it is the moment the caught feeling (movement 5, re-meet) is needed most. The dip is a predictable gate of the path (uniqueness holomap 4→5: «once the mirror-session glow fades»), so it should be named to the person in advance and met, not avoided.

### How to tell true recognition (checks)
- **By shape, not strength.** Quiet and certain, «like a drop of water into a sponge» (Ignatius of Loyola), surprise with certainty, relief together with a little embarrassment. Loud, dramatic, flattering states are suspect (makyō in Zen; peak experiences are «self-validating» and prove nothing).
- **Linked to the person's own episodes.** A true read connects to many of their memories (Singer's self-defining memories); a Barnum read connects to none.
- **It has a cost.** The shadow lands with charge.
- **It holds the next day.** Re-evoked, it is the same or deeper.
- **It bears fruit.** Within 30 days the person makes one choice from it. A reveal without use fades (only 3% meet all four strengths-use items in Gallup's data).

### The witness
Asks, never supplies. «The body scores: if the facilitator or the AI says 9 and the founder feels 7, it's 7» (Unique Business Canvas). In sessions: where did it land, what moved; two dials, resonance for the gift and charge for the shadow. The AI is a witness of the same kind.

### Guards (sovereignty)
Consent before reflecting (Technology 125). Offer the mirror once and let go (Technology 137). Never name the feeling for the person. No installed triggers: NLP anchoring found no support in most studies (54.5% unsupportive, Witkowski 2010). Intensity is never proof. No harsh initiations, no dependence on a guide.

### Where it lives (each grows from the technology; no screen decided)

- **The AI prompt** (people who run Apple Seed in their own AI): the prompt can end with the pause and the two questions.
- **The game:** later, from the technology, with Sasha's yes to each exact change (core flow).

### Shift check
