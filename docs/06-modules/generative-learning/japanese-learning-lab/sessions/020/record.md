Learner explicitly forgot `kamo shiremasen`; after reminder, first gave positive `iku kamo shiremasen`, then used past `ikimashita` or noun-negation `iku ja nai`. This showed the intent versus possibility distinction after support, not independent retrieval of the ending.
- New embedded yes/no question `S plain + ka wakarimasen` (“do not know whether S”): learner first produced `watashi no tomodachi wa ashita Kyōto ni iku ka wakarimasen`; tutor explained `ga` inside the inner question to distinguish who might go from who does not know. Learner rebuilt `watashi no tomodachi ga Kyōto ni iku ka wakarimasen`, with `ashita` absent from transcript. Next transfer to embedded wh-question: learner explicitly forgot `doko` and received a dock/life-ring image and role contrast `doko ni` (destination) versus `doko de` (action location). - Learner explicitly forgot `nani`; tutor gave a nanny/mystery-box image, and learner independently built `watashi no tomodachi ga ashita nani o kaitai ka wakarimasen`.
- New conditional `V plain past + ra`: tutor introduced `iku → itta → ittara` (“if/when go”), explained that the past-shaped form can refer to a future condition, and distinguished this from Spanish `si yo fuera` (closer here to `si voy`). Learner initially constructed `ashita Kyōto ni ittara ... ocha ... kaimasu`, with end distorted by ASR. On final attempt, learner self-corrected `ocha o kaitai desu` (“want to buy”) to `ashita Kyōto ni ittara, ocha o kaimasu` (“if I go to Kyoto tomorrow, I will buy tea”). Final correct.
