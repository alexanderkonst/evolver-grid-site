# Session 009 — companion, possession and purchase

Date: after session 008; exact local date not independently verified. Evidence is the available chat and dictated text, not audio. User requested Russian teaching and explicitly limited the finish to two final constructions. Session closed after those.

## New keys and demonstrated use
- tomodachi to: with a friend. Distinguished companion to from noun-joining to. Initial friend + de repaired to to; correct subsequent question: ashita tomodachi to doko ni ikitai desu ka.
- dare to: with whom. Correct polite question after ka reminder: ashita dare to ocha o nomitai desu ka.
- watashi no tomodachi: my friend; no then extended to tomodachi no ocha, dare no, and Kyōto no ocha (origin/association). Final answer to whose tea: watashi no tomodachi no ocha desu, with understood subject.
- mo after whole object noun phrase: watashi no tomodachi no ocha mo nomitai desu; first attempt self-repaired.
- adjective before noun: oishii ocha and takai ocha; correct transfer kono takai ocha wa oishii desu.
- kaimasu / kai- + tai desu / kaimashita for buy, want to buy, bought. Correct kono oishii ocha o kaitai desu, doko de kono ocha o kaitai desu ka after doko and ka reminders, and kinō Kyōto de ocha o kaimashita after o and desu repairs.
- mise: shop, Kyōto no mise de: in a shop in Kyoto. Correct kinō Kyōto no mise de ocha o kaimashita after omission and desu cues.

## Friction and teaching implications
Ni/de/to and o/de still occasionally substituted; explain roles concretely. Doko/kinō needed reminders. Desu continued to be added after -mashita; teach the contrast in context. Dictation transcribed oishii as oishita and other fragments unclearly; do not diagnose pronunciation from the text. Assistant briefly reversed who bought/tasted tea; learner caught it and scenario was corrected. Careful role setup matters. The learner still lacks Japanese script literacy; output romaji only. Russian remains preferred for explanations.
