## Measurement rule

Record attempts and assistance, not just success totals. A novel generation requires a correct intended meaning, familiar ingredients, no answer-bearing cue, and no earlier occurrence of the complete answer in the available record. Unknown exposure means novelty unknown. A repeated successful sentence counts as retrieval, not novelty. Delayed means material from a prior session used before reminders; record the interval when known.

Session 001 verified novel-generation count: not established from the partial record. Delayed novel generation: not assessed. Do not manufacture a baseline of zero or count duplicated streaming fragments as attempts. Keep self-corrected and first-attempt successes distinguishable.
