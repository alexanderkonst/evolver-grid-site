## What Is the Evolutionary Avantgarde?

Communities at the **frontier of technology AND consciousness**. They share three beliefs:

1. The current system is broken
2. A new paradigm is possible  
3. Change starts with **self-development → system change**

**Not just one movement.** A constellation of subcultures — each with different focus, unified by shared coordination infrastructure.

## The silo problem

**Each flavor cooks in its own juice.** Each one sees something real about where the planet is going, and that is exactly why it is sure of its own view. The result: flavors rarely talk to each other, and often do not know the others exist. The person building digital twins of cities is unlikely to be talking with New Earth leaders. New Earth leaders are unlikely to be talking with web3 people, pop-up cities and network states, and the other way round. The same holds for most pairs in the table above.

**The integral view is not the felt map.** Integral people do have a view of the whole, but usually not the texture of each flavor: its language, its nuances, what its people actually need on a Tuesday. A map of the whole without the felt map of the parts does not connect anyone.

**What the silos cost.**
- Every flavor rebuilds the same infrastructure on its own: its own events, its own programs, its own marketplace, its own community platform, usually half-finished.
- A practitioner has to pick one tribe to make a living in, so their clients are capped at the size of that petal.
- Complementary people never meet: the breathwork facilitator, the city-twin builder and the venture studio partner each hold a piece the others are missing.
- The avant-garde as a whole stays a set of subcultures, not a force.

**The intersection.** Someone has to read all the flavors at once, in their own languages, and see the links between them. That is the role FindYourTopTalent.com and its founder take: not a fourth framework on top, but the bedrock underneath.

## One primitive across all flavors (bedrock)

**The claim.** Whatever the flavor, its people need the same set of things. Sasha's list (October 27, 2026): **a village, uniqueness, shops (stalls), an economy, intentional growth, transformational programs, coworking, masterminds, hackathons, a venture studio, playbooks for creating a business, and AI to amplify one's own signal.** This set exists, or will exist, in every flavor. It is the primitive, the bedrock. No flavor holds all of it; each holds two or three pieces and lacks the rest.

**The game is the primitive made playable.** The Game of Being You (`game_of_being_you.md`) packages exactly this set as one world: the Digital Village (§13.1), where each piece of the primitive is a place:

| Piece of the primitive | Place in the village |
|---|---|
| Village | the village map itself (§14.6) |
| Uniqueness | Gate (Top Talent test) and Your House |
| Shops, economy | Market (your stall, §14.12) and Town Hall (residents' income) |
| Intentional growth, transformational programs | Academy (webinar, 21-day practice) and Your House (private QoL level) |
| Coworking | Co-working (Party & Guild, matchmaking) |
| Masterminds | Fire Circle |
| Hackathons, venture studio | Town Hall (Venture Co-op) |
| Business-creation playbooks | Workshop (Unique Business Builder) |
| AI to amplify one's signal | Oracle (AI OS) |

San Pancho is Village #1 of the same primitive in the physical world (§16). The scale ladder: villages, then cities, then bioregions, where the village meets the digital-twin flavor (§16.5).

**Flavor-neutral by design.** The Gate is the same for everyone: the uniqueness test does not ask which petal you belong to. The flavor shows up in what you build, not in what you must believe to enter. This is what lets one village hold all of them.

### Reading the game through this prism

- **One Journey, many doors.** The Journey (§14.2) stays one. What changes per flavor is the copy at the door and the first offer shown, not the path.
- **Practitioners first, partners second.** Flavors whose people are solo practitioners with an offer (breathwork, energetic and archetypal work, spiritual teachers, consciousness technologies, uniqueness) are players and early stall owners. Flavors that build infrastructure (digital twins, network states, venture studios, AI-native builders, game designers, Enspiral-type communities) are partners: they enter through Town Hall, Oracle and village design.
- **Koster facet check (§15.8), extended.** For every new place, add one line: which flavor will recognize this place as its own door?
