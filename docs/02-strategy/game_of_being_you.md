## 1. Core metaphor: one light in, your spectrum out

The same white light enters everyone. Your Uniqueness, a crystal, refracts it into a spectrum that belongs to you alone. You don't become someone else: you reveal your own configuration.

- **White beam** = the shared source (life, consciousness), the same for every player.
- **Crystal (dodecahedron)** = your Uniqueness.
- **Spectrum on the way out** = your soul colors, your personal signature in the world.
- **Reflection in still water** = "as above, so below": the inner world mirrored in the outer one.

## 2. Five paths = five Platonic solids = five elements

Terminology: we say **Uniqueness**, not "Genius" (Sasha, 2026-10-26). The Plato and classical mapping lines up exactly:

| Path | Solid | Element | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | Cube | Earth | vitality and foundation |
| Emotions | Icosahedron | Water | mastery of inner weather |
| Mind | Octahedron | Air | clarity and perception |
| Spirit | Tetrahedron | Fire | the fire that moves you |
| **Uniqueness** | **Dodecahedron** (center) | **Aether** | your unique gift, refracted into the world |

The dodecahedron sits in the center. The four smaller solids orbit it. (Correction 2026-10-26: an earlier draft swapped Spirit and Uniqueness. Spirit is fire, the tetrahedron.)

## 3. Soul colors: the Spectrum mechanic (core of the game)

**Observation (Sasha, 2026-10-26):** he can already read a person's soul colors from their uniqueness. It worked several times and was confirmed by people who got the same colors elsewhere, independently. AI can infer the colors from a uniqueness description, as if uniqueness gives off color.

**In the game:** the soul colors are the player's spectrum, which is exactly what comes out of their crystal. This is the central reveal and the identity token:

1. **Character creation (NEW GAME):** Uniqueness test, then a *Spectrum Reading*. The white beam enters the player's crystal, and *their* colors come out instead of a generic rainbow.
2. **Your Crystal card:** a shareable artifact showing your crystal and your spectrum, your name, the one-line essence of your uniqueness, and the hex codes of your colors. This is the viral artifact (sign of belonging).
3. **The world takes your colors:** after the reveal, the player's UI accents (cursor, rules, highlights) switch from neutral ice blue to their own spectrum. The game literally becomes theirs.
4. **Multiplayer:** spectra can be laid side by side. Resonant palettes and complementary palettes become the basis for matching and tribes. A team is a composite light.
5. **Profile page = your crystal:** the profile centers on your crystal refracting light into your colors. The five solids around it show the level on each path. The spectrum is the signature on everything you create or offer (marketplace cards, mastermind seats).

Open: the reading method (prompt + validation against the cases where colors were confirmed), naming of colors, and whether the spectrum can evolve over the game (inferred idea: it deepens as the profile deepens).

## 5. Visual system and anti-kitsch rules

Synthesis of three directions, converging on **one real object under real light**:
- **Cinema** gives the space and the camera (landscape, still water, mist, slow motion, film grain).
- **Prism** gives the object and the only color (refraction is the only source of saturated color).
- **1970s metaphysical paperback** gives typography and paper (serif + thin mono, chapter numbering, paper grain on the reading sections).

Rules: near-black background; one accent color (ice blue `#bcd7e6` until the player's own spectrum takes over); physical materials only (stone, water, topaz, paper, gold); physically correct light; huge negative space; small type. No neon, no glow outlines, no stock mandalas, no gradient-behind-text, no emoji.

Rejected: topaz on pure black with a prism beam reads too close to Pink Floyd's *Dark Side of the Moon* cover. Amber topaz was replaced by clear, faintly blue topaz (Sasha, 2026-10-26).

## 7. The golden thread of cult virality

Cult things spread when they turn the audience into authors:
1. **A world, not a product:** dense enough to live in, with gaps for interpretation.
2. **A small, dense tribe of creators:** the first players create the culture.
3. **Tools for remix:** players make their own things inside the world.
4. **A sign of belonging:** your Crystal card and your colors.
5. **Mystery and ritual:** we have plenty already. Locked levels, Masterclass as the recurring ritual.

Applied: Mastermind = the creative "Factory"; participants are co-authors, not clients.

## 10. Core loop v0.1: goal, levels, XP (2026-10-26)

**Goal of the game** (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «прикольно»)**:** a high quality of life, as defined by the Quality of Life Map (8 domains × 10 stages: Wealth, Health, Happiness, Love & Relationships, Impact, Growth, Social Ties, Home; `src/modules/quality-of-life-map/qolConfig.ts`).

- **Level = your bottleneck:** the lowest stage across all 8 domains. To level up, the whole map has to move up at least one stage (Sasha: "a level is at least one transition across the whole map"). Theory of Constraints / Liebig's barrel.
- **Main quest = suggested from the bottleneck, confirmed by the player.** Life is rarely black and white, so the game proposes the bottleneck domain as the main quest and the player confirms or picks another (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «не всегда будет черно белое… только если игрок подтверждает»).
- **No artificial XP (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «может просто наша цель — точно показать саму жизнь? А не придумывать искусственные XP? Мы же саму жизнь артикулируем»).** Progress is the player's real movement on the QoL map, read from life, not points. What matters to people is what the platform shows, as far as possible. The five paths (Body, Emotions, Mind, Spirit, Uniqueness) are the skill tree; their practices feed XP into domains.
- **Leveling down is real** (life goes down too), so two meters: **Current level** (can fall, shown as weather, not punishment) and **Peak** (best level reached, kept). A drop opens a **Comeback quest** with bonus XP.
- **Input (confirmed):** a 60-second weekly **Save Point** ritual (each domain: up / same / down + one line) and a full map re-take each season.
- **Rejected (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «ерунда! Не сработает»):** evidence from quests and peer witness from the party as XP/input.
- **The crystal shows the level:** clarity and brightness of the player's spectrum grow with level; new facets unlock (Spectrum insights).

### Motivation mechanics
1. Bottleneck suggests the main quest; the player confirms it (no forced black-and-white).
2. Crystal brightens with level: visible identity progression.
3. ✅ **Approved (Sasha, 2026-10-26).** Daily 5-minute quests (practices), gentle streak with freezes.
4. Party / guild = Mastermind: shared season quest, contribution to others (feeds Impact and Social Ties).
5. Seasons of ~6 weeks: Masterclass opens a season; a closing ceremony shows the before/after map.
6. ✅ **Approved.** Real loot: first income from your unique offer is a boss drop.
7. ✅ **Approved.** Bosses = real-life thresholds (first sale, a move, a hard conversation).
8. ✅ **Approved.** Compare only with your past self. No QoL leaderboard; if any leaderboard, only for contribution to others.
9. Variable reward: new Spectrum insights and crystal facets.


## 10a. Fidelity principle (2026-10-26, Sasha)

**The game articulates life itself.** It does not invent a parallel economy of points; it mirrors the player's real state as precisely as possible and makes it legible, beautiful and playable. Test for any mechanic: *does it show something true about the player's life, or does it add something artificial?* Only the first passes. This is the stage-7 move in the almanac: fidelity = 100%, nothing is simulated.

Stage-7+ mechanics that pass the test (proposed, Evolver):
1. **Character sheet = the QoL map**, Current vs Peak: the HUD is your life.
2. **Main quest = a real intention** the player names (bottleneck suggests, player confirms); completion = a real event.
3. **Loot = real outcomes**: income, a relationship, a home, health markers.
4. **Bosses = real thresholds** (first sale, a move, a hard conversation).
5. **Fog of war = the next stage's description**: the map reveals what the next level of a domain looks like, so the path is visible without invented tasks.
6. **AI game master narrates your real life**: season recaps, patterns noticed across Save Points, the story of your chapter (the generative GM of stage 7).
7. **Spectrum insights = real self-knowledge** unlocked as you play (soul colors, Uniqueness).
8. **Seasons = real life rhythms**, closed with a before/after map.
9. **Party = real people** walking real quests together (stage-8 on-ramp).

## 11. The Fifth Element (2026-10-26, Sasha)

**Insight:** the dodecahedron is Plato's fifth solid. Plato's *Timaeus* uses it for the cosmos as a whole; Aristotle's fifth element is aether, which later tradition calls *quintessentia*, "the fifth essence". In our map it is **Uniqueness**. So the player's uniqueness is literally their quintessence: the fifth element that the four (Body/earth, Emotions/water, Mind/air, Spirit/fire) are held by and refracted through.

**Proposal (Sasha):** name the game **The 5th Element**, with tagline **"the game of being you"**.

**Evolver's read (open fork, his decision):**
- The mythos is exact and should be canon: the four elements orbit, the fifth sits in the center, light passes through it, and "find your fifth element" is the core quest.
- The name collides: Luc Besson's film *The Fifth Element* (Gaumont, 1997) and its 1998 video game (Kalisto) sit in the same entertainment/games classes. That brings trademark risk and searches that land on the film.
- **Decision for now (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «давай пока так»):** option (a). Name stays THE GAME OF BEING YOU; "You Are The Fifth Element" is the headline of the second screen. Trademark facts: Gaumont US reg. 2621600 THE FIFTH ELEMENT (class 9, renewed 2023); pending application serial 50125666 THE FIFTH ELEMENT, class 42 software, filed 2026-09-23 by Wild Woman Haus, LLC (Tempe, AZ). The concept itself (Plato, aether) is public domain.
- Options: (a) title stays THE GAME OF BEING YOU, and "The Fifth Element" becomes the central chapter and quest ("Discover your Fifth Element"); (b) a distinct word for the same meaning: *Quintessence*, *Aether*, *The Fifth*; (c) take the name anyway after a trademark check.

**Visual consequence:** the axis hero (light from source → through the center of the crystal → grounded into earth, spectrum radiating 360°) already shows the fifth element as the medium through which source light becomes a personal spectrum.
