## 1. Core metaphor: one light in, your spectrum out

The same white light enters everyone. Your Uniqueness, a crystal, refracts it into a spectrum that belongs to you alone. You don't become someone else: you reveal your own configuration.

- **White beam** = the shared source (life, consciousness), the same for every player.
- **Crystal (dodecahedron)** = your Uniqueness.
- **Spectrum on the way out** = your soul colors, your personal signature in the world.
- **Reflection in still water** = "as above, so below": the inner world mirrored in the outer one.

## 2. Five paths = five Platonic solids = five elements

Terminology: we say **Uniqueness**, not "Genius" (Sasha, 2026-10-26). The Plato and classical mapping lines up exactly:

| Path | Solid | Element | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Body | Cube | Earth | vitality and foundation |
| Emotions | Icosahedron | Water | mastery of inner weather |
| Mind | Octahedron | Air | clarity and perception |
| Spirit | Tetrahedron | Fire | the fire that moves you |
| **Uniqueness** | **Dodecahedron** (center) | **Aether** | your unique gift, refracted into the world |

The dodecahedron sits in the center. The four smaller solids orbit it. (Correction 2026-10-26: an earlier draft swapped Spirit and Uniqueness. Spirit is fire, the tetrahedron.)

## 3. Soul colors: the Spectrum mechanic (core of the game)

**Observation (Sasha, 2026-10-26):** he can already read a person's soul colors from their uniqueness. It worked several times and was confirmed by people who got the same colors elsewhere, independently. AI can infer the colors from a uniqueness description, as if uniqueness gives off color.

**In the game:** the soul colors are the player's spectrum, which is exactly what comes out of their crystal. This is the central reveal and the identity token:

1. **Character creation (NEW GAME):** Uniqueness test, then a *Spectrum Reading*. The white beam enters the player's crystal, and *their* colors come out instead of a generic rainbow.
2. **Your Crystal card:** a shareable artifact showing your crystal and your spectrum, your name, the one-line essence of your uniqueness, and the hex codes of your colors. This is the viral artifact (sign of belonging).
3. **The world takes your colors:** after the reveal, the player's UI accents (cursor, rules, highlights) switch from neutral ice blue to their own spectrum. The game literally becomes theirs.
4. **Multiplayer:** spectra can be laid side by side. Resonant palettes and complementary palettes become the basis for matching and tribes. A team is a composite light.
5. **Profile page = your crystal:** the profile centers on your crystal refracting light into your colors. The five solids around it show the level on each path. The spectrum is the signature on everything you create or offer (marketplace cards, mastermind seats).

Open: the reading method (prompt + validation against the cases where colors were confirmed), naming of colors, and whether the spectrum can evolve over the game (inferred idea: it deepens as the profile deepens).

## 5. Visual system and anti-kitsch rules

Synthesis of three directions, converging on **one real object under real light**:
- **Cinema** gives the space and the camera (landscape, still water, mist, slow motion, film grain).
- **Prism** gives the object and the only color (refraction is the only source of saturated color).
- **1970s metaphysical paperback** gives typography and paper (serif + thin mono, chapter numbering, paper grain on the reading sections).

Rules: near-black background; one accent color (ice blue `#bcd7e6` until the player's own spectrum takes over); physical materials only (stone, water, topaz, paper, gold); physically correct light; huge negative space; small type. No neon, no glow outlines, no stock mandalas, no gradient-behind-text, no emoji.

Rejected: topaz on pure black with a prism beam reads too close to Pink Floyd's *Dark Side of the Moon* cover. Amber topaz was replaced by clear, faintly blue topaz (Sasha, 2026-10-26).

## 10. Core loop v0.1: goal and levels (2026-10-26)

**Goal of the game** (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «прикольно»)**:** a high quality of life, as defined by the Quality of Life Map (8 domains × 10 stages: Wealth, Health, Happiness, Love & Relationships, Impact, Growth, Social Ties, Home; `src/modules/quality-of-life-map/qolConfig.ts`).

- **Level = your bottleneck:** the lowest stage across all 8 domains. To level up, the whole map has to move up at least one stage (Sasha: "a level is at least one transition across the whole map"). Theory of Constraints / Liebig's barrel.
- **Main quest = suggested from the bottleneck, confirmed by the player.** Life is rarely black and white, so the game proposes the bottleneck domain as the main quest and the player confirms or picks another (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «не всегда будет черно белое… только если игрок подтверждает»).
- **No artificial XP (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «может просто наша цель — точно показать саму жизнь? А не придумывать искусственные XP? Мы же саму жизнь артикулируем»).** Progress is the player's real movement on the QoL map, read from life, not points. What matters to people is what the platform shows, as far as possible. The five paths (Body, Emotions, Mind, Spirit, Uniqueness) are the skill tree; their practices move the player's real level in each domain.
- **Leveling down is real** (life goes down too), so two meters: **Current level** (can fall, shown as weather, not punishment) and **Peak** (best level reached, kept). A drop opens a **Comeback quest**; its reward is the real recovery.
- **Input (confirmed):** a 60-second weekly **Save Point** ritual (each domain: up / same / down + one line) and a full map re-take each season.
- **Rejected (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «ерунда! Не сработает»):** evidence from quests and peer witness from the party as XP/input.
- **The crystal shows the level:** clarity and brightness of the player's spectrum grow with level; new facets unlock (Spectrum insights).

## 10a. Fidelity principle (2026-10-26, Sasha)

**The game articulates life itself.** It does not invent a parallel economy of points; it mirrors the player's real state as precisely as possible and makes it legible, beautiful and playable. Test for any mechanic: *does it show something true about the player's life, or does it add something artificial?* Only the first passes. This is the stage-7 move in the almanac: fidelity = 100%, nothing is simulated.

Stage-7+ mechanics that pass the test (proposed, Evolver):
1. **Character sheet = the QoL map**, Current vs Peak: the HUD is your life.
2. **Main quest = a real intention** the player names (bottleneck suggests, player confirms); completion = a real event.
3. **Loot = real outcomes**: income, a relationship, a home, health markers.
4. **Bosses = real thresholds** (first sale, a move, a hard conversation).
5. **Fog of war = the next stage's description**: the map reveals what the next level of a domain looks like, so the path is visible without invented tasks.
6. **AI game master narrates your real life**: season recaps, patterns noticed across Save Points, the story of your chapter (the generative GM of stage 7).
7. **Spectrum insights = real self-knowledge** unlocked as you play (soul colors, Uniqueness).
8. **Seasons = real life rhythms**, closed with a before/after map.
9. **Party = real people** walking real quests together (stage-8 on-ramp).

## 11. The Fifth Element (2026-10-26, Sasha)

**Insight:** the dodecahedron is Plato's fifth solid. Plato's *Timaeus* uses it for the cosmos as a whole; Aristotle's fifth element is aether, which later tradition calls *quintessentia*, "the fifth essence". In our map it is **Uniqueness**. So the player's uniqueness is literally their quintessence: the fifth element that the four (Body/earth, Emotions/water, Mind/air, Spirit/fire) are held by and refracted through.

**Proposal (Sasha):** name the game **The 5th Element**, with tagline **"the game of being you"**.

**Evolver's read (open fork, his decision):**
- The mythos is exact and should be canon: the four elements orbit, the fifth sits in the center, light passes through it, and "find your fifth element" is the core quest.
- The name collides: Luc Besson's film *The Fifth Element* (Gaumont, 1997) and its 1998 video game (Kalisto) sit in the same entertainment/games classes. That brings trademark risk and searches that land on the film.
- **Decision for now (Sasha, 2026-10-26: «давай пока так»):** option (a). Name stays THE GAME OF BEING YOU; "You Are The Fifth Element" is the headline of the second screen. Trademark facts: Gaumont US reg. 2621600 THE FIFTH ELEMENT (class 9, renewed 2023); pending application serial 50125666 THE FIFTH ELEMENT, class 42 software, filed 2026-09-23 by Wild Woman Haus, LLC (Tempe, AZ). The concept itself (Plato, aether) is public domain.
- Options: (a) title stays THE GAME OF BEING YOU, and "The Fifth Element" becomes the central chapter and quest ("Discover your Fifth Element"); (b) a distinct word for the same meaning: *Quintessence*, *Aether*, *The Fifth*; (c) take the name anyway after a trademark check.

**Visual consequence:** the axis hero (light from source → through the center of the crystal → grounded into earth, spectrum radiating 360°) already shows the fifth element as the medium through which source light becomes a personal spectrum.

### 13.4 Victories: the path of uniqueness, step by step (Sasha, 2026-10-27)
**Principle.** Real wins are collected from the very first day of use, so people recognize these victories in themselves instead of overlooking them. Collected over time, they prove «I can», show the next goal and give the motivation to walk towards it. Sasha: «Нутром чую что это будет мега приятно людям». No points: each victory is a real event, a trace in reality.

**On every completed step:**
1. Congratulate the player (a moment of recognition, never a generic toast).
2. Ask: «How much did this resonate, from 1 to 10?»
3. Record the victory and the rating in the player's save file (Character Sheet), as a growing collection.

### 14.1 Principles (absurdly simple)

1. **The Journey is the spine.** One ordered path (14.2) drives the village hints, the rail order, the lock states and Your House. A screen that serves no Journey step is an extra, never in the way.
2. **One world, one map, one menu.** The village is the navigation. The rail is the same places, same order, same names as the map. The title screen is the only door in.
3. **The village is where you play.** It is the main game screen, not a menu: it always shows the next move you can make and what it will give you (the transformational result), and what just changed.
4. **Every module has one form: Intro → one button → the result → Outro → back to the village.** Intro: the transformational result you will get, and one button. Outro: what just happened, congratulations, the §13.4 ritual (1–10), and the way back to the village where the next hint is waiting.
5. **Open is alive; closed is faded.** The world is in color. A place or possibility you cannot enter yet is dim/desaturated, cannot be entered, and says in one line what opens it and what is inside. One component, every place, room, quest, module, in every theme. No «locked / disabled / coming soon / paywall / unlock» words; the picture says it.
6. **Places open in the order life earns them.** Reception → Your House → Workshop → Market → Town Hall. You get a voice after you have fed someone.
7. **Progress shows in the world, never in numbers.** No XP, no public levels, no score cards. The one personal number (real QoL level) is private (§13.2). The one public number is the village's: residents' income.
8. **Many themes, one token system.** Existing themes stay exactly as they are; a new **Lunar** theme joins them. Every Journey screen reads semantic tokens, so every theme renders every screen, and the lock rule holds in all of them.
9. **Every door that asks for money is a real checkout in one step,** titled with the same price and length as the page. No `mailto:` for a seat; no «free» headline over a paid offer.
10. **Nothing internal on the surface.** No repo names, version numbers, maturity stages, «not wired yet» buttons.

### 14.4 Module form: Intro → one button → result → Outro

*Sasha, 14:44: every product and module has one transformational result and one button. Intro explains the result and shows the button; Outro says what just happened and congratulates. Canonical source (Sasha, 15:08: «Integrated product builder»): [integrated_product_building_workflow.md](../03-playbooks/integrated_product_building_workflow.md) → **The Transformative-Result Pattern** (Day 80, 2026-05-22, «most important UX principle»): every product / module / step has two paired screens, Before (Promise) and After (Confirmation); the After-screen IS the deliverable, a toast or silent redirect is not. Supporting detail: [ux_playbook.md](../03-playbooks/ux_playbook.md) Parts 4, 5, 7. The lock rule (14.3) also echoes [customer_journey_progression.md](../05-specs/customer_journey_progression.md) (Jan 2026): the platform reveals itself progressively.*

- **Intro = Before-screen (`ModuleIntro`):** place scene; eyebrow + headline that state the result (must match the module's Master Result, workflow §1.1); 1–2 line silhouette of what will happen; **one button whose label is the result as a verb** («Find my mission →», never «Continue»). Faded (`closed`) when the step is not open yet, with its line.
- **Passage:** the module as it is (unchanged inside).
- **Outro = After-screen (`ModuleOutro`), a stable surface, never a toast:** celebration eyebrow («Done · Your mission is saved»); **the result itself, displayed prominently**, in the player's own words; where it lives (Your House); **what it unlocks** (which place in the village just opened or brightened); congratulations; «How much did this resonate, 1–10?»; the victory recorded in the save file; **one forward button: Back to the village**, where the next hint waits. (The workflow's «Continue your journey → JOURNEY homebase» now lands in the village, the game reading of JOURNEY.)
- **Village hint** = the next Intro's promise: «Your House · Discover your mission — you'll know what is yours to do in the world.»
- **DoD per module (WP-5):** Intro headline = Master Result; button label = result verb; Outro shows the artifact + what it unlocked + one button back. Modules lacking any of the three are listed in 14.5.

### 14.11 Lunar: how color enters as you play (Sasha, 2026-10-27: «продумай как именно добавляются краски по мере прохождения»)

Reference mockups: `docs/assets/lunar/lunar-sheet.jpg` (title, village map, module Intro, module Outro). Fonts in the mockups are reference only.

Lunar starts as a readable silver moonlit world (never a punishing black-and-white). Color is not a reward currency and has no counter: it is **your** color arriving in the places you have actually lived through. One rule, five steps:

| State | Look in Lunar | When |
|---|---|---|
| closed | dark silhouette, unlit windows, label at low contrast; the «Opens when… Inside:…» line on hover/tap | its condition is not met |
| open | bright silver, soft moonlight, gentle motion | condition met, module not done |
| next | open + the one teal accent (outline, ring marker) | the single next move |
| done | **the place takes on color**: your soul colors as the dominant hue, warm lit windows | its module's Outro reached |
| live | done + a slow pulse | something happening now (webinar, Mastermind) |

The arc:
1. **Reception** done (Top Talent found) → the first color in the world appears on **Your House**: your soul colors (until Spectrum Reading exists: the colors of your top talent archetype). The first color is you.
2. **Mission** done → your house's windows light warm; the path from your house to the Workshop lights up.
3. Each further module done → its place turns to color; the paths between colored places glow, so the colored part of the map is literally the shape of your journey.
4. **First sale** → your stall in the Market takes color and **your house shows in color on other residents' maps**; Town Hall opens.
5. Residents: other houses in color = people who have walked the road. The village's color is collective.
6. Sky: as the Journey fills, the sky moves from deep night toward pre-dawn blue at the horizon; full Journey = first light. Subtle, never a progress bar.

Outro moment: the color fills into the place on screen (the «what opened» beat, WP-15 motion). Other skins: the same 5 states via lock/live tokens; only Lunar uses saturation as the carrier. Implementation: `placeState` gains `done`; Lunar CSS maps `closed/open/next/done/live` to filter + tokens (`--place-saturation`, `--place-glow`); soul colors read from `useCharacterSheet`. Lands in WP-2 + WP-3 (state) and WP-6/WP-15 (reveal).

### 15.7a Two loops: the moon and the sun (Sasha's extension)

- **Macro loop = the lunar–earth cycle** (~29.5 days): the village sky follows the real moon (new moon → let go, waxing → build, full moon → harvest, waning → digest). Read from the date, no input, no counter. Same source of truth as Equilibrium (`src/lib/equilibrium-cycles`, `getLunarState`).
- **Daily loop = the solar–earth cycle** (one Earth rotation): familiar to everyone and underused. The key is **stacked daily habits**: small practices that attach to the day's natural moments (waking, midday, dusk, night), so the day itself is the loop. Connects to §10 daily 5-minute quests (approved) and the Equilibrium solar cycle (`getSolarState`). Design of the daily loop is the next step after WP-16; not yet specified.
- **Layering with §14.11:** the Journey still moves the horizon from night toward first light (your progress); the real moon sets the sky's phase and mood (shared time). Two independent layers: the moon is the same for every resident tonight, the dawn is yours.

## 18. The player raises their own AI (Sasha, 2026-10-29)

**Insight.** All the parts are already built; what's missing is integration and words a person understands. Every move in the game feeds one asset that outlives the game: the player's personal AI. It maps one-to-one onto the AI thesis (docs/ai/README.md §0):

| Shift (thesis) | Where it is built in the game | What the AI gains |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hyperpersonalization | Character sheet, top talent (Zone of Genius) | It knows **you** |
| 2. Activation of AI's potential | AI OS (today's Oracle place) | It is **awake**, on top of any model |
| 3. AI that knows your business | Workshop artifacts → the player's corpus | It knows **your work** |

**Design rule.** Feel first, name second. The player first sees the difference (same real question: a plain model's answer vs. the answer from an AI that knows them), and only then gets one line: "Models have become alike. The difference is how well your AI knows you." Then one button takes their personal prompt into their own AI.

**Make it visible.** The player should see their AI growing as they play: each completed module adds to it (a short "your AI just learned…" moment), so the three gifts fill up over the journey.

**Naming (open).** The Oracle place gets a new name centred on the player's own AI. Candidates: "Your Familiar" (Evolver's recommendation; verb inside: "Train your familiar"), "personal AI harness" (keep as internal/docs term), "AI training". Decision: Sasha.
