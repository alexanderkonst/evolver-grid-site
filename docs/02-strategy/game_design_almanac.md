## Invariant parameter: the scope of player authorship

What a player is allowed to author keeps growing: first a move, then a strategy, a performance, an identity, a path through a world, whole worlds for others, and finally their own life. Each phase shift widens the frame by one order of magnitude.

Octave reading: Do→Mi run inside fixed rules. The **Mi–Fa interval** (3→4) is the jump from performance to identity, when the player becomes a character. The **Si–Do shock** (7) is the game leaving the "magic circle": real life becomes the game state, and the game becomes generative around the player.

| # | Stage | Era | Player authors | One-line description |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rules & Fate | c. 3000 BCE–1700s | the move | Senet, Go, chess, dice: fixed sacred or martial rules where the player chooses moves within a cosmos given by gods, chance or tradition. |
| 2 | Designed Systems | 1780s–1950s | the strategy | Kriegsspiel, The Landlord's Game/Monopoly: games become deliberately engineered systems with an author, built to model and teach the world. |
| 3 | Machine Reflex | 1958–1980 | the performance | Tennis for Two, Spacewar!, Pong, arcades: the machine enforces rules in real time and the player authors a score, a skill, a high-score name. |
| ⟡ | Mi–Fa interval | 1974–1980 | | D&D and early text adventures bring imagination and persona in: the leap from playing a game to being someone in it. |
| 4 | Character & Story | 1974–1995 | the identity | D&D, Zork, Ultima, Zelda, Final Fantasy: the player becomes a persona on a hero's journey, with stats, inventory and moral choices. |
| 5 | Worlds & Emergence | 1989–2008 | the path | SimCity, Doom mods, Ultima Online, GTA III, The Sims, WoW: persistent sandboxes and systems where play emerges and each player writes their own route. |
| 6 | Platforms & Creators | 2003–2022 | worlds for others | Second Life, Minecraft, Roblox, Dota (mod → genre), Fortnite Creative, Twitch: players build games, economies and culture for millions; the game becomes a platform. |
| 7 | Life as the Game (Si–Do) | 2023– | their own life | Generative AI game masters, location and life-gamified play (Pokémon GO, Strava, Duolingo), games whose state is the player's real self: the magic circle opens and a new octave begins. |

## Second axis: fidelity to reality

How close the game's state is to the real world. It rises with every stage and reaches its asymptote at stage 7, where the map *is* the territory. Then the arrow flips: the game stops imitating reality and starts shaping it.

| # | Stage | Fidelity: what the game state is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rules & Fate | **Symbol.** Chess is war reduced to 64 squares; dice stand in for fate. |
| 2 | Designed Systems | **Model.** Kriegsspiel uses real maps and real unit tables; Monopoly models rent. |
| 3 | Machine Reflex | **Physics.** Tennis for Two computes a real ballistic arc, live. |
| 4 | Character & Story | **Psyche.** A believable person: stats, memory, moral consequence. |
| 5 | Worlds & Emergence | **System.** Economies, cities and ecologies that run without the player (SimCity, The Sims, EVE). |
| 6 | Platforms & Creators | **Society.** Real money, real labor, real culture inside the game (Roblox earnings, Twitch careers). |
| 7 | Life as the Game | **Reality itself.** Real GPS, real heartbeat, real income, real QoL. Simulation fidelity = 100%, because nothing is simulated. |
| 8 | Shared Reality (forecast) | **Reality as medium.** The game edits the world a group lives in. |
| 9 | Civilization Game (forecast) | **Reality as output.** The world is the rendered result of rules people design. |
| 10 | The Self-Playing World (forecast) | **Reality as self.** Designer, player and world coincide: the world plays itself, consciously. |

**Insight (Sasha: «Это ЗОЛОТО», 2026-10-26):** for 7 stages games chased reality; at 7 they catch it. After that, reality starts approaching the game: life designed with the clarity of good game design. That inversion is the value proposition of THE GAME OF BEING YOU.

## Forecast: the next octave (8 and 9)

The invariant keeps widening by one order of magnitude: move → strategy → performance → identity → path → worlds for others → own life → **shared life** → **the rules of civilization**.

| # | Stage | Horizon | Player authors | One-line description | Precursors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Shared Reality (Re) | ~2026–2035 | a shared life | A party or tribe plays its real life together: joint quests, pooled economies, co-created ventures and places; the game is the coordination layer of a community. | Alternate reality games (McGonigal's *World Without Oil*, 2007), Burning Man, DAOs, network states, Masterminds |
| 9 | Civilization Game (Mi) | ~2035– | the rules of civilization | Money, governance, education and cities are designed and iterated as games, with players authoring the rules themselves; Carse's infinite game made operational. | Buckminster Fuller's *World Game* (1960s), mechanism design, quadratic funding, policy sandboxes |

Octave reading: 7 = Do of the new octave, 8 = Re, 9 = Mi. The next **Mi–Fa interval** waits after 9: the leap from authoring civilization's rules to whatever lies beyond the human scale.

**Where we sit:** THE GAME OF BEING YOU is a stage-7 game (your life is the game state) whose multiplayer layer (party, Mastermind, contribution leaderboard, "Finding Your Tribe") is an on-ramp to stage 8.

### Stage 10 (forecast, beyond the Mi–Fa interval)

| # | Stage | Horizon | Player authors | One-line description | Precursors |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | The Self-Playing World (Fa) | not datable | the player itself, at planetary scale | Humanity, with its intelligences, becomes one character with its own uniqueness: a civilization that knows what it uniquely is and plays its part among other living systems and minds. Designer, player and world coincide. | Teilhard's noosphere, Lila (the world as divine play), Gaia, Kardashev's scale |

Mirror of the first octave: there the Mi–Fa interval (3→4) was the moment the **player became a character**. In the new octave the same interval (9→10) is the moment the **civilization becomes a character**. An interval does not happen by itself: it needs an outside shock (in the first octave: story and role-play; here, plausibly, AI as a second kind of mind, contact with the planet's limits, or a crisis of meaning).

### How the forecast was made (method)

1. **Invariant extrapolation.** In stages 1–7 the thing the player authors widens by one order each time (move → strategy → performance → identity → path → worlds for others → own life). The next orders of the holarchy are a shared life (8), the rules of civilization (9), and the civilization as a self (10). Same holonic primitive as FYTT's canvas: person → venture → community → civilization.
2. **Fidelity axis turns over at 7.** Before 7 the game approaches reality; after 7 reality approaches the game: medium (8), output (9), self (10).
3. **Octave law.** The first octave's structure (three notes inside fixed rules, then an interval needing a shock, then a closing Si–Do shock) is mapped onto the second: 7 = Do, 8 = Re, 9 = Mi, interval, 10 = Fa.
4. **Timing by compression.** Stage durations shrink: millennia (1), centuries (2), decades (3–6), ~20 years (6). Stage 7 began ~2023, so 8 is plausibly already starting (its precursors exist) and grows within a decade; 9 is datable only roughly; 10 not at all.

Honest limit: this is extrapolation from a pattern, not data. Precursors confirm the direction, not the dates.
