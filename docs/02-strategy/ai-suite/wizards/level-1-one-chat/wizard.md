## Exit result (proposed, Sasha sets)

**Get bold, decided answers about your situation from any chat.**
The founder briefs an AI like a colleague, iterates to a second version, keeps it short and honest, and has **AI OS v6.0 Lite** ([lite.md](../../../../../system-prompts/v6.0/lite.md)) installed in their chat so this happens by default.

Sasha's line this wizard stands on (client workshop B, closing slide): *«Las herramientas evolucionarán, los principios se quedarán: ver IA como colega y ser · definir la meta · describir el contexto · refinar en ciclos.»* (Tools will change; the principles stay: see AI as a colleague, define the goal, describe the context, refine in cycles.)

## Entry state

Exit of Level 0 (talks about AI without fear or hype), or **none**: anyone with access to one chat model (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or similar) who today uses it like a search box and gets generic answers.

## Micro-modules (proposed, Sasha sets)

| # | Micro-module | Result in one line (button label) | Main source |
|---|---|---|---|
| mm-01 | [Brief like a colleague](./mm-01-brief-like-a-colleague.md) | Brief your AI, get an answer about your situation | «Prompting como poner a alguien al corriente»; input quality = output quality |
| mm-02 | Talk, don't type | Dump your full context by voice in two minutes | «Texto, voz o diálogo · notas de voz»; voice mode vs dialogue mode |
| mm-03 | Roast, then next version | Turn a first answer into a sharper second version | «Haz el roast → produce la siguiente versión»; counter-thesis |
| mm-04 | Tame the quirks | Get short, honest answers with assumptions shown | Quirks and fixes: verbosity, pleasing, hallucination, shallow result |
| mm-05 | Install AI OS Lite | Get bold, decided answers from any chat by default | AI OS v6.0 Lite (= Level 1 setup piece); spec §5 filled example |

Order rule: mm-01 to mm-04 teach by hand what mm-05 then installs as the default, so the founder sees the before/after of Lite with their own eyes (inferred from the ladder principle "the setup piece is handed over when the check passes, not before").
