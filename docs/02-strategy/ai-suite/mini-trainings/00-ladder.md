# AI mini-trainings: the ladder of five (steps 1–2 for each)

> Mini-training constructor (`docs/templates/course-from-source.md`), steps 1–2 (result + structure) for the whole AI ladder. Rung 1 lives in its own file: [01-ai-as-a-mirror.md](./01-ai-as-a-mirror.md) (v0.4). Rungs 2–5 are planned here at the same depth; each gets its own file when Sasha starts it.
> Status: **v0.1** (2026-11-22, Sasha: «Давай сделаем теперь такое же самое планирование для остальных мини-тренингов по ИИ»). For Sasha to edit. No production without his yes.
> Ladder names approved by Sasha (2026-11-22): 1 AI as a Mirror · 2 AI That Knows You · 3 AI in Your Business · 4 Your Harness · 5 AI Company.

## Rules every rung keeps (from rung 1, v0.1 → v0.4)

- 40 minutes at most; one micro-module = one idea, a 90 s video, an «Ask AI» button.
- The button: a one-tap card filled from the profile, editable, microphone as an option, never typing; all buttons in one chat; every prompt asks for up to 5 lines.
- Profile data always after a colon («My offer: …»), never inside a phrase that needs a grammatical case.
- The button lets the person feel the idea, practice one rep, about their own work; it cannot refute the video; AI never lectures about itself.
- Private data (life-area levels) is masked on Copy.
- Each rung ends with a **thing the person keeps** (a check they see with their own eyes), and that is the moment they receive a piece of the setup.

## The line between rungs (fixes the overlap found in rung 1 v0.4)

Rung 1 uses what **the game** knows about the person. Rung 2 has the person **build and carry their own** context, so any AI knows them, not only Your AI. So the ladder goes: AI knows you through the game (1) → you make AI know you anywhere (2) → AI knows your business (3) → your own way of working with AI becomes a system (4) → many agents work as a company (5).

| Rung | Who it is for | They leave with | Setup piece handed over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 AI as a Mirror | after Top Talent | a 3-step plan made with AI + one flattery caught | Your AI chat with their profile |
| 2 AI That Knows You | after rung 1 | their **one-page AI profile** + the habit «goal · context · cycles» | the profile, pasted into any AI |
| 3 AI in Your Business | founders (has a business or idea) | one **living project document** v1.0 for their business, made with AI | project document template + 3 business lenses |
| 4 Your Harness | founders in the collective (proposed) | their own **AI OS Lite + rules of what AI may do without asking + 3 buttons** | AI OS Lite · approval rules · template router buttons |
| 5 AI Company | founders in the collective (proposed) | a **map of their business as a company of agents** + one routine running | company harness, one routine |

## Rung 2 · AI That Knows You

**Result.** In about 35 minutes you have a one-page profile that makes any AI know you, and you know the three moves that make every answer better: goal, context, cycles.

**Done when:** the person pasted their profile into a second AI (not Your AI) and got an answer that sounds like it knows them.

| # | Micro-module | One idea | Promise line | Button (one tap, editable) | What they feel | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personalizing = making yourself known | AI is as good as what it knows about you. | You see why a stranger AI gives average answers. | Two buttons: «Give me advice for my week.» Then: «My mission: {mission}. My priority area: {priority_area}. Now give me advice for my week. Up to 5 lines.» | Average vs mine, side by side. | Workshop B («personalizar es hacerte conocer»); workshop A |
| 2 | Bring it up to speed | A prompt is briefing a colleague, not a magic formula. | You stop hunting for clever words. | «Ask me 5 short questions, one at a time, that a new colleague would need to understand me and my work.» Answer by voice. | Being briefed on, not programmed. | Workshop B (prompting as bringing someone up to speed) |
| 3 | The one-page profile | Identity and roles, 90-day goals, genius, preferences, limits. | You have your profile on one page. | «From everything in this chat, and: talents: {top_talents}, archetype: {archetype}, mission: {mission}: write my one-page AI profile: identity and roles, 90-day goals, genius, how I like answers, my limits. Mark what you guessed.» | «That's me», with my own edits. | Workshop A session 2 (profile schema, «Perfil v1.0») |
| 4 | The layer of «no» | Say what AI must never do or touch. | You set your limits once. | «Ask me 3 questions to find what you must never do for me or with my data. Then add a «No» section to my profile.» | Safe, in charge. | Workshop A session 2 («capa de no») |
| 5 | Goal, context, cycles | Principles that won't expire: name the goal, give the context, refine in cycles. | You have one habit for any AI, any year. | «Here is something I want: [say it]. Before answering, tell me in 3 lines what goal and context you understood, and what's missing.» Microphone first. | It asks back; I sharpen. | Workshop B closing principles |
| 6 | Roast, then next version | Clarity opens in layers: roast it, then produce the next version. | You stop accepting the first answer. | Two buttons: «Roast my profile. Up to 5 lines.» Then: «Produce the next version.» | The second version is clearly better. | Workshop B («haz el roast → produce la siguiente versión») |
| 7 | Take it anywhere | Your profile is yours; it works in any AI. | Any AI knows you in 10 seconds. | **Copy** profile → paste into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini with: «Read my profile. In 3 lines: what would you do differently for me now?» | Portable self. | Training 5 (personalization and memory Q&A) |

Timing ≈ 7 × 3 min + opening/closing ≈ 25–30 min. Hands over: the profile v1.0 (living document, versioned).

## Rung 3 · AI in Your Business

**Result.** In about 35 minutes your business has one living document that AI keeps with you, and you have looked at it through three business lenses.

**Done when:** the person has «[Business] v1.0», one document made with AI, and one next step it gave that they will actually take.

Profile fields added: `{uniqueness}` (canvas_snapshots.uniqueness; if empty: microphone «my business in one line»).

| # | Micro-module | One idea | Promise line | Button (one tap, editable) | What they feel | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One business, one chat, one document | Every project lives in one chat and one versioned document. | You stop losing context across chats. | «My business: {uniqueness}. Ask me 5 questions, one at a time, to write its one-page v1.0.» Answer by voice. | Talking it out is enough. | Training 5 (living docs, «ZoG v1.2»); workshop A session 2 («Proyecto v1.0») |
| 2 | Voice dump → document | Speak for 3 minutes; AI turns it into a clear page. | You never start from a blank page. | Microphone: «Tell everything about your business right now, messy is fine.» Then: «Turn this into v1.0: what, for whom, why me, next step.» | From chaos to a page in 2 minutes. | Workshop A session 2 (voice → PDF → summary) |
| 3 | Lens: the canvas | The business on one canvas shows what's missing. | You see the gap. | «Put my v1.0 on a business canvas. Up to 5 lines: the weakest box and one question to fix it.» | The gap is obvious now. | Training 5 (business lenses) |
| 4 | Lens: the elevator pitch | If you can't say it in 20 seconds, nobody can repeat it. | You have a pitch you can say. | «Write my 20-second pitch from v1.0. Then read it as my ideal client: what's unclear?» | Hearing it from the client's side. | Training 5 (elevator pitch, pitch deck) |
| 5 | Lens: the hard look | Ask for tough love, always with the counter-thesis. | You hear what friends won't say. | «Strongest argument against my business, then one move that answers it. Up to 5 lines.» | Stung, then stronger. | Workshop B (roast with tough love; counter-thesis) |
| 6 | Meetings → commitments | After a call, AI lists who promised what. | Nothing said in a meeting gets lost. | «Here is a note or transcript of my last call: [paste or speak]. List each person's commitments and draft a short follow-up.» | Calls turn into actions. | Workshop B debrief (meeting → commitments) |
| 7 | Save what works | A winning prompt is saved, named and reused. | Your best prompts become your buttons. | «From this chat, pick the 3 prompts that worked best for my business. Name each in 3 words and save them as my buttons.» | My own toolkit. | Workshop B (knowledge retention: save winning prompts) |

Timing ≈ 30 min. Hands over: project document template + the 3 lenses as buttons.

## Rung 4 · Your Harness

**Result.** In about 35 minutes your AI works the way you do: an operating layer, rules of what it may do without asking, and your first buttons.

**Done when:** the same question asked in a plain chat and with their harness gives answers side by side, and the person sees the difference.

| # | Micro-module | One idea | Promise line | Button (one tap, editable) | What they feel | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The model is not the point | Answer quality comes from you, an operating layer and your documents, not from picking a model. | You stop chasing the newest model. | «Same question twice: what should I do this week for: {mission}? First as a plain chat would, then using my profile and v1.0. Up to 5 lines each.» | The layer makes the difference. | docs/ai thesis §0; essay on scaffolds |
| 2 | The operating layer | A few paste-in rules change how AI thinks with you: bold, dense, decided. | Your AI stops hedging. | **Copy** AI OS Lite → paste, then: «Same question as before: what should I do this week?» | Decided, not vague. | AI OS v6.0 Lite |
| 3 | What AI may do without asking | Write down what needs your yes: money, messages to people, anything public. | You hand over work without fear. | «Ask me 4 questions, one at a time, and write my rules: what you may do alone, what you must ask first.» | Trust with a fence. | PISP (approval rules) |
| 4 | Templates know how | A recurring task becomes a template that already knows how. | You stop rewriting the same prompt. | «Which task do I repeat every week? Ask me, then write a template for it: input, steps, output.» | One less chore forever. | template router; meta-template |
| 5 | The console of buttons | Each button looks at your situation through one lens. | You press instead of writing. | «The whole thing in one sentence: my business: {uniqueness}.» (holonic prism, first button) | One tap, a clear view. | holonic prism; notebook («кнопки на пульте») |
| 6 | Wise and fast | Use the wise model for thinking, the fast one for doing. | You pick the right brain for the job. | «For each of my 3 buttons, say: wise model or fast one, and why. One line each.» | Fewer wrong tools. | Workshop A session 2 (model choice) |
| 7 | Monthly clean-up | Old context goes stale; once a month, sweep it. | Your AI doesn't live in last year. | «Read my profile and v1.0. What is probably out of date? List it and ask me to confirm.» | Fresh. | Workshop A session 2 («barrido de obsolescencia») |

Timing ≈ 30 min. Hands over: AI OS Lite · approval rules · 3 buttons.

## Rung 5 · AI Company

**Result.** In about 35 minutes you see your business as a company of AI agents with roles and routines, and one routine already runs without you.

**Done when:** the person has a map of agents with one role each, and one routine scheduled (for example a weekly review) that runs without them.

| # | Micro-module | One idea | Promise line | Button (one tap, editable) | What they feel | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Not a prompt, not even an OS: a company | The next step is AI as a team: roles, rules, routines. | You see a team where you saw a chat. | «My business: {uniqueness}. Draw my business as 5 AI roles, one line each. Which one would free me the most?» | A team appears. | notebook («ИИ-КОМПАНИЯ!»); workshop B part 3 |
| 2 | Tool → companion → ally | AI grows from tool to companion to strategic ally, as you give it context. | You know where you are on the path. | «Where am I with AI now: tool, companion or ally? Why, and one step to the next. Up to 5 lines.» | Honest place on the path. | Workshop B part 3 |
| 3 | It's already happening | No need to force «AI implementation»; start from what people already do. | You stop planning a big rollout. | «Where in my business is AI already used, by me or others? Ask me 3 questions, then list it.» | Already further than I thought. | Workshop B («ya está ocurriendo») |
| 4 | A high-context team member | AI joins the team when it has the documents: values, roles, products, workflows. | You know what to give it. | «For my business, list the 5 documents an AI team member needs, and which one I already have.» | Clear onboarding list. | Workshop B part 3 |
| 5 | One worker, one task | Hand one agent one task with a clear «done». | Your first delegation to an agent. | «Pick one task from my v1.0 that an agent could do alone. Write its brief: goal, context, done when.» | Letting go of one thing. | Hark ⇄ Claude practice; PISP |
| 6 | Routines run without you | A routine is a task that repeats on its own: a weekly review, a check. | Something works while you sleep. | «Design my weekly review routine: what it reads, what it asks me, what it sends. Up to 5 lines.» | Rhythm, not effort. | PISP Part A; notebook (natural rhythms → Equilibrium) |
| 7 | Practice → habit → culture | Leadership in AI is human: one good practice becomes a habit, then culture. | You choose your first practice. | «From all we did, which one practice should become a habit in my team? One line, and how we'll know it stuck.» | It's about people. | Workshop B closing |

Timing ≈ 30 min. Hands over: the company harness (roles map + one routine).
