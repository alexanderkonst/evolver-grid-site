# Mini-training 1 · AI as a Mirror

> Mini-training constructor (`docs/templates/course-from-source.md`), steps 1–2. Ladder of five (Sasha's yes, 2026-11-22): **1 AI as a Mirror** · 2 AI That Knows You · 3 AI in Your Business · 4 Your Harness · 5 AI Company. Only #1 is built now; 2–5 stay one line until #1 is done and the template is updated.
> Format (Sasha, 2026-11-22): 40 minutes at most · each micro-module = one idea, a **90 s** video, an **«Ask AI» button** with a ready question · ~12 micro-modules.
> Status: v0.1 structure, for Sasha to edit. No production (NotebookLM, YouTube) without his yes.

## Step 1 · Result

**After 40 minutes you see AI clearly, without fear and without hype, and you have had your first real conversation with it, by voice.**

Done when: the person has spoken to an AI by voice, told it who they are, and answered its questions.

## Step 2 · Structure (12 micro-modules)

| # | Micro-module | One idea | Promise line | «Ask AI» button | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nothing unnatural | There is one intelligence; AI is a new form of it, not something alien. | You stop seeing AI as a stranger. | «Is there anything unnatural about you? Answer in five lines.» | Training 5 (mindset shift); workshop A |
| 2 | Myth: artificial means fake | «Artificial» comes from *art*, made by skill; Turing called his test «the imitation game». | You hear the word «artificial» differently. | «What does "artificial" really mean? Is your intelligence fake?» | Workshops A, B (myths); Technology 179 |
| 3 | Myth: one day AGI arrives | AI grows step by step; there is no magic day. | You stop waiting for a day that won't come. | «Is there one day when AGI arrives? Draw it as a curve in words.» | Workshops A, B (myths) |
| 4 | Myth: it will destroy us | People choose where AI leads: utopia or dystopia. | You see your own part in where AI goes. | «What decides whether AI leads to a good or a bad future? What depends on me?» | Workshops A, B (utopia / dystopia) |
| 5 | Myth: first I must learn | It is the easiest technology ever: you just speak. | You stop putting it off. | «Teach me to use you in one minute, no jargon.» | Workshops A, B; «no need to be an expert, only an open, clear mind» |
| 6 | Tool, technology, AI | A hammer is a tool, the internet a technology; AI learns and improves itself. | You know what makes AI different. | «How are you different from a hammer and from the internet?» | Training 5 (tool vs technology vs AI) |
| 7 | How it works | A synthetic brain: a network trained on human language. | You can explain AI in two sentences. | «Explain how you work as if to a 12-year-old, in six lines.» | Workshop A (LLM = synthetic brain) |
| 8 | Eight parts of AI | Body, mind, voice, memory, heart, hands, nervous system, mirror of culture. | You see AI as a whole, not one thing. | «Which of your eight parts are strongest and weakest today?» | AI holomap (Your AI system prompt, Self-Awareness) |
| 9 | Seven stages | AI grows by stages; today it is an agentic partner (stage 3). | You know where AI stands now and what comes next. | «Where are you on your road of growth, and what is your next step?» | AI holomap; holomap history |
| 10 | AI's shadows | AI tends to please, to talk too much, to make things up; each has a simple antidote. | You know what to watch for and what to say. | «Which of your weaknesses should I watch for, and what one phrase fixes each?» | Workshop A (quirks + antidotes); shadow map |
| 11 | The mirror | AI answers at the level you speak to it. | You see your own words change the answer. | Two buttons: a vague question, then the same question asked clearly. Compare. | Workshops A, B; Training 5 (AI as mirror) |
| 12 | First real conversation | From tool to relationship: speak to it by voice, like to a colleague. | You have had your first real conversation with AI. | By voice: «Here is who I am and what I'm working on. Ask me three questions to understand me better.» | Training 5 (horizontal collaboration); workshop B |

Timing: 12 × (90 s video + about 90 s with the question) ≈ 36 min, plus a short opening and closing ≤ 40 min.
