## Step 1 · Result

**After 40 minutes you see AI clearly, without fear and without hype, and you have had your first real conversation with it, by voice.**

Done when: the person has spoken to an AI by voice, told it who they are, and answered its questions.

## The «Ask AI» button: why it exists

The video names the idea; the button lets the person **live** it in their own conversation. Each button does three things at once:

1. **Feel.** The person experiences the idea, not hears about it again. (Feel the difference first, then name it.)
2. **Practice.** Each button is one rep of the skill «talking to AI»: own words, real context, a clear question, asking for the counter-argument, letting it ask you. By micro-module 11 the person already knows how.
3. **About you.** The person brings their own work or life into the question («[…]» = fill in your own), so the answer is useful by itself.

**Rule:** the question works in any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Your AI) and **cannot refute the video**, whatever the answer. Never ask AI to lecture about itself.

**Order (hypothesis, to test on people):** button first, then the 90 s video: feel, then get the name.

## What changed from v0.1 (2026-11-22)

- Buttons rewritten: v0.1 asked AI to retell the video (10 of 12 were about AI itself, some could contradict the video, e.g. «is there an AGI day?»). Now each one is felt, practiced and about the person.
- «Eight parts» and «Seven stages» merged into one micro-module (8); the full map moves to rung 4 (Your Harness). Beginners need «very basic, with examples».
- Myths interleaved with doing: every micro-module now has a real action.
- Last micro-module gets ~5 min: a real conversation doesn't fit in 90 s.
- Button-first order added as a hypothesis.
