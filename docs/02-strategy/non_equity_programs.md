# Non-equity programs: the queue

*Grants, fellowships, residencies and prizes that take no equity, ranked by how well they fit Find Your Top Talent. Built 2026-11-03 from three research passes; amounts, terms and deadlines verified on each program's official page unless marked \*. Times in MST (UTC-7, Mazatlán; Mexico has no DST). Re-check terms on the day of applying.*

**Why non-equity:** the product is built as a commons (revenue-share pool, anti-extractive), so selling equity does not fit.

**Eligibility note (our reading, not legal advice):** US funders screen by where the applicant is located (OFAC list), not by passport. Residence in Mexico plus a Delaware C corp formally passes; Mozilla states this explicitly, and Games for Change's rules use the same "located outside OFAC-sanctioned countries" test. MIT Solve excludes people "ordinarily resident" in Russia; residence is Mexico. ITU AI for Good lists both Mexico and the Russian Federation in its application country list. Crypto foundations (W3F, Prezenti/Celo) run KYC/KYB (Sumsub or contract + KYC); a Russian passport may trigger extra review there. Not tested.

**AI-writing rules (new, important for the application loop):** Emergent Ventures now rejects applications that use AI for anything beyond proofreading and says it tests submissions. Mozilla (Incubator and Fellowship) allows AI only for brainstorming, clarity and translation; "heavily or entirely AI-generated" applications get extra review. For these, Sasha writes the text himself; Hark only gathers facts, structures and proofreads.

**Not a fit:**

- Crypto: Web3 Foundation general grants discontinued (official GitHub); NEAR Foundation has no direct grants (near.org/funding lists Protocol Rewards, DevHub, Potlock, Meta Pool, all NEAR-specific); Celo Prezenti S3 (open to 2026-12-29) needs Celo mainnet deployment plus 10K+ daily tx or agent infrastructure, with KYC; Filecoin Open Grants (up to $50K, rolling) must advance the Filecoin ecosystem; Giveth only lists projects for donations, no QF round announced\*; Lens and Farcaster: no foundation grant program found\* (Farcaster has only a community Clanker fund).
