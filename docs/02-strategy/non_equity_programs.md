# Non-equity programs: the queue

*Grants, fellowships, residencies and prizes that take no equity, ranked by how well they fit Find Your Top Talent. Built 2026-11-03 from two research passes; amounts, terms and deadlines verified on each program's official page unless marked \*. Times in MST (UTC-7). Re-check terms on the day of applying.*

**Why non-equity:** the product is built as a commons (revenue-share pool, anti-extractive), so selling equity does not fit.
**Eligibility note (our reading, not legal advice):** US funders screen by where the applicant is located (OFAC list), not by passport. Residence in Mexico plus a Delaware C corp formally passes; Mozilla states this explicitly. MIT Solve excludes people "ordinarily resident" in Russia; residence is Mexico.
