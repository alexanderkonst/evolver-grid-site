## The question

When a founder joins the Sovereign Founder Collective, or runs the program under licence, **where does the 10% go, and what does the page promise?** Today the written sources answer three different ways.

**Therefore:** the public pages promise a treasury and a collective share that no legal document creates, and `/playbook` step 7 offers equity, which Sasha's rule excludes.

## Options

**A. Direct links only.** Each founder's rev share goes to Sasha under a consulting contract. The collective is a network and a rhythm, no money in common.
Gets: simplest, matches SFC and the Three Terms. Cost: drop "treasury" and "to the Collective" from three page texts. Risk: the collective has nothing shared to point to beyond relationships.

**B. A real common treasury.** 10% goes into a pot the members govern together.
Gets: matches `/products` and the artel idea literally. Cost: an entity, a bank account, governance rules. Risk: pooling members' money, which SFC excludes to stay outside fund rules; heavy for three core founders.

**C. Steward now, commons later (pick).** 10% goes to Sasha as steward of the collective; he spends part on shared infrastructure; at ~25 licensed nodes it moves to a purpose entity, then a council (already written in commons_treasury_evolution.md).
Gets: one honest sentence that fits every source and the legal agreement. Cost: rewrite four page texts to say it. Risk: "steward" may read as "goes to Sasha" to some founders.

**Do nothing.** Pages keep promising a treasury that does not exist and equity he does not take. Risk: a founder takes the page at its word.

Recommendation: **C**, вероятно (55–80%) the best fit, because it is already the legal reality and the written trajectory, and it removes the equity line.
