## The one idea

**One house, one shop window.** The collective lives in one place, the Town Hall, which is already the village's place for building ventures together. Every player sees the collective through one shop window, the Market shelf of members' landings. Everything else in the village stays shared by all.

## The path

```
Entry Portal → reveals (unchanged) → Home · Quest Board → Your AI → Workshop · FMF
  → first sale (opens the Town Hall door, as today)
  → Sasha's invitation (makes the player a member)
  → Town Hall = the collective's house
```

One rule: the first sale opens the door, as it does now; membership comes only from Sasha's invitation.

## Town Hall: the collective's house

Everything the collective has sits here, in one place:

| Part | What a member does there |
|---|---|
| 7 Terms | Sees the one line of terms and their own agreement |
| Rev share | Records the 10% as a real deed, so the money lives in the game, not only in the terms text |
| 12 Internal collaborations (key) | Opens or joins a joint offer, shared client or co-built product |
| 4 Scaling experts | Books a named expert |
| 5 Mini-hackathons | Joins a dated hackathon; what ships lands in the deeds feed (A16) |
| 11 Shared improvement budget | Sees what it improves this moon |
| 6 Rhythm | Collective dates sit on the moon ring of the four-ring clock (A9) |

A player who opened the door by a first sale but is not yet a member sees the house and what members do in it, so they know what they are invited into.

## Market: the shop window for everyone

Part 13, mutual promotion: each member's landing sits on a shelf in the Market, open to every player. It is living proof that a unique business works, and the reason a new founder wants into the collective.

## Stays shared, not split

Co-working (matchmaking), Café, Your AI, Academy stay one space for all players. Too few players to split the network.
