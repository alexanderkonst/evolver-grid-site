## Rule for this doc

A collective part goes into the game only when it has a clear use case: a real player, a real moment, a real action. No place gets built for the collective until then.

## The one use case so far

**Market shelf of members' landings (part 13, mutual promotion).** A new founder in the game sees real people with working unique businesses and can go to their landings. Members get clients; players get proof and a reason to want in. The Market already exists and is open to everyone.

## Everything else

Lives outside the game for now (with Sasha, in real life), until it shows a use case: terms and rev share, collaborations, experts, hackathons, shared budget, train-the-trainer, the fund. Shared game places (Co-working, Café, Your AI, Academy) are not split into members and others.
