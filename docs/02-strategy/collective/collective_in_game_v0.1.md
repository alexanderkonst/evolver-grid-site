## The one idea

The collective is not a new section of the game. It is the **Earth quarter coming alive**, plus a members' layer in the places that already exist. A player sees the collective lit up ahead the whole way; it opens by a real deed and an invitation, like every place in the village opens by a real deed.

## The path through the village

```
Entry Portal → reveals (unchanged) → Home · Quest Board        Ether
  → Your AI: rungs 1–3 of the AI ladder                        Air
  → Workshop (unique business) · FMF fire circle               Earth · Fire
  → [deed: Promise + first offer] + [invitation] → MEMBER
  → Town Hall = the collective's house                          Earth
```
