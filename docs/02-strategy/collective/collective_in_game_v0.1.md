## The decision

**Collective is its own space in the game.** It opens when a person reaches the point of a working unique business.

## Two ways in

1. **Through the game.** A player walks the path (reveals → Your AI → unique business) and reaches a working unique business. Then Collective opens.
2. **Straight in.** Sasha invites founders who already have a working unique business: his peers, or people further along. They are offered the founder collective right away, without walking the earlier path.

## What is inside

The parts listed in the architecture: Founder Cockpit, AI harness, matchmaking, scaling experts, mini-hackathons, rhythm, terms, shared infrastructure, media synergy, shared improvement budget, internal collaborations, mutual promotion, train-the-trainer, the fund later. Which of them show inside the space first is decided part by part, each by its use case.
