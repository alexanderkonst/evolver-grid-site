## The one line

A collective of founders, each building a business only they could run, with everything a founder needs to grow it in one place: the method, the cockpit, the AI, each other, experts for scale, and a shared rhythm.

## How a founder moves through it

```
Game (Top Talent → Mission → Assets)          ← unchanged, the door
        ↓
Unique Business (Sasha's method, FMF)         ← the founder finds the business
        ↓
COLLECTIVE (by invitation)
  ├─ 1 Founder Cockpit      run the business
  ├─ 2 AI harness           the business does more without hiring
  ├─ 3 Matchmaking          the right people meet
  ├─ 4 Scaling experts      grow past the first clients
  ├─ 5 Mini-hackathons      build a real thing together in a day
  └─ 6 Rhythm               all of it on one lunar cycle
        ↓
Terms: 10% rev share, steward now → member-governed entity (2027–28). No equity.
```

## How the parts feed each other

- The game and the method make a founder; the cockpit runs the business; the AI multiplies it; matchmaking connects businesses; experts scale them; hackathons ship pieces; the rhythm holds the pace. Shared infrastructure and the shared budget carry everyone; media makes each member louder; internal collaborations are the heart: what members can only do together. Mutual promotion brings each member clients; train-the-trainer makes growth exponential; the fund, later, brings capital.
- Each part's result is visible: that is what the landing shows, part by part.
- The founders who grow become the ones who lead the next (trainers, nodes): this is how the 10% moves to the entity (terms roadmap).

## What this architecture does not change

- The Top Talent, Mission and Assets reveals and how a player enters the game.
- Sasha's Unique Business method, sessions and /playbook.
