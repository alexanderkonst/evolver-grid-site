## What it is

A small circle of founders, each building a unique business: one only they could run, because it grows out of their own gift. We work on the same method, keep the same rhythm, and help each other where our businesses meet.

- **Same method.** Uniqueness → Shadow → Myth → Tribe → Promise → offer → landing → first clients ([the path](/path)).
- **Same rhythm.** [Slot, open question С5: shared lunar rhythm or one-to-one?] Draft line: we plan and review by the lunar month: quiet weeks to gather and plant, loud weeks to lead and harvest.
- **Each other.** Introductions, joint offers and shared clients where two businesses fit together.

## Who it is for

Founders who have walked the path at least to a clear Promise and a first offer, and who want to keep building with others rather than alone. Membership is by invitation.

## Terms

- **10% revenue share** once your business earns. No equity, ever, and no pooling of anyone's investment money.
- **Today** the 10% is held by Aleksandr as steward of the collective and partly funds shared infrastructure.
- **Over 2027–2028** his roles pass step by step to members: trainers, founders running the program for others, people running operations.
- **Later** the 10% moves to an entity that cannot be sold and that the members govern.

The gift is free, the system is paid, the name is permanent.
