A small circle of founders, each building a unique business: one only they could run, because it grows out of their own gift. Same method, same rhythm, and each other.

## 4. What you get inside

Each card: name · what it gives · result · status tag.

1. **Collaborations** (the heart, visually larger): joint offers, shared clients, co-built products, referrals. Result: work a solo founder can't get alone. *Coming.*
2. **Introductions**: the right people meet: clients, partners, mentors. Result: introductions that turn into work. *Live in the game.*
3. **Founder Cockpit**: one screen to run your business: offers, clients, the one next move. Result: a business that is steered, not chased. *Live.*
4. **Your AI**: a ladder from AI as a mirror to your own AI company. Result: the business does more without hiring. *First steps live.*
5. **Shared rhythm**: the lunar month. Quiet weeks to gather and plan, strong weeks to lead and harvest. Result: one beat, no meetings for meetings' sake. *Live.*
6. **Mutual promotion**: each member's page shown here, each member introduced by the others. Result: new clients from the collective's audience. *Coming.*
7. **Scaling experts**: sales, marketing, finance, legal, operations. Result: the next stage of revenue with an expert beside you. *Coming.*
8. **Mini-hackathons**: one day or a weekend; founders ship one real piece together (landing, offer, funnel, AI routine). Result: a shipped thing by evening. *Coming.*

## 6. Terms

- 10% revenue share, only once your business earns. No equity.
- Capped: when your revenue grows 10x, we sit down and set the terms again, on both sides. Same at every next 10x.
- Today Aleksandr holds it as steward of the collective; it partly funds the shared tools.
- Over 2027–2028 his roles pass step by step to members.
- Later the 10% moves to an entity the members govern and nobody can sell.

*The gift is free, the system is paid, the name is permanent.*
