## The shape we are moving toward

- **Linux Foundation:** a neutral body stewards the open core (the game, the method, the platform); its founder is paid, not an owner. ([connector.md](../modular_collaboration/connector.md) uses the same model for partners.)
- **Red Hat:** a commercial company lives around the open core and pays its way by serving clients. Here, each founder's unique business is that company.
- **What stays from the steward-ownership path:** the entity cannot be sold; no equity, no investors, no exit (Sasha's rule, 2026-11-04: capped rev share or debt only).

To align later: commons_treasury_evolution.md says "This is not the Linus path" (because the 10% is a revenue share, not corporate membership). Both hold if Linux Foundation names the **governance** shape and rev share stays the **money** mechanism.

## What holds at every stage

1. **The gift is free, the system is paid, the name is permanent** ([the_three_terms.md](../../05-reference/the_three_terms.md)).
2. **No equity, no pooling of anyone's investment money** ([sovereign_founder_collective.md](../sovereign_founder_collective.md)).
3. **Deals already made by hand are honoured until they close** (client 1: 30% to the first $5,555, then 10%; a $277 rev share with another founder). New members join on the one public line.
