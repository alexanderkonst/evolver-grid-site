## Research limits and next use

- Public offers and biographies establish what people claim and provide useful fit evidence; revenue, results and credentials were not independently audited.
- Search-indexed LinkedIn content can lag live profiles. Undated founding offers, cohort announcements and relative post dates are not proof of current availability.
- No assumptions about private spiritual practice, psychological readiness or personal finances were used as qualification facts.
- These are 20 unique individuals plus 5 organizations with 5 additional named contacts. Four organization contacts have verified public LinkedIn URLs; Robert Stefan has a verified founder website connection only.
- Begin with a few specific conversations. Record what the person corrects, whether an opening is real, and which next step they choose. Do not equate this shortlist with 25 sales opportunities.
