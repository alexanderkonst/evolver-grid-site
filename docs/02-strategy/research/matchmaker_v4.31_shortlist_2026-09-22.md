## What to learn from the first conversations

For each authorized conversation, record: the person's correction to the hypothesis; whether a relevant group or individual exists; the outcome they actually want; whether current provision already covers it; who would pay; and the next step they choose. Distinguish paid delivery, contributed hosting, referral, own-business work and peer exchange. A useful first result can be a clear no, a not-now with agreed follow-up, or a small scoped proposal—not simply a warm exchange.

**The stronger output of v4.31 is a smaller set of specific possibilities with explicit disqualifying evidence and payment questions. It is not a stronger claim that these people will buy.**
