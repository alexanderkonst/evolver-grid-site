# Ready Player One → GoBY

Filled: 2026-11-21 · Status: draft, awaiting Sasha
Almanac stage: 7 (a world players live and build in) · Fidelity: the novel is fiction; evidence comes from what real people did with its mechanics (Cline's 2012 contest) and from the closest real OASIS (Roblox)
One line: a dead founder hides an Easter egg in the world he built; whoever knows his life well enough to pass three gates inherits the world. The world itself (OASIS) is built by its users.
Sasha's direction (2026-11-21, this session): players get the right to change the game gradually: interiors, new buildings, their own modules, quests. Focus first on the games closest to this: Ready Player One, Heroes III, LinkedIn, Facebook. "No mush."

## 2. Mechanics that demonstrably worked

### M1. Gates hidden in the founder's world (the Easter egg hunt)
- **In the book:** Halliday hides three keys and three gates; only someone who studied his life and loves deeply what he loved can pass them.
- **Real evidence (grade B for the funnel, small n):** Cline hid a URL in the paperback (contest 2012-06-04 → 2012-08-09). Gate 1: an Atari 2600 game; gate 2: a Facebook game by Richard Garriott; gate 3: a world record on a classic arcade game. 1,329 people passed gate 1, 759 passed gate 2, 24 declared an attempt on gate 3; Craig Queen won a DeLorean with a Joust record [4][5][6]. People spent hours a day on it for weeks [7].
- **Driver:** a mystery someone you admire left for you; each gate filters and raises status; scarcity of finishers.

### M2. A world its players build (OASIS → Roblox, the closest real one)
- **In the book:** OASIS planets, schools and shops are made by users; the world is the sum of what they built.
- **Real evidence (grade B, company 10-K):** Roblox: 82.9M average daily users in 2024 [8]; creators earned $922.8M in 2024 and $1,503.1M in 2025; 35,500+ creators in its payout program at end of 2025 [9]. Roblox's own account of why: better creator content attracts more users, which attracts more creators [8]. The catch: the median creator in the payout program got $1,575 for 2024 [10]; income concentrates at the top.
- **Driver (grade A, lab):** the IKEA effect. People who assembled a plain IKEA box bid $0.78 vs $0.48 for an identical ready box, a 63% premium; novices valued their origami near expert work. **It vanished when they failed to finish or their creation was destroyed** [11]. Labor leads to love only when it ends in something finished.

### M3. One avatar, anonymous (OASIS)
- Not a fit: GoBY's canon is the real person behind the avatar (§21.2, §23.2 AI avatar from the player's photo). Dropped.

## 4. Where it lands: the creation ladder (Sasha: gradually)

Each rung opens only after a real milestone, so labor ends in something finished (IKEA boundary) and the game doesn't turn into mush.

| Rung | What the player may change | Opens after | Lands at | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Interior of Your House:** place earned artifacts/badges, arrange rooms | first badge (Reveal) | A12 shelf, §23.2, A5 | High (18) |
| 2 | **Write a quest for the next player** from a milestone you passed ("I did it this way, here's your step"), reviewed by Sasha or two players who passed the same step | passing that milestone | §23 principle extended to all players | Medium (12) |
| 3 | **Your own building / module** in the village (a practice, a circle, an academy class) | a priced offer or a first sale (J11), i.e. a real practitioner | A11 village places, A7 | Low now (6): later |

Rung 2 is the strongest new idea: it turns §23 ("Sasha's path becomes the player's quest") into a chain: everyone who passes a step can light it for the next. It's how Couchsurfing references and Wayfarer reviews scaled: players reviewing players.

## 5. Smallest testable version
- Rung 1: Your House lets the owner move the badges they've earned (no new art needed). Signal: share of players with a badge who rearrange at least once.
- Rung 2 (no code): ask 3 players who passed the Reveal to write the micro-quest for the next player in their own words; Sasha picks; show it to the next 10. Signal: completion of their quest vs the current A4 micro-quest.

## 6. Risks and anti-patterns
- **Mush:** open creation without gates fills the world with half-made things. Ladder + review.
- **Unfinished work kills the effect** [11]: a quest draft that never goes live leaves its author with less love, not more. Only open a rung when finishing is easy.
- **Creator income concentrates** (Roblox median $1,575 [10]): never promise players income from rung 3.
- **Trivia gates:** Easter eggs that test knowledge of Sasha instead of real progress turn the path into fandom.
- **Protecting Sasha's designs** (§23.2 open question) applies to rung 1 artifacts.
