# Real-life iconic games: candidate list for GoBY

Brief (Sasha, 2026-11-21): «надо брать игры которые сделаны под реальную жизнь. Самые культовые по максимуму.»
Written 2026-11-21. Excludes what the registry already covers or queues (Heroes III, Couchsurfing, Ready Player One, Duolingo, Pokémon GO/Ingress, LinkedIn, Facebook) and Jane McGonigal's games (analysed separately).

Ranking = (a) iconic status: players, years alive, money, press; × (b) how directly the game's moves are real acts with real people.
Evidence grade: **A** study · **B** hard numbers (official counts, records) · **C** consensus or company claim.
Figures are as published; company figures are graded C unless they are counts of a verifiable thing (certificates, registrations).

## Top 10

| # | Game | What the player does in real life | Iconic evidence (grade, source) | Mechanic most worth analysing for GoBY | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scouting badges** (Baden-Powell, 1908) | Learns a real skill (first aid, camping, cooking, a trade), shows it to an adult who checks it, sews the badge on | ~60M members est. 2025, 176 national orgs, record high (C, WOSM estimate: https://www.scout.org/news/news/world-scoutings-membership-reaches-historic-highs); 57M official-census basis (B, https://www.scout.org/); 500M+ people ever (C, https://www.scout.org/who-we-are/scout-movement/scoutings-history); proficiency badges 1908, 57 merit badges in 1911 handbook (C, https://scoutmasterbucky.com/history/) | **The badge is signed off by a person who checks the real skill** (counselor / leader), not by an app | 117 years of a badge economy that never drifted into vanity because every badge is a demonstrated act witnessed by a human. Direct template for a Journey step being closed by a villager, not by a tap |
| 2 | **Camino de Santiago: credential + Compostela** | Walks (or cycles) the route, collects stamps (sellos) from albergues, churches, cafés; shows the stamped credential in Santiago to receive the Compostela | 530,987 Compostelas in 2025, record, +6% on 2024; 4th record year in a row; 93,929 in 2005 → 5.7× in 20 years (B, Pilgrim's Office via https://santiago.es/en/news/record-pilgrims-2025 and https://caminantes.co/guides/how-many-people-walk-the-camino-de-santiago); rule: last 100 km on foot / 200 km by bike, proved by stamps (B, https://www.oursundayvisitor.com/spains-famed-camino-sets-record-with-half-a-million-pilgrims-in-2024/) | **The stamped passport: proof of presence collected from real hosts along the way, redeemed at the end for a certificate issued by people** | The purest "the game is the walk" design at half a million players a year, growing with zero marketing. Every stamp is a real place and a real person. Maps onto GoBY's village places: a passport stamped by the café, the stall, the Fire Circle |
| 3 | **parkrun** (2004) | Shows up every Saturday at 9:00 for a free, timed 5 km in a local park: runs, walks, or volunteers | 12M+ registrations, ~2,800 events in 23 countries, 1 millionth event June 2026 (B, Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/sports/amazing-parkrun-celebrates-millionth-event-2026-06-11 ); average 450,000 weekly participants FY2025/26, 100k+ new volunteers (C, https://www.parkrun.com/about/the-organisation/annual-report/); 22 years alive | **Milestone clubs that count persistence, not performance, and count volunteering equal to running** (25, 50, 100 … 1,000; each a coloured T-shirt others can see) (B, https://support.parkrun.com/hc/en-us/articles/205904361 ; history: https://blog.parkrun.com/uk/2026/09/16/the-history-of-milestones) | Fixed weekly ritual + visible milestones + "helping counts as playing" grew 13 runners into 12M with no fee. Directly answers how GoBY should count contribution (hosting, helping) alongside one's own steps |
| 4 | **Burning Man + Regional Network** (1986) | Builds a temporary city with others for a week, gives gifts, makes art; or runs a local "burn" at home | 72,181 in Black Rock City 2025 (B, census via https://camperchamp.com/usa/burning-man-statistics/); ~110,000 at 96 Regional Events in 30+ countries 2025 (C, https://burningman.org/news/press-media/press-kit/); 106 regions in 34 countries (C, https://burningman.org/global-events-groups/burning-man-regional-network/); "1M+ have participated" (C); 40 years | **10 Principles as the whole rulebook + volunteer Regional Contacts who run local events under them** (Principles written 2004 *for* the Regional Network: https://burningman.org/about-us/10-principles) | A culture that scales city by city through volunteers holding shared principles, not through an app. Template for how GoBY villages beyond San Pancho could be started by locals under one short canon (gifting, participation, leave no trace) |
| 6 | **Strava** (2009) | Runs, rides, walks; records it; races friends and strangers on the same stretch of road ("segments"); gives kudos | 180M users Dec 2025, 14B kudos, 7.6M KOM/QOM/course records in 2025 (C, company: https://press.strava.com/articles/strava-releases-12th-annual-year-in-sport-trend-report-2025 , https://www.bikeradar.com/news/strava-year-in-sport-2025); 200M claimed mid-2026 (C, https://business.strava.com/why-strava/audience); revenue ~$415M 2025 (C, estimate: https://www.businessofapps.com/data/strava-statistics) | **Segments with a "Local Legend" crown for most efforts in 90 days, beside the speed crown** (Local Legend rule from Strava's own feature, not fetched here: C) | Turns a real stretch of road into a shared, contested place. The Local Legend variant rewards showing up over being fastest, which fits GoBY's contribution-only leaderboards (registry candidate #8) |
| 7 | **Mafia / Werewolf** (Moscow State University, 1986) | Sits in a circle with others; a minority secretly knows each other; in the "day" everyone argues face to face and votes | Folk game spread by word of mouth from MSU dorms and summer camps → Europe 1990s → US; Werewolf theme 1997; basis of *The Traitors* TV franchise; used as AI theory-of-mind benchmark (C, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mafia_(party_game) , https://www.vulture.com/article/mafia-creator-dimma-davidoff-the-traitors.html); 40 years. No credible total-player count exists | **A host-run circle ritual (night / day, speak in turn, vote) that turns strangers into a talking group in 15 minutes with zero equipment** | The most-played real-people game born in Sasha's own culture, spread with no company at all. Worth analysing the ritual structure (moderator, rounds, turn-taking), not the lying, for GoBY's Fire Circle |
| 8 | **Reddit Secret Santa (redditgifts)** (2009–2021) | Gets matched to a stranger, reads what they post about, buys and mails a fitting gift; posts a photo of what they received | Guinness record: 89,421 completed participants 2013 (B, https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/largest-online-secret-santa-game); 122,000 signed up from 163 countries (C, https://usustatesman.com/worldrecord-secret-santa-reaches-usu-community/); $1.8M spent on gifts + shipping 2012 (B/C, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/reddit-smashes-own-secret-santa-record-with-a-whopping-58502-participants-across-126-countries-184272551.html); shut 2021, community revived it (B, https://www.npr.org/2024/12/23/nx-s1-5235596/reddit-secret-santa-gift-exchange-christmas) | **Matching + "read their profile to choose a gift that fits them" + public thank-you photo** | Tens of thousands of strangers trusting each other with real gifts once a year. Closest model for GoBY gifting: a gift chosen from what a villager has said they love, closed by a visible thanks. Lesson in its death: scale without local ties broke it |
| 9 | **Humans vs Zombies** (Goucher College, 2005) + Assassin/Killer lineage | Plays a week-long game of tag across campus *during normal life*: classes, meals, walking home; tagged humans become zombies; daily missions | 1,000+ locations on six continents (C, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humans_vs._Zombies); 2,282 games, 141,000 registered players by 2015 (C, organiser claim in https://dailyillini.com/uncategorized/2015/04/29/humans-vs-zombies-a-chilling-game-of-tag); lineage: MIT Assassins' Guild since 1982 (B, https://assassin.scripts.mit.edu/wiki/The_MIT_Assassins'_Guild) | **The game layered on ordinary days: one week, missions at set times, the whole campus as the board** | Shows people will carry a game through their real routine for a week if it has a start, an end and daily missions. Relevant to GoBY's week as the unit (H3-5); the infection/conflict frame itself does not fit |
| 10 | **Apple Watch Activity rings** (2015) | Moves, exercises and stands enough each day to close three rings; shares rings with friends, 7-day one-on-one competitions | Causal study, 76B minutes from 160,000 people: the stand nudge raises standing by up to 43.9%, and ring-closers respond almost 3× more (A, Science Advances 2023: https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adi1752); "close your rings" is a mass catchphrase (C, https://www.apple.com/watch/close-your-rings); Apple designed sharing "for support, not leaderboards" (C, Jay Blahnik: https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/jay-blahnik-what-separates-apple-watch-other-fitness-trackers) | **Three small daily goals shown as rings that visibly close, shared with friends who can send a high-five** | The strongest causal evidence on this list. The act is real but mostly solo, so it ranks last; the lesson is the visual (a closing ring) and support-first sharing, which overlaps DL-1 streaks |

## Considered, not in the top 10

- **BookCrossing** (2001): 1.2M members, 13M+ books "released into the wild" with a tracking ID (B/C, https://www.bookcrossing.com/about , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BookCrossing). Strong GoBY fit (follow a gift through strangers), weaker iconic. First reserve.
- **Habitica** (2013): "4M+" users (C, https://habitica.com/); party takes damage when one member skips a Daily. Real tasks, but the people are online party members, not neighbours.
- **Foursquare / Swarm mayorships**: iconic in 2010–2014, mayorships removed then restored; vanity check-ins, no real act with people. Not searched for numbers.
- **Zombies, Run!, Nike Run Club, Fitbit challenges, Forest, Finch, Tamagotchi**: real or care acts, but solo; Tamagotchi's care is for a virtual pet.
- **ARGs (The Beast 2001, Cicada 3301, Year Zero)**: high iconic, but the acts are puzzle-solving online; RPO-1 already covers the hidden-quest pattern.
- **LARP, Jet Lag-style chase, Turf**: real-world, but no comparable sourced scale found in this pass.

## Gaps

- Mafia/Werewolf has no reliable total-player figure; its iconic status rests on spread, longevity and press (C).
- Strava's Local Legend rule and Scouting's counselor sign-off are stated from general knowledge of the products, not from a page fetched in this pass (C).
- Burning Man regional and lifetime figures are the organisation's own (C); Black Rock City counts are census-based (B).
