## Four kinds of "level" (used in the table)
- **(a) Content unit:** a chapter of the world (Mario 1-1, a Duolingo unit). Says *where you are*.
- **(b) Earned right:** a step that opens what you may *do* (edit others' posts, teach, promote).
- **(c) Public rank:** a status others see and compare (belt colour, title, league).
- **(d) Number:** a running score (XP, Elo, reputation) that feeds (b) or (c).

## Pattern across the eight
1. Levels matter where they **open something real**: a right to act (LV-1, LV-2, LV-6), access to people and places (LV-5), or a chapter of the world (LV-4 units, LV-8). Where a level opened nothing, even WoW cut it (LV-5).
2. The strongest promotions are **made by people already on the step** after watching a real act (LV-1, LV-6), or by peers' votes on real work (LV-2).
3. Public comparison (Elo, leagues) does drive activity (LV-4: +17% time) but fails our canon: it ranks people.

## Verdict
- **Kind (b), earned right, fits GoBY**, and only on the **constructor ladder** (in-game #5), not as a new "level" scale. Concrete: each rung (interior → building → quests for others → own village) is a *right*; the next rung opens when **two people already on that rung confirm one real deed they saw** (from LV-1, LV-6; two, not one, to avoid a single veto). No minimum time, no points. Name them **steps/rungs**, never "levels": §19.6 already warns of three competing level scales.
- **Kind (a)** is already canon (Journey J1–J12, chapters). Nothing to add.
- **Kind (c) and (d)** rejected: ranks people or adds artificial XP. The one keeper, "earned once, kept for life" (LV-3, LV-6), is already canon as Peak and badges.
- **Spec landing:** the new constructor row (from in-game #5) gets one done-when line: "rung N+1 opens when two rung-N players confirm a real deed". No change to §10 private level (LV-7 confirms it).
- **Open for Sasha:** can a rung be paused if someone harms the village (SO and Masonry both allow removal), or is a rung permanent?
