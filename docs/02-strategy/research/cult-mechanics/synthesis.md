## Dropped (12) and why
Like and reaction counts, News Feed, friend count, minutes-in-app as meaning, sign-ups as proof (Facebook: numbers with no real act; feed works against real life). Who viewed your profile, one-click endorsements, contact import (LinkedIn: vanity count, no link to skill, $13M settlement over emails sent in members' names). Walking as currency (Pokemon GO: steps aren't our act, effect fades by week 6). Anonymous avatar (RPO: we're real people). Hero XP skills, tournaments (Heroes III: artificial XP, ranking people against each other).
