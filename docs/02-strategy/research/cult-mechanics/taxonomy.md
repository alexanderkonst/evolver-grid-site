# Integral holonic taxonomy of social engineering (v1, 2026-11-21)

Premise (Sasha, 2026-11-21): everything is a game; reality already is one, the question is how aware we are of it. So we learn mechanics not only from video games but from any social structure that proved itself, best over many years. See [game_design_almanac.md § Everything is a game](../../game_design_almanac.md#everything-is-a-game-sasha-2026-11-21).

The taxonomy has two parts. **Part A: sources** — what kinds of "games" we learn from (three levels: class → kind → examples). **Part B: map** — where each mechanic we take lands in our game (holon × quadrant × rhythm).

# Part A. Sources: what we learn from

Lens for every source (filled when it is analysed): **holon** it works at (H1 person … H6 civilization), **quadrant** it is strongest in (I / IT / WE / ITS), **longevity** (years it kept working), **evidence grade** (A/B/C). Examples below are candidates, not analyses: the "learn" line is the question we take to it, not a finding.

## A6. Long-lived social structures and dynamics
| Kind | Examples | What to learn |
|---|---|---|
| A6.1 Self-governing communities | kibbutz, Mondragon cooperatives, Quaker meetings, open-source projects (Linux, Apache) | decisions without a boss; a maintainer who holds quality |

Rule for choosing the next source: longevity and evidence first (structures that kept working for decades), then fit to a gap in Part B. ✓ = already analysed.

# Part B. Map: where mechanics land

Purpose: one map where every mechanic we study has an address. Two axes.
- Holon (scale, each whole is part of the next): H1 Person -> H2 Pair -> H3 Circle (5-15, has a host) -> H4 Village (a real place, ~150) -> H5 Network of villages -> H6 Civilization (Foundation layer).
- Quadrant (integral, Wilber AQAL): I = inner of one (meaning, motivation, identity); IT = outer of one (acts, body, skills, proof); WE = inner of many (culture, trust, rituals, charter); ITS = outer of many (rules, roles, places, infrastructure, money).
- Third axis, time: day / week / month / year (accepted Rhythm).

Rule from the research: quality at every holon is held by a person who already walked that path (accepted principle #15).

## Gaps seen in the map (where our study is thin)
- H3 Circle WE/ITS: governance (host succession, decisions, conflict) -> Sasha's templates: group formation from zero, integral leadership, founders-into-one-product.
- H5/H6: almost empty. Candidates: Burning Man regionals, Scouting national structure, Foundation, Wikipedia governance.
- I quadrant at H3-H5: what a group feels as "we". Rituals, songs, origin story.
