## The whole in one sentence
Initiation is a consented return to one's etalon: the person is called by name, steps out of the ordinary, pays a real but safe cost, is shown what is already true about them and feels it, is witnessed and marked, comes back with a gift to give, and is walked through the dip that follows, again and again, up a ladder.

## 1. Organizing principle: the return is the rite
Every tradition agrees on three movements (separation → threshold → incorporation; van Gennep, Turner, Eliade, Campbell). The research adds what they underplay: **the change fails at the return, not at the peak.** Outward Bound effects grow after the program (+0.17 at follow-up, 1997 meta-analysis, A) when there is a return; programs that skip it lose the effect; the psychedelic «afterglow» lasts ~6–8 weeks and «without active effort, lessons fade» (B); Campbell: «dreams… momentous by night may seem simply silly in the light of day». Sasha's devaluation dip is this law, seen from inside. So the rite is designed from the return backwards.

In the Etalon system: initiation does not add an identity, it returns the person to their own measure. What dies is the borrowed self; what is born is the recognized one.

## 3. The etalon ritual (the most powerful form, technology-neutral)
Seven movements, each answering the invariants once. Plain names first (the secular surface), the depth in brackets.

1. **The Call** [being named]. An invitation that speaks to the person's longing to be themselves, with the cost and the path stated up front. A free yes. *(I1, I12)*
2. **The Threshold** [separation]. A clear step out of the ordinary: a quiet space, a moment of stillness, the familiar dropped (title, world, no distractions). *(I2)*
3. **The Offering** [the cost]. The person gives something true: their own story, their own words, the honest answer. Effort and truth, never pain or money as the price of being seen. *(I3)*
4. **The Mirror** [revelation, darkness then light]. What is already true about them is shown: the gift and its shadow together, then silence. The felt moment is caught, body first, then their own word (Apple Seed v2). *(I4, I5)*
5. **The Mark** [rebirth, new name]. A name or sign they shape themselves and the community recognizes; a token that is a real capability. *(I7)*
6. **The Return** [incorporation]. Back into the village with a role and a first gift to give; witnesses who matter see it. *(I6, I8)*
7. **The Proving** [integration through the dip]. The dip is named in advance («in a few days the words will feel ordinary, and your mind may call them nothing; that is the old program, and it is the next gate»). The person re-meets the feeling, repeats the act before witnesses, and each proving opens the next degree, up to pulling Excalibur. *(I9, I10)*

Running through all seven: a **guide** who has crossed (Sasha, the AI as a witness that asks, never supplies) and **one symbol with two readings** (I11). Guard rails throughout (I12).

## 5. What we cut
Severity as proof. Secrecy as power. Mandatory belief. Gurus and titles. Peak without return. Recognition conditional on paying or recruiting. Pseudo-historical claims (no documented pharaonic initiation; pyramid initiation legends come from Blavatsky and a 1731 novel).

## 6. Acceptance test
A first-timer goes through the seven movements and, 30 days later: can still evoke the feeling, has met the dip and stayed, has a mark the community recognizes, and has given one gift from it.
