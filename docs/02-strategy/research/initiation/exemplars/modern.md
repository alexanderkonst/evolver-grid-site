## 1. Robert L. Moore: ritual elders and sacred space
**Header.** Robert L. Moore (Jungian analyst, Chicago), *The Archetype of Initiation: Sacred Space, Ritual Process, and Personal Transformation* (2001). In as **core theory**: why modern initiation fails without ritual leadership.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) life crisis | challenge breaks the old order | life itself | disoriented |
| (b) sacred space/time | container for "needed reorganization" | prepared ritual elder | held, reorganized |
| (c) return | "newly transformed personal and social world" | elder + community | renewed purpose |
Source for the three steps: https://robertmoore-phd.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=page.display&page_id=47 ; return "with renewed understanding and sense of purpose": https://archive.org/stream/robert-moore-facing-the-dragon/Robert%20Moore%20-%20Facing%20the%20Dragon_djvu.txt (excerpt).

- Q1 Call: the crisis is the call (page_id=47 above).
- Q2 Separation: entry into "sacred space" distinct from profane, after Eliade/Turner/van Gennep (same).
- Q3 Ordeal: elder keeps the heat high while "stewarding the boundaries" so people don't run (Rohr citing Moore, http://www.natcath.org/NCR_Online/archives2/2002a/021502/021502k.htm, excerpt).
- Q4 Revelation: not addressed specifically.
- Q5 Death/rebirth: implied in reorganization; not detailed in sources opened.
- Q6 Witness: central. Without elders initiation cannot happen; elders have "abdicated… wholesale" (https://robertmoore-phd.com/index.cfm?fuseaction=page.display&page_id=37).
- Q7 Mark: not addressed.
- Q8 Return: into service of "an inclusive… vision about a world of justice and peace" (page_id=37).
- Q9 Integration: an "order, a brotherhood" that holds a man at 40, in illness, at death; "not… a place for men to go through their mid-life crises and leave" (page_id=37).
- Q10 Degrees: lifelong, inter-generational (page_id=37).
- Q11 Surface/depth: sacred space need not be religious (*Judgment*, video gloss https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4unDD4OUUNQ, excerpt).
- Q12 Risks: a bogus vision captures warrior energy (SS, militias) (page_id=37); group narcissism in initiatory orgs, incl. MKP (http://menstuff.org/columns/overboard/moore.html).
- Q13: unritualized need returns as "pseudo-ritual": addictions (menstuff).

**Mechanics.** (1) *Container held by a trained elder*: crisis → bounded sacred space → return; C; driver: holding tension long enough for reorganization without flight; page_id=47. (2) *Standing order for after-care*: C; page_id=37.
**Failures/criticism.** Mythopoetic men's movement criticized as essentialist and appropriating Native rites (Kimmel ed., *The Politics of Manhood*, https://xyonline.net/sites/xyonline.net/files/2021-01/Kimmel%2C%20The%20Politics%20of%20Manhood%20-%20%20pp%201-111.pdf).
**Quotes.** "There's a rule, either you do spiritual practices, or the unconscious will force you to act them out in pseudo-ritual ways." (menstuff). "We do not need an organization that simply provides a place for men to go through their mid-life crises and leave." (page_id=37)

## 2. Michael Meade / Mosaic Multicultural Foundation
**Header.** Michael Meade, Mosaic (Seattle), youth gatherings since c. 1993 with gang-involved and alienated youth. In as **core**: genius + second birth, mentor chosen by youth; **closest to target** on "the gift already in you".

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| separation | weeklong camp, remote nature | Mosaic | lowers guard (no weapons/violence/drugs) |
| clans | meets mentors/elders, joins group | elders | belongs |
| older brothers | men slightly older who passed similar paths | peers-ahead | sees a path |
| practice | ongoing work, arts, skills | mentors | builds |
| closing ritual | co-created by youth | youth + elders | "new attitude toward life" |
All: https://sojo.net/magazine/may-june-1998/essential-spirit

- Q1 Call: alienated youth; "youth have to choose their mentors" (Sojourners).
- Q2 Separation: remote place, rules, a week (Sojourners).
- Q3 Ordeal: "conflict that is ritualized and contained" (Sojourners).
- Q4 Revelation: genius, "the spirit that's already there" (https://forthewild.world/podcast-transcripts/michael-meade-on-cultivating-mythic-imagination-124); "genius hides behind the wound" (https://embodimentmatters.com/a-mythic-response-to-our-times-a-conversation-with-michael-meade, excerpt).
- Q5 Death/rebirth: initiation is "an inner labor… through which a second birth occurs" (https://courses.mosaicvoices.org/courses/paths-of-initiation).
- Q6 Witness: elders, story, drumming (Sojourners).
- Q7 Mark: not addressed.
- Q8 Return: no way back to old identity (Paths of Initiation course page).
- Q9 Integration: the rite "might not happen the first time… initiation… means you have begun" (Sojourners).
- Q10 Degrees: life as repeated initiations (*Judgment* from course page).
- Q11 Surface/depth: "genius" not IQ; myth framed as story and art (forthewild).
- Q12 Risks: see criticism.
- Q13: genius-to-genius mentoring is the core relation (Sojourners).

**Mechanics.** (1) *Gift-first reveal*: you already carry a genius; C; driver: dignity, "intrinsic value" (https://www.mosaicvoices.org/genius-project). (2) *Youth co-author the closing ritual*: C; driver: not imposed from outside (Sojourners). (3) *Youth choose mentors*: C; Sojourners.
**Failures.** No outcome studies found. Movement-level critique as for Moore (Kimmel, above).
**Quotes.** "Most people think that initiation means you have arrived. But in fact it means you have begun." / "The relationship is between the genius in the youth and the similar genius in the mentor." (Sojourners)

## 3. Bill Plotkin / Animas Valley Institute
**Header.** Founded 1980, Colorado; Soulcraft practices, Animas Quest (vision fast) (https://www.animas.org/). In as **core**: descent to soul; explicitly initiation "not into any social, religious, or spiritual group, but into your own soul" (https://www.animas.org/programs/offerings-descriptions).

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| preparation 5 days | dreams, poetry, council, drumming | guides, group | slips out of everyday |
| solo 3 days/nights | fasting (water), alone in nature, own ceremonies | self, land | "birthing a vision" |
| reincorporation 3 days | prepare to embody it "among your people" | guides | brings it back |
https://mkpusa.org/animas/ . Larger Descent arc: Preparation, Dissolution, Soul Encounter, Metamorphosis, Enactment, over weeks-months (https://noetic.org/blog/journey-soul-initiation-bill-plotkin).

- Q1 Call: self-selected seekers; deep trainings by application, "some months" (https://www.animas.org/?page_id=431).
- Q2 Separation: solitude, fasting, wild nature (mkpusa).
- Q3 Ordeal: real (fast, exposure), with safeguards (*Judgment*; buddy system described for the vision-fast genre by Davis, below).
- Q4 Revelation: Soul Encounter: vision of "unique place in the greater Earth community" (noetic).
- Q5 Death/rebirth: "you die to your familiar way of belonging to the world" (mkpusa).
- Q6 Witness: guides and council on return (mkpusa).
- Q7 Mark: "mythopoetic identity" (page_id=431); no token.
- Q8 Return: "retrieve the gift that is yours alone to bring to the world" (mkpusa).
- Q9 Integration: Enactment phase; yearlong immersion (https://www.animas.org/offerings/429/the-yearlong-soulcraft-immersion-cultivating-soul-infused-artistry-and-leadership-in-a-time-of-global-change-2023-2024). Genre risk: idealizing the wild creates a "split" that fosters "dissatisfaction and depression" (Davis, https://schooloflostborders.org/journal_school/wilderness-rites-of-passage-by-john-davis).
- Q10 Degrees: Immersions → Quest → trainings (page_id=431).
- Q11 Surface/depth: secular-sounding "soul" and "purpose" language.
- Q12 Risks: appropriation of the vision quest (https://development.thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/vision-quest).
- Q13: Davis: lasting change comes from "the totality of the rite rather than a specific experience" (Davis).

**Mechanics.** (1) *Prepared solo + structured reincorporation*: B (survey: 240 vision questers 1988–97, 78% return; benefits 56% self-connection, 44% connection to other; only 6% cited community; 98% rated wild solitude "very important") https://www.fs.usda.gov/rm/pubs/rmrs_p014/rmrs_p014_128_135.pdf (a different provider, Wilderness Transitions). Driver: displacing everyday consciousness. (2) *Vision returned as a gift with a task*: C; mkpusa.
**Failures.** Appropriation critique (above); no controlled outcome data found for Animas.
**Quotes.** "…a ceremonial descent to the underworld, in which you die to your familiar way of belonging to the world… and retrieve the gift that is yours alone to bring to the world." (mkpusa)

## 4. Evidence-based programs: Outward Bound and Rite of Passage Journeys
**Header.** Outward Bound and adventure education (meta-analysis, Hattie et al. 1997); Rite of Passage Journeys (WA, 50+ years). In as **control/evidence**: the only cluster member with A-grade outcome data.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| send-off | ceremony with families at basecamp | families, staff | severance |
| threshold | 8–10 day backpacking, skills | mentors, group | competence |
| solo | 24-hour vigil by the ocean; writes intention statement | self | vision |
| incorporation | council; Parent Incorporation Day | parents, mentors | "beginning of young adulthood" |
https://www.riteofpassagejourneys.org/mountain-quest-young-men ; https://www.riteofpassagejourneys.org/faqs

- Q1 Call: by age band (8–10 … 15–18) (https://www.riteofpassagejourneys.org/youth-programs); about half of past participants came because they wanted to (Ottey survey, https://wedgeblade.net/files/archives_assets/10835.pdf, excerpt).
- Q2 Separation: Olympic wilderness, 1–3 weeks (youth-programs).
- Q3 Ordeal: real physical challenge; OB tasks with immediate consequences ("a leaking tent") (https://www.edweek.org/leadership/adventure-programs-found-to-have-lasting-positive-impact/1997/05).
- Q4 Revelation: "Who am I?" "What can I offer the world?"; intention statement (youth-programs; faqs).
- Q5 Death/rebirth: "Separation from Childhood and Return as Young Adult" (youth-programs).
- Q6 Witness: parents at send-off and incorporation (mountain-quest page).
- Q7 Mark: not addressed.
- Q8 Return: back to family as young adult (same).
- Q9 Integration: OB gains *kept rising* after the program: ES .34 at end, +.17 more at follow-up (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.3102/00346543067001043, abstract).
- Q10 Degrees: age-graded ladder ending in Solo Crossing "capstone" (https://www.riteofpassagejourneys.org/solo-crossing).
- Q11 Surface/depth: sold as summer camp / leadership (https://www.seattleschild.com/rite-of-passage-journeys-summer-camp).
- Q12 Risks: wilderness *therapy* evidence too weak to estimate effect (WSIPP, https://www.wsipp.wa.gov/ReportFile/1748/Wsipp_Wilderness-Therapy-Programs-A-Systematic-Review-of-Research_Report.pdf).
- Q13: Hattie: "remove that tone of religious experience from the outcomes" (edweek).

**Mechanics.** (1) *Immersion + hard goal + structured success + dense feedback*: A (96 studies, 1,728 effects, 151 samples; longer programs and older participants larger effects) SAGE abstract above; drivers hypothesized in edweek. (2) *Family-witnessed send-off and incorporation*: B (Ottey survey: 87.2% "valuable"; 70% even among unwilling attendees, excerpt only, PDF scanned). 
**Failures.** "What we don't know is why" (Hattie, edweek); rites-of-passage models in adventure therapy criticized as selective ethnography and possibly unethical appropriation (https://doi.org/10.1080/14729679.2011.633380, abstract).
**Quotes.** "It seems that adventure programs have a major impact on the lives of participants, and this impact is lasting." (edweek)

## 5. Burning Man: liminoid rite
**Header.** Black Rock City, since 1986 (Baker Beach origin) (https://burningman.org/arts-innovation/philosophical-center/glossary/). In as **core-adjacent/control**: mass, voluntary ("liminoid", Turner) ritual with an explicit re-entry problem (https://medium.com/beyond-burning-man/jumping-the-shark-the-enigma-of-burning-man-62a5c596cc06, excerpt).

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate |
|---|---|---|---|
| precompression | pre-event parties | community | anticipates |
| Gate/Greeters | "the outside world is left behind" | volunteers | crosses |
| liminal week | art, gifting, playa name, communitas | all ("No Spectators") | self-expression |
| the Burn | effigy burned | collective | climax |
| exodus/decompression | parties; post-event melancholy | regionals | re-entry |
Glossary (all terms) above; communitas framing: https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/977249/1/Mccafffrrey_MA_S2013.pdf

- Q1 Call: "Radical Inclusion… No prerequisites" (https://burningman.org/about-us/10-principles/).
- Q2 Separation: desert, Gate, "Default World" left (glossary).
- Q3 Ordeal: real: heat, dust, self-reliance; "Donner Award" (glossary).
- Q4 Revelation: "Immediacy… recognition of our inner selves" (10 principles).
- Q5 Death/rebirth: burning of the Man; meaning left open (*Judgment*; Gilmore: effigy an open signifier, http://relwest.blogspot.com/search/label/NRMS, excerpt).
- Q6 Witness: fellow participants; no elders.
- Q7 Mark: playa name "given by fellow participants", not self-chosen (glossary).
- Q8 Return: principles as guideposts for "global culture" (10 principles).
- Q9 Integration: "Decompression" is both a party and "a state of melancholy, anger or frustration" (glossary); folk advice: no radical decisions for 30 days (https://hearthackersclub.com/decompress-burning-man).
- Q10 Degrees: annual repetition; Regionals (glossary).
- Q11 Surface/depth: "spiritual" but explicitly not "religious" (https://religiondispatches.org/2010/06/16/burning-man-religious-event-or-sheer-hedonism).
- Q12 Risks: hedonism; communitas becoming "normative or ideological" (relwest, excerpt).
- Q13: principles written 2004, "not as rules" (10 principles).

**Mechanics.** (1) *Mandatory participation, no spectators*: B (ethnography, Gilmore *Theater in a Crowded Fire*, https://www.ucpress.edu/books/theater-in-a-crowded-fire/paper); driver: "being through doing" (10 principles). (2) *Name given by peers*: C; glossary. (3) *Named re-entry rite (Decompression)*: C; glossary.
**Failures.** The culture names its own crash ("post-playa depression") (https://burners.me/2013/09/23/post-playa-depression-how-to-come-down-from-burning-man/); no elder holds the individual's return (*Judgment*).
**Quotes.** "We believe that transformative change, whether in the individual or in society, can occur only through the medium of deeply personal participation." (10 principles)

## Cluster patterns
- **Elder/container is the load-bearing element.** Moore's thesis; Meade, Animas and Rite of Passage Journeys all supply guides; Burning Man has none and names its own crash; Ray shows the elder role inverted into domination. *Judgment*.
- **Return is designed, not assumed.** Animas gives 3 days of reincorporation; RoPJ has a Parent Incorporation Day; Burning Man has Decompression; Davis warns that the wild-vs-home split breeds depression.
- **"Beginning, not arrival."** Meade (initiation means you have begun), Moore (lifelong order), OB data (gains grow after the program, +.17) all treat the rite as the start of an arc.
- **Gift-framed revelation.** Genius (Meade), the "gift that is yours alone" (Plotkin), "What can I offer the world?" (RoPJ): a gift to bring back, not a verdict.
- **Witness by significant others.** Parents (RoPJ), elders and mentors chosen by youth (Meade), a name given by peers (Burning Man).
- **Real but bounded ordeal.** Effects rise with program length (Hattie); safeguards and free exit separate initiation from abuse (Native critique of Ray).
- **Secular surface works.** Camp, leadership, "soul", "genius", "spiritual not religious" carry deep forms to ordinary people; the cost is appropriation critique (Kimmel; vision-quest critics).
- **Evidence gradient.** Only adventure education has A-level data; the mythopoetic and soul-initiation lineages rest on C/B testimony and surveys.
