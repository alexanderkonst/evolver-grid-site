Cluster: science. "Exemplar" = research line. Sources: web_search/web_fetch only; every fact carries its URL. *Judgment* = my inference, not a source claim. Evidence: A controlled/meta-analytic, B credible data/field study, C anecdote/theory/retrospective.

## 1. Harvey Whitehouse: modes of religiosity, dysphoric ritual, identity fusion
**Header.** Harvey Whitehouse (Oxford, Centre for the Study of Social Cohesion) with Jonathan Lanman; theory from PNG fieldwork 1990s, formal version Whitehouse & Lanman 2014. Why in: **core** — the only mature theory of why a rare, intense rite changes identity for life (https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/678698).

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare high-arousal rite (imagistic) | infrequent, often painful/dysphoric, causally opaque acts | elders, small group | fear, pain, awe; vivid episodic memory |
| Reflection | person ruminates on what it meant | self | rite becomes part of autobiographical self |
| Fusion | "visceral sense of oneness" with the group | group | willing to sacrifice for it |
| Doctrinal counterpart | frequent, low-arousal repetition of teachings | clergy, texts | identification with a large anonymous group |
Sources: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1090513810001029 ; https://rai.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1467-9655.14048 ; https://www.nature.com/articles/srep44292

- Q1 Call: not addressed (theory of effect, not recruitment).
- Q2 Separation: not addressed directly; imagistic rites belong to small exclusive groups (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modes_of_religiosity).
- Q3 Ordeal: real dysphoric arousal; shared pain predicts fusion across veterans, fraternity hazing, football fans, martial artists, twins (https://www.nature.com/articles/srep44292).
- Q4 Revelation: not a content reveal; meaning is self-generated by reflection on an opaque act (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0145611).
- Q5 Death/rebirth: personal and group self become porous ("fusion") (same).
- Q6 Witness: co-sufferers are the witnesses; frontline Libyan fighters fused with battalion as with family (https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1416284111).
- Q7 Mark: not addressed.
- Q8 Return: fused member acts for the group, up to self-sacrifice (https://www.nature.com/articles/srep44292).
- Q9 Integration: imagistic memories persist via reflection; doctrinal mode sustains identity by **repetition into semantic memory** (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1090513810001029). But a 2025 longitudinal set (COVID, Ukraine) shows dysphoria-driven fusion **rises then falls back** when the crisis turns chronic (https://doi.org/10.1111/bjso.70026; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41392441/).
- Q10 Degrees: two modes often co-exist in one tradition (https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:936b902a-e15c-4464-af6c-55a03ac9f2f8/files/m107d33de829377a511f603a5445ec19b).
- Q11 Surface/depth: not addressed.
- Q12 Risks: same mechanism explains suicide terrorism, holy wars, gang violence (https://www.nature.com/articles/srep44292); Atran notes no comparison group in the Libya study (https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1420474111).
- Q13: Self-shaping events (crucial wins/losses), not time invested, drive lifelong club loyalty (https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0160427).

**Mechanics.**
1. *Shared intense experience → reflection → fusion.* B (surveys, special populations; one experimental reflection study). Driver: episodic memory + rumination fold the event into the self. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0145611
2. *Pair the peak with repetition.* B/C (cross-cultural coding of 645 rituals). Driver: intensity bonds, repetition stores and spreads. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1090513810001029

**Failures.** Cross-sectional bias, no controls (Atran, above); fusion decays without renewal (Bautista 2025, above).

**Quotes.** "emotionally intense rituals have an enduringly transformative effect on personal identity because they give rise to indelible episodic memories and processes of reflection that become part of the essential autobiographic self" — Whitehouse 2024, https://rai.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1467-9655.14048

## 2. Dimitris Xygalatas: fire-walking, kavadi, *Ritual* (2022)
**Header.** Xygalatas (Aarhus → UConn), field experiments 2011–2022; book *Ritual: How Seemingly Senseless Acts Make Life Worth Living* (2022; British Academy Book Prize 2023, https://www.thebritishacademy.ac.uk/british-academy-book-prize/2023-british-academy-book-prize/ritual-how-seemingly-senseless-acts-make-life-worth-living). Why in: **core** — measures witness effects physiologically in real rites.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Procession | village gathers (San Pedro Manrique, 600 people) | community | rising arousal |
| Ordeal | 28 walkers cross coals ~5 s each in a 30-min rite | performer | peak arousal |
| Witness | relatives watch | kin/friends | heart rhythms align with the walker's |
| Aftermath (kavadi) | piercing, hours carrying kavadi | devotees + observers | higher generosity |
Sources: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1016955108 ; https://www.nytimes.com/2011/05/03/science/03firewalker.html ; https://pure.au.dk/ws/files/54437983/Psychological_Science_2013_Xygalatas_0956797612472910.pdf

- Q1: not addressed. Q2: public festival time and place (PNAS, above).
- Q3 Ordeal: real (coals ~1250 °F) (https://www.nbcnews.com/health/body-odd/fire-walking-inspires-real-heart-heart-study-shows-flna1c6437415); kavadi pain correlated with donation r = .36 (Psych Sci PDF).
- Q4: not addressed.
- Q5: not addressed.
- Q6 Witness: synchrony found between walkers and **related** spectators, not unrelated ones; closeness predicted similarity of heart dynamics (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3306345 ; https://spsp.org/news-center/character-context-blog/psychology-ritualized-suffering). High-ordeal **observers** donated as much as performers (161 vs 133 rupees; low-ordeal 81) (Psych Sci PDF).
- Q7–Q8: not addressed.
- Q9 Integration: not measured long-term. *Judgment:* effects shown are state effects at the event.
- Q10–Q11: not addressed. Q12: not addressed in these papers.
- Q13: anxiety makes behavior more ritualized; ritualized action reduces anxiety only slightly more than controls in a preregistered n=268 test (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9649661/).

**Mechanics.**
1. *Witnessed ordeal by people who matter.* B (field physiology, n=38). Driver: empathic arousal alignment requires a relationship. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1016955108
2. *Costly display → prosocial spillover to performer and audience.* B (field, n≈86). https://pure.au.dk/ws/files/54437983/Psychological_Science_2013_Xygalatas_0956797612472910.pdf

**Failures.** Small samples, single sites; synchrony not tied to later behavior. *Judgment.*

**Quotes.** "Ritual pre-dates every single one of the hallmarks of civilisation, including farming, writing, pottery and the wheel." — Xygalatas, quoted in https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10157566

## 3. Ritual effects on grief, anxiety, performance (Norton & Gino 2014; Brooks et al. 2016; Hobson et al. 2018)
**Header.** Harvard/Chicago/Toronto social psychology, 2013–2018. Why in: **closest to target** — small, novel, secular rituals tested in labs.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loss or threat | lost loved one/lover/lottery; pre-performance stress | life / experimenter | grief, anxiety |
| Scripted act | fixed sequence of symbolic steps, "lacking direct instrumental purpose" | self | sense of control |
| Labeling | told it is a "ritual" | frame | belief in it |
Sources: https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/norton%20gino%202014_e44eb177-f8f4-4f0d-a458-625c1268b391.pdf ; https://www.hbs.edu/faculty/Pages/item.aspx?num=51401

- Q1–Q2: not addressed.
- Q3: symbolic only.
- Q4–Q5: not addressed (losses, not identity).
- Q6: Hobson et al. list social connection as one of three ritual functions (emotion, performance goal states, social connection), via bottom-up (physical form) and top-down (meaning) processes (https://doi.org/10.1177/1088868317734944).
- Q7–Q8: not addressed.
- Q9: no follow-up beyond session. *Judgment.*
- Q10: not addressed.
- Q11 Surface/depth: grief relief worked "also to those who did not" believe in rituals; mediated by regained control (Norton & Gino PDF). Brooks: calling it a "ritual" beat calling it "random behaviors" (HBS page) — but see failures.
- Q12: compulsive rituals (OCD) are the dark twin (https://healthresearchusa.net/rituals-anxiety-grief-family-routines/).
- Q13: arbitrary week-long home ritual dampened the brain's error signal (ERN), preregistered (https://peerj.com/articles/3363).

**Mechanics.**
1. *Novel ritual restores felt control after loss.* B (3 experiments, not widely replicated). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23398180
2. *Form vs meaning split:* segmented actions calm anxiety; symbolic links mobilize goals (review prediction, untested). C. https://escholarship.org/uc/item/88p7357w

**Failures.** Brooks et al. 2016 is **retracted** after all authors requested it; audit found raw data missing for a pilot and three experiments (https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obhdp.2016.07.004 ; https://researchmisconduct.medleg.ro/cases/RW-2024-58007 ; https://healthresearchusa.net/rituals-anxiety-grief-family-routines/). Co-author Gino separately lost Harvard tenure in 2025 over misconduct findings in other work (reported via the same review, citing The Harvard Crimson). Norton & Gino 2014 is not retracted; treat as B-minus. *Judgment.*

**Quotes.** "our results suggest a common psychological mechanism underlying their effectiveness: regained feelings of control." — Norton & Gino 2014, https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/norton%20gino%202014_e44eb177-f8f4-4f0d-a458-625c1268b391.pdf

## 4. Psychedelic science: set & setting, mystical experience, integration
**Header.** Johns Hopkins (Griffiths, Barrett), Imperial (Carhart-Harris, Haijen, Roseman), integration scholars (Bathje 2022); 2006–2025. Why in: **core** for the "glow fades" problem — the only field that measures what predicts persistence after a peak.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparation (set) | screening, intention, relationship with guides | facilitators | expectancy, trust |
| Session (setting) | supportive room, music, eyeshades | guides | peak; MEQ30 measures unity, noetic, sacred, ineffable |
| Afterglow | ~6–8 weeks of lifted mood, openness, suggestibility | self | window for new patterns |
| Integration | revisit, make sense, translate into life | self + others | lessons kept or fade |
Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5203697 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10613493/ ; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.824077/full

- Q1 Call: volunteers, screened; intention-setting is part of set (Bathje, above).
- Q2 Separation: controlled setting; setting predictors include music, other people, structured support (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/02698811221101061).
- Q3: psychological risk; challenging experiences measured (https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0269881119855974).
- Q4 Revelation: "complete" mystical experience in 58% at 14-month follow-up (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3050654).
- Q5: ego dissolution is a core feature (Bathje).
- Q6 Witness: therapeutic alliance correlated with depression decrease up to one year (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10939230/); group "communitas" predicted enduring wellbeing (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33995022).
- Q7: not addressed.
- Q8: "sharing one's transformations with the world" is placed late, to buffer inflation and impulsive evangelizing (Bathje).
- Q9 Integration: 67% rated session among top-5 spiritually significant at 14 months; MEQ on session day, not drug intensity, predicted meaning (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3050654; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8033615/). Ratings "undiminished" at 14 months and matched community observers (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3308357/). Integration = active revisiting across mind, body, spirit, relationships, lifestyle, nature; "without such active effort, valuable lessons tend to fade" (Bathje). Emotional breakthrough adds prediction beyond MEQ (Roseman 2019, above).
- Q10: ascending dose sequence gave greater positive effects (PMC3308357).
- Q11 Surface/depth: critics warn against imposing spiritual interpretations; use participant's own language (Bathje, citing Sanders & Zijlmans 2021).
- Q12: heightened suggestibility in afterglow must be respected (PMC10613493); post-session difficulties include ontological shock and disappointment (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12954-025-01347-0).
- Q13: long-term predictors beyond 6 months rest on one or two studies each (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13310303/).

**Mechanics.**
1. *Quality of peak (MEQ, breakthrough), not intensity, predicts persistence.* A/B (RCTs in healthy volunteers, n≈36; prospective n=654→212). https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3050654 ; https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/pharmacology/articles/10.3389/fphar.2018.00897/full
2. *Relationship continuity (alliance) before, during, after.* B. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10939230/
3. *Integration as active, multi-domain, long process, planned before the peak.* C (concept analysis of 24 definitions, 10 models). Bathje 2022.

**Failures.** See §6 (unblinding, expectancy).

**Quotes.** "Without such active effort, valuable lessons tend to fade, and difficult experiences can reinforce traumas or existing patterns and defenses." — Bathje et al. 2022, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.824077/full

## 5. Fading and persistence: hedonic adaptation, quiet ego, Quantum Change
**Header.** Sheldon & Lyubomirsky (HAP model, 2012); Wayment & Bauer (quiet ego, 2008–2017); Miller & C'de Baca (*Quantum Change*, 2001; 10-year follow-up 2004). Why in: **core** — direct models of why the thrill fades and what keeps the change.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seminal positive change | new state/insight | life event | boost |
| Erosion path 1 | fewer fresh positive events → fewer emotions | time | thrill fades |
| Erosion path 2 | aspirations rise | self | "not enough" (devaluation) |
| Moderators | continued **appreciation** + **variety** of change-related experience | self | boost persists |
Source: https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1nr1250m

- Q1: quantum changers say "None of them claimed to have done it themselves" (https://williamrmiller.net/quantum-change). Recruited via newspaper ad (https://www.guilford.com/books/Quantum-Change/Miller-Baca/9781572305052).
- Q2–Q3: not addressed.
- Q4 Revelation: two types: insightful ("aha", noetic) and mystical (https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.159.9.1620).
- Q5: "one-way door" (williamrmiller.net).
- Q6: not addressed.
- Q7: many remembered exact date and time, on average 11 years later (Guilford excerpt, https://www.guilford.com/excerpts/miller5.pdf, read via search excerpt).
- Q8: changes in relationships, spirituality, life priorities (AJP review).
- Q9 Integration: 30 of 55 re-interviewed at 10 years; "none described a return to old ways"; some had repeated or milder further changes, some framed it as one step of continuing growth (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15048699/). HAP tested over 3 months, n=481 (escholarship, above). Activity-based changes adapt slower than circumstance changes because they vary (https://bpb-us-w2.wpmucdn.com/sites.chapman.edu/dist/5/366/files/2014/05/Boehm-Ruberton-Lyubomirsky-2017-2fiaujy.pdf).
- Q10: quantum change shows repeated episodes in some (PubMed 15048699).
- Q11: quiet ego = inclusive identity, perspective-taking, detached awareness, growth; framed as compassionate self-identity, not ego loss (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10902-014-9546-z ; https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/beautiful-minds/the-pressing-need-for-everyone-to-quiet-their-egos/). A 3-session quiet-ego intervention raised flourishing in an RCT (n=75) (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9421624).
- Q12: rising aspirations fuel overconsumption (HAP abstract).
- Q13: *Judgment:* HAP path 2 (aspirations rise) is the formal shape of Sasha's step 4, the mind devaluing the phrase.

**Mechanics.**
1. *Appreciation + variety block adaptation.* B (longitudinal path analysis). https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1nr1250m
2. *Turn the change into an activity, not a possession.* B/C. Boehm et al. 2017, above.
3. *Frame the peak as a step in ongoing growth.* C (retrospective, n=30). PubMed 15048699.

**Failures.** Quantum Change is retrospective, self-selected, no informant corroboration (https://www.psychiatryonline.org/doi/full/10.1176/appi.ajp.159.9.1620).

**Quotes.** "The happiness that comes from a particular success or change in fortune abates with time." — Sheldon & Lyubomirsky 2012, https://escholarship.org/uc/item/1nr1250m
"it was like passing through a one-way door" — Miller, https://williamrmiller.net/quantum-change

## 6. Negative: replication problems and peak manipulation
**Header.** 2016–2026 critiques. Why in: **negative**.

| phase | what happens | who acts | initiate feels/does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hype/expectancy | media testimonials prime "life-changing" | culture, sellers | high expectancy |
| Staged peak | setting + suggestion produce the experience | facilitator | peak, possibly without active cause |
| Suggestible window | ego defenses weakened for weeks | facilitator | open to any frame |
| Bond via pain | shared suffering fuses group | leaders | loyalty up to violence |

- Q1–Q2: not addressed.
- Q3: hazing is a costly ritual; emotional synchronization, not shared suffering per se, predicted cohesion and well-being up to 4 months (https://bpspsychub.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/bjso.70064).
- Q4: placebo psychedelics in a staged setting with music and imagery produced strikingly similar reports (https://slc.theconversation.com/can-a-psychedelic-induced-mystical-experience-really-improve-your-mental-health-274330).
- Q5–Q8: not addressed.
- Q9: dysphoric fusion fades when crisis turns chronic (https://doi.org/10.1111/bjso.70026).
- Q10–Q11: not addressed.
- Q12 Risks: blinding failed >90% in psilocybin/LSD/ayahuasca trials that checked; only 29.5% of 112 RCTs checked (https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamapsychiatry/fullarticle/2847667). Trials "likely to significantly overestimate treatment effects" (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9481484/). Insight and breakthrough may matter as much as mystical experience (https://www.akjournals.com/view/journals/2054/7/1/article-p18.xml). Ritual-performance flagship retracted (§3). Proposed anxiolytic mechanism (cognitive load) failed in preregistered test; repetition reduced arousal (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12110-018-9325-3). Synchrony→cooperation failed several replications (https://doi.org/10.26686/wgtn.17072576.v1). Golf "lucky ball" ritual effect failed preregistered replication (https://skepticalinquirer.org/2018/03/do-superstitious-rituals-work/). Integration critiqued as commodified and individual-centered (https://doi.org/10.1556/2054.2024.00411).
- Q13: not addressed.

**Mechanics (to avoid).** 1. *Expectancy-manufactured peaks.* A (systematic review of blinding). JAMA Psychiatry, above. 2. *Exploiting the suggestible afterglow.* C. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10613493/

**Quotes.** "premature and uncritical promotion ('hype') of psychedelics as medicines is not only misleading, but also directly influences participant expectancy" — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9481484/

## Cluster patterns (science only)
- **Peaks fade by default; persistence is engineered after the peak.** Fusion from dysphoria falls back (Bautista 2025); HAP shows emotions decay and aspirations rise; integration literature says lessons fade without active effort.
- **What sustains change after the glow (strongest evidence first):** (1) quality of the peak (meaning, unity, emotional breakthrough) rather than raw intensity (Griffiths 2008; Roseman 2019); (2) a continuing relationship with the guide and a witnessing group (alliance to 1 year; communitas; related-spectator synchrony); (3) repetition that moves the episode into semantic memory (doctrinal mode); (4) continued appreciation plus **variety** of change-related experiences (HAP); (5) converting the change into an activity or practice, not a possession (Boehm 2017); (6) framing the peak as one step in an ongoing ladder (Quantum Change follow-up).
- **Witnesses must be related.** Synchrony and generosity appear in kin/close observers, not strangers (Konvalinka 2011; Xygalatas 2013).
- **Reflection is the bridge from event to identity.** Fusion arises via personal reflection on the shared event (Jong 2015).
- **Devaluation has a formal shape.** HAP's "rising aspirations" path matches the mind devaluing the revealed phrase. *Judgment:* the counter is appreciation rituals and fresh applications, not a bigger second peak.
- **Integration starts before the peak.** Plan practices, people and space in preparation (Bathje 2022).
- **Small secular rituals work modestly, via control and repetition;** belief in them is not required (Norton & Gino), but the most-cited performance study is retracted and effect sizes in preregistered tests are small.
- **The suggestible window is an ethical obligation.** Afterglow weakens defenses for weeks; dogma or upsell there is manipulation (PMC10613493; blinding/expectancy critiques).
- **Evidence quality:** mostly B; A only for blinding critiques and a few small RCTs. Long-term (>6 months) predictors rest on one or two studies each.
