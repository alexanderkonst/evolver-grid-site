# Dossier schema: initiation (fill exactly; 200-400 words per exemplar)
1. Header: exemplar, people, era; why in (core / negative / control / closest to target).
2. Phases, outermost to innermost: | phase | what happens | who acts | what the initiate feels/does |
3. Seed questions, one line each or "not addressed":
 Q1 Call and consent: who is initiated, how chosen, how they say yes.
 Q2 Separation: how the old world is left (place, time, clothing, silence).
 Q3 Threshold and ordeal: the test, risk or cost; is it real or symbolic.
 Q4 Revelation: what is shown or recognized (secret, mirror, vision, name); how.
 Q5 Death and rebirth: what dies, what is born, how it is symbolized.
 Q6 Witness: elders, guide, community; who recognizes the change.
 Q7 Mark: new name, token, sign, degree.
 Q8 Return: how the initiate comes back, with what role or responsibility.
 Q9 Integration over time: what happens after the glow fades (doubt, devaluation, backsliding) and how the tradition sustains the change.
 Q10 Degrees: is it one rite or a ladder of initiations.
 Q11 Surface and depth: how a deep or mystical rite is framed so ordinary people accept it.
 Q12 Risks and abuses: manipulation, hazing, dependence, bypass.
 Q13 Open: anything else.
4. 1-3 strongest mechanics: exact form; evidence A (controlled/meta-analytic), B (credible data, ethnography with detail), C (tradition, anecdote); driver (why it works); source URL.
5. Failures and criticism with source.
6. 1-2 verbatim quotes with source.
End of file: Cluster patterns (5-10 bullets).
Rules: cite every fact with URL (or file path for local transcripts); mark *Judgment*; verify, don't trust memory; web_search/web_fetch only (local files where named); no browser; no cross-cluster synthesis.
