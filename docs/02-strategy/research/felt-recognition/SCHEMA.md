# Dossier schema: felt recognition (fill exactly; 200-350 words per exemplar)
1. Header: exemplar, people, years; why it is in (core / negative / control / closest to target).
2. Layers, outermost to innermost: | layer | what happens | who/what does it | when |
3. Seed questions, one line each or "not addressed":
 Q1 How is the felt moment EVOKED (what triggers it: a reflection, a word, a question, a body cue)?
 Q2 How is it RECOGNIZED as true vs. flattering or projected (the difference between resonance and the Barnum effect)?
 Q3 How is it CAUGHT and NAMED (words, image, body location, handle)?
 Q4 How is it ANCHORED or stored (memory, ritual, object, record)?
 Q5 How is it RE-EVOKED later, and does it stay the same or deepen?
 Q6 How is it USED as an inner reference for decisions or growth (calibration)?
 Q7 How is the outcome MEASURED (scales, studies, observable signs)?
 Q8 What are the RISKS (manipulation, false memory, dependence on the guide, faking)?
 Q9 What role does the WITNESS/mirror (another person, a text, an AI) play?
 Q10 Open: anything else this exemplar shows.
4. 1-3 best practices: exact mechanics; evidence grade A (controlled/meta-analytic), B (credible data, practitioner numbers), C (anecdote/tradition); driver (why it works); source URL.
5. Known failures/criticism with source.
6. 1-2 verbatim quotes with source.
End of file: Cluster patterns (5-10 bullets).
Rules: cite every fact with URL; mark *Judgment*; verify claims, do not trust memory; web_search/web_fetch only; no browser; no synthesis across clusters.
