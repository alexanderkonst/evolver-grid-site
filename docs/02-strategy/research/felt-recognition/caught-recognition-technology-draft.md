# Technology draft: Caught Recognition (the felt etalon)

*Draft for Sasha's signature, 2026-11-27, Evolver (Hark). Not yet in the [Phase Shift Technology Library](../../../01-vision/phase_shift_technology_library.md): the library carries Sasha's transmission. Source: Sasha 2026-11-27 14:29 MST + [synthesis](./synthesis.md).*

**The law in one sentence:** at the moment a true reflection lands, a person feels their own etalon directly, before words; that feeling, caught lightly and carried, is a truer reference than any phrase, and it becomes a tool of return only when it is re-met and used.

**ESC.**
*Essence:* the phrase points; the feeling is the contact. «Тонкое и очень вибрационно близкое к его сути… делит на до и после» (Sasha). It is the 3→4 crossing of the uniqueness holomap (Recognition, «landed in my body»), lived by the body.
*Significance:* it is the only direct measure of E0 (did the person come closer to their etalon). Words and 1–10 ratings are indirect instruments. It is also the sword of truth: what does not resonate with it is not you, so it cuts mental garbage, and articulation becomes both a result of contact and a tool for more contact (PST 115).
*Consequences:* the reveal catches the feeling (body first, own word after), the game re-meets it, and decisions check against it.

**Mechanics (the five moves)**
1. **Evoke** with a true mirror that has a cost: the phrase and the shadow together (Barnum guard).
2. **Pause:** a quiet beat after the reveal, nothing to press.
3. **Catch:** where in the body did it land (tap), then one word or image in the person's own language (optional). Body before word, because naming dampens.
4. **Keep:** the word sits on the character card as a token; the moment ends calmly.
5. **Re-meet and use:** next visit, «is it here now?»; before a choice, «does this feel like that?». Fresh contact, not replay.

**Check (the shift happened):** the person can re-evoke it the next day with the same or deeper shape, and makes one choice by it within 30 days.

**Guards:** consent before reflecting (PST 125); offer the mirror once, let go (PST 137); never name it for them; no installed triggers; intensity is not proof.
