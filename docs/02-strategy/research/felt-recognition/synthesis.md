## The whole in one sentence
The feeling is the etalon and the words only point at it: evoke it with a true mirror that has a cost, catch it in the body before the word, check it by its shape and not its strength, bring it back by context, and prove it by what it leads to.

## 1. Organizing principle: the felt etalon
In metrology the kilogram moved from a bar in a vault to a constant of nature. Here the person's etalon moves from a phrase on a screen (can be forgotten, re-worded, flattered) to a felt reference they carry (can be re-evoked and checked against). Every kept practice serves one of three moves: **evoke truly · catch lightly · carry and use.**

Correction to my earlier line: the Rogers–Gendlin research measured the **Experiencing Scale** (raters judged how much clients spoke from directly felt experience), not «felt sense» as such. Clients higher on it, often from the first sessions, tended to do better; a 2017 meta-analysis puts the link at small to medium (r ≈ .19). Focusing was created to teach that capacity ([therapy](./exemplars/therapy.md)).

## 4. What we cut
Rating intensity as proof. Installing anchors (NLP). Long rituals or harsh initiations. Naming for the person. Replaying the same words as «re-evoking». Statistics from one phrase.

## 5. What we already have and what is missing ([corpus](./exemplars/corpus.md))
Have: a reveal prompt written to produce the felt moment («TRANSMIT, DON'T REPORT… the "that is me, and I feel it" jolt»); a live 1–10 «How much does this sound like you?» (one number, not logged to `resonance_events`); session practice with body scoring, «where in the body did it land, what moved», Two Dials (resonance for the gift, charge for the shadow); PST 118, 125, 137, 154, 156; holomap stage 4 «landed in my body».
Missing: the product never catches the feeling itself (body place + own word), has no charge dial for the shadow, stores nothing to re-meet the next day, and never uses it for a decision. The quiz «recognition» answer means holomap stage 3, a term clash.
