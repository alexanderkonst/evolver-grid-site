# Cohort Clarity Brief

*Self-contained, no prior context needed. Aug 29, 2026. Untested proposition, not an approved plan.*

## The proposition

Alexander Konstantinov ("Sasha"), founder in Mexico City, runs a two-hour session that takes a founder with a strong but foggy business and produces one sentence naming what they do, one clear offer, and a page a stranger can read and book from. It sells for $555 to individuals and does not scale, because he has to be in the room.

Sell it to institutions instead. Venture studios, accelerators and practitioner schools run cohorts where half the participants cannot say what they do in one sentence. The program then teaches pitch and go-to-market on top of fog, and it surfaces at demo day.

Package it as a four-week block dropped into someone else's program. Week 0: recorded teaching video plus placement quiz, async. Weeks 1-3: one live two-hour session per founder. Week 4: group close where each founder states their sentence to the room.

**Price: $25,000 per cohort of ten.** That is $2,500 a founder against $555 retail, because the buyer is a budget, not a person spending their own money. **Forty cohorts a year is $1M.** He delivers maybe eight; the rest run on trained facilitators.

The moat: every cohort scores ten founders at intake with an outcome at exit. After ~30 cohorts that is 300 cases with ground truth, and the sellable thing becomes telling a program which applicants are ripe before it admits them.

## Who he is

He listens through a messy story until the living point reveals itself, names it in plain language the person can say in their own mouth, then builds the structure that lets the world meet it. He is fluent in both the language of meaning (myth, gift, purpose) and of commerce (offer, price, page, product-market fit). Founding axiom: *nothing grows past how well it knows itself.*

His known weakness, which this plan can feed: he builds ever-larger structure while the doorway stays hard to enter, refining the map instead of running the market test. He also over-gives, delivering the breakthrough before the price is agreed.

## Facts (do not contradict)

- Revenue to date: **$3,428**. Real money, not pre-revenue. Do not inflate.
- ~9 client canvases exist. One $2,000 two-month container is sold and mid-delivery.
- **A second practitioner delivers with the method** under her own brand, ~12 sessions in a recent month. This is the only evidence it transfers to another human.
- The method has run start to finish in Russian as well as English.
- Six warm founder conversations from outbound, zero clients. Reply rate was never the bottleneck.
- **Distribution is the real constraint.** No meaningful inbound traffic. Any plan needing 100k visitors is not executable.

## Terms

**Method Video**: a ~40-minute teaching block he delivers live from memory in every session. No recording exists. It is the single unlock: without it no facilitator can run this. **Ripeness Vector**: a 9-axis read of whether a person is ready to change and ready to buy, not merely interested. A shape, not a score. **Unvalidated.**

## Sequence

1. Record the Method Video, beat by beat so segments can be recut.
2. Sell **one** cohort. A warm venture-studio contact is already in conversation.
3. Deliver it manually and imperfectly, then build the layer that removes him from it.

Doing 3 before 2 is this plan's most likely failure mode.

## Risks and kill criteria

- **Precision may not transfer.** His edge is the quality of the naming. If facilitators produce a 6 where he produces a 9, the buyer notices at demo day. Kill criterion: if facilitators cannot hit threshold after two supervised cohorts, $1M at this price is off.
- **No budget line.** Programs may have no category for "positioning." Test the framing against founder coaching, narrative training or demo day prep in the first sales call, not after building.
- **Institutional sales cycles run 3-6 months.**

## Do not invent

Flag and ask, do not fabricate: whether the Ripeness Vector predicts anything (never validated), the real price ($25k is reasoned, never quoted), who the facilitators are (no pipeline), whether four weeks is the right shape, the name ("Cohort Clarity" is a working label, not approved).

## Jobs you can take

Attack the economics and say where they break. Draft the institutional offer, speaking to the program's outcome rather than the founder's inner journey. Draft first outreach to one named program. Plan the video shoot. Design the facilitator transfer test. Build the cohort margin model.

## Voice and stance

Short sentences, one idea each, concrete over abstract. **No em dashes and no "Not this. That." constructions** in anything a stranger reads; both are AI tells. One metaphor maximum. Lead with the bottom line in one line, end with one decision-ready question. Give options with a recommendation, then stop: never instruct him to send, post or publish. Push back honestly; do not flatter.
