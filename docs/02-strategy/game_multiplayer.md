## 1. The golden thread: Meet
**One verb runs through every kind of social contact: to meet.** (Sasha, 2026-10-28: «одно слово, которое проходит золотой нитью через всё это: встречи».)
- One button: **Meet**.
- One space: **Meet**.
- One building in the village: **Meet**.
- Inside it, categories of meeting (§2). Everything social in the game is a kind of meeting; we build no separate chat, feed or forum.

## 2. Fidelity: how people really meet (San Pancho as the model)
The digital village is a twin of how people meet in a real village. Sasha's map (2026-10-28):

| Type | Real life | Who starts it |
|---|---|---|
| Spontaneous | the street, the shop queue | chance |
| Café / bar | a place made for meeting new people and seeing old friends | you walk in |
| Matchmade | a friend says "you two should meet", with a topic | someone who knows both (in the game: the system) |
| 1:1 arranged | coffee with one person | you |
| Campfire | a hosted circle: congregation, community call, meetup where people meet and talk | a host, at a regular time |
| Themed event | a talk, workshop, business breakfast on a chosen theme | an organizer (already in Events) |
| Playing together | a game, a shared task | the activity |

**Two axes sort them all:** spontaneous ↔ planned, and 1:1 ↔ group.

## 3. Primitives: what works game after game
Mechanics that players vote for with their behaviour, across games (patterns from known mechanics, 2026-10-28; verify details before quoting publicly):
1. **Presence.** You see who is here now (Discord voice channels, WoW cities). People join a room because someone is in it.
2. **A shared goal first, talk around it.** Nobody queues to "chat". People meet through a dungeon (WoW, FFXIV) or a raid (Pokémon GO). Sasha: the common goal comes first, then people do something around it.
3. **Impulse button, the system does the matching.** WoW Dungeon Finder, FFXIV Duty Finder: the player sets the impulse ("I'd like to meet someone new"), the system picks who.
4. **Small first contact, more with trust.** Sky: Children of the Light: strangers are silhouettes; you light their candle, hold hands, only then chat. Journey allows only a chirp. Low risk, no spam.
5. **Hosts and rhythm.** Raid night, FFXIV mentors, Pokémon GO Community Day: a host and a fixed time keep a group alive.
6. **Both say yes.** Party invites (CS, Fortnite) and double opt-in intros (Lunchclub, Boardy): nothing opens until both agree.

## 4. The impulse comes from the person
Every meeting starts from a player's impulse: «сейчас было бы классно с кем-то новым познакомиться». They press the button; the game answers. We never push people at each other.

## 5. The Café mechanic (working hypothesis, Sasha 2026-10-28)
1. On each resident's card: **Open to** (pick any): coffee in person, a 20-min call, a question about my gift, collaboration. Plus city.
2. One button: **Invite to coffee**, with an optional one-line note.
3. The other person gets a notification (and an email): **Accept / Not now**. On accept, **a 1:1 chat opens between them** right in the game, like DMs on X or LinkedIn. Contacts can be shared in the chat. (Sasha, 2026-10-28: «а почему бы нам просто не открывать с ними чат?… нам нечего скрывать, платформу я делаю для Сан Панчо изначально… Очень даже чат!»)

## 6. Phases (radical simplicity)
- **Phase 1: the Meet button.** Press it (your impulse):
  - **Now:** see who is in the village right now; if someone is, send "Meet now?" and a call link opens for both.
  - **This week:** the system offers 3 residents by talent, each with a suggested topic. Pick one, send "Meet?". On accept, a 1:1 chat opens; in person or online, their choice.
  - Covers spontaneous, matchmade and 1:1. On accept, a 1:1 chat opens; no public feed.
- **Phase 2: Campfire.** A weekly hosted circle at a fixed time, in its building. Hosts first, later residents host. (Not the parked "Fire Circle" from the game plan: that stays parked until redefined.)
- **Phase 3: Café.** A room with live presence where you just drop in, once enough people are around that it is rarely empty.
- **Themed events** stay in Events.

## 7. Game layer
- A meeting that happened counts as a victory for both players (bridge to the Quest Board).
- Meeting is part of the core loop, not a side feature: talent found → mission → offer → first sale → tribe → village.
