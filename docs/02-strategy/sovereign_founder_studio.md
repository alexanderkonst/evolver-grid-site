# The Sovereign Founder Studio

> *Concept doc, started Day 154 (August 13, 2026). Emerged from the Marc Winn
> call (the "become the capital partner, stop selling" reframe) and worked into a
> bootstrapped, sovereignty-preserving model. Working name only; the exact word is
> Sasha's to set (seeds: Origination Studio · Founder Studio · The Original Studio ·
> Sovereign Founders).*

## One line

You do not sell a program and you do not run a fund. You **select ripe
founder-material, read their uniqueness, and build their next business as a direct
crystallization of who they are**, bootstrapped to first sales, aligned by a
capped revenue-share, with the founder keeping 100%.

## The category question

Anchor to a known word: **studio** (you originate the business from the founder,
you do not fund an existing one). But founder-first + bootstrapped + rev-share +
no-equity makes it a new category to derive and name, per the category-design
discipline (Play Bigger, derived-not-invented). "Spark investing" is a vibe, not an
owned category; the space is unclaimed.

Two faces, kept strictly separate:
- **Founder-facing:** the studio (a commercial consulting + revenue-share
  relationship). This is the whole business.
- **Investor-facing (optional, later):** Sasha as a *scout* who introduces
  graduated, opt-in founders to aligned capital for a **finder's fee**. He never
  holds, pools, or structures anyone's investment money.

## The bootstrapped principle (load-bearing)

The founder needs **no capital injection**. They pay $555, build to product-market
fit on their own revenue, and keep 100% ownership. Downside near zero. This purity
is the thesis and belongs in the founder conditions verbatim. (Angel-funded runway
repaid from revenue is debt financing / an investment and is deliberately excluded;
it would break both the bootstrapped promise and the clean legal position.)

## Who it is for (ideal client)

**Identity:** founder-material. Has founded or is unmistakably founder-DNA (built
things, operator, maybe raised money, maybe a fractional PM/operator with an
entrepreneurial spine).

**The moment (the sharp filter, one of):**
- serial pivoter, tired of pivoting
- post-exit or post-shutdown, between ventures
- slowing down / stepped back, questioning the old game
- in-between, actively considering their next venture

Common denominator: **considering their next venture, and ripe to move.**

**Readiness gates (measured by the quiz, not guessed):** surrendered to the search ·
can find the $555 (means) · buying frame open · chronic pull not a whim · consent as
pull, not push.

**Psychographic:** gets that uniqueness becomes the business · open to inner work ·
coachable · not a tire-kicker.

**Explicit non-fits (state black and white):** not-yet-ripe · needs convincing ·
wants capital not clarity · no entrepreneurial spine · won't put down the $555 ·
wants to be talked into it. *Not everybody benefits, and pretending they do is the
years-long mistake being corrected. Filter with the quiz.*

## The read (how fit is measured)

- the quiz → transition stage + readiness/ripeness
- the hero's-journey 7 chapters → where on the arc
- the uniqueness map / articulation → how clear their signal is

Every applicant gets a personal read (where they are, why an offer is or is not
made), so value is delivered regardless.

## The containers (value ladder)

1. **Productize Yourself** — $555, skin in the game, the only from-pocket payment.
   Paying it makes it real for the founder; this is deliberate.
2. **Build → PMF** — ~6-week guided cohort where the playbook is offloaded and the
   business is built to first sales. Priced at **10% of the first $100k of revenue,
   capped at $10,000.** Founder keeps 100%. A free onboarding call each, outsourced
   to trainers (train-the-trainer) as it scales.
3. **Scale to $1M** — delivered by a team Sasha assembles (automation/ops
   specialists), not by Sasha personally, paid from the founder's ongoing 10%. The
   one-person AI-run company reaches a well-oiled state.
4. **Node license (opt-in)** — the founder runs the instrument for their own
   founders, takes 10% of their revenue; Sasha takes 10% of the node's. A fractal
   revenue-share tree (BIG IDEA #2: Fractal + Viral, made literal).

## The three key stakeholders

- **Founder** — pays $555, keeps 100%, gets uniqueness → business → PMF, and pays
  meaningfully only as they succeed. Downside near zero.
- **Investor (optional, later)** — access to pre-vetted, actively-developed
  founders; when a graduate opts to raise, a direct founder↔investor deal that
  Sasha sources for a finder's fee. No fund, no pooling.
- **Community host / node (Oyi, Brenda, Club DAO)** — the deal-flow engine that
  dissolves the distribution bottleneck. Brings a cohort of ripe members, earns a
  referral slice, community gets activated (dormant potential → businesses →
  inter-node trade). Lifecycle capstone: graduates become the next hosts and
  nodes, the regenerative loop.

## Economics (base case)

Per founder: $555 + up to $10,000 (capped, contingent). A founder at $250k revenue
still owes only the $10k cap, then keeps $239,445. Cohort of 10 with a realistic
outcome spread throws off ~$52,500 to Sasha over ~12 months, delivered as one
6-week group program with low marginal cost (group + AI + outsourced onboarding).
Full model: `sovereign_founder_studio_model.xlsx` (private scratch).

## The legal blue ocean (radically simple)

- Core = a **commercial contract**: consulting + a capped revenue-share. Normal,
  how agencies and consultants bill. No fund, no securities apparatus.
- **Never hold or pool anyone else's investment money.** That one rule keeps Sasha
  out of fund/securities regulation.
- Investors, if ever, do **direct** deals with graduated founders; Sasha takes a
  finder's fee as the scout. A pool, if one exists, is someone else's; he only
  feeds it deal flow.
- On budget-constrained founders: not gifting-as-strategy. Case-by-case discounts,
  occasional stipends, or Sasha flexing down himself, only for genuine ripe fits who
  truly cannot do the $555.
- Blockchain/tokenized investor instrument: possible but heavy securities law;
  parked, not the MVP.

## Closest existing references

- **Entrepreneur First** (entrepreneurfirst.com) — invests in the individual before
  the idea; closest philosophy.
- **Calm Company Fund** / Earnest Capital's SEAL (calmfund.com) — closest legal
  cousin: rev-share, keep equity, bootstrapped.
- **TinySeed** (tinyseed.com) — bootstrapped-founder accelerator.
- **Indie.vc** (Bryce Roberts, defunct) — the no-forced-exit rev-share ethos.

The uniqueness-instrument front-end (measuring founder-market-fit) is the piece no
one else has.
