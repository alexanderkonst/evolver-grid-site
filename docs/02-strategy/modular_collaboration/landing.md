# Module 2 · Phase 5: Landing «Bring your module»

Draft v1 · 2026-11-21 · Hark. Research of how the industry builds partner pages, then a page outline for us. Nothing built or published. Part of [Module 2](./README.md).

## Summary for decision

- **Answer:** the industry has one settled shape: a partners page for founders (not for customers), one call to action, honest rows of live integrations, and a clear «how it works» in 4–5 steps. Tiers and badges come later, if ever.
- **For us:** one page «Bring your module into the game», built around the connector and roadmap 0–4, with our own platform as the first live row and a sample partner as the second.
- **Decision from you:** build the page now with our rack as proof, or wait for the first real partner row. My pick: wait for the sample partner (Phase 3), then build. Certainty: likely.
- **Signal worth knowing:** Ordana (joinordana.com) works almost exactly on our idea: each member's own coding agent gets a build prompt for the integration, revenue share on attributed sales. A peer or a competitor to study before we position.

## 1. What the industry does (facts, sources)

| Who | What they do | Takeaway for us |
|---|---|---|
| Ordana, «How to build a SaaS partners page» (2026-11-07) | Page for founders at /partners: headline naming who you partner with; live integrations one row each, each as what it does for a shared customer; what you bring a partner; what you look for; how it works in 4–5 steps («each side builds its own half»; revenue share on attributed sales); one CTA; short FAQ. Leave off: logo walls, affiliate language, tiers at small scale, numbers you can't defend. | Our outline below follows this order. |
| Maven, Expert Policy Guidebook (2026-10-02) | Experts keep IP; Maven keeps 10%, expert gets 90%; attribution per sale by UTM links; listing on the marketplace after 100 leads or 2 paid enrollments and a quality review. | Benchmark for course commission; attribution by link is standard; a light traction bar before listing. |
| HubSpot technology partners | No fee to join or list; build an integration and get 3 active users to list; four tiers that unlock with traction. | Free entry, a tiny proof bar, levels that grow. Our roadmap 0–4 plays the role of tiers. |
| Zapier partner program | Tiers Bronze to Platinum; embedding the integration on the partner's own site moves them up faster. | Reward partners who link back to the game from their own site. |
| Freshworks technology partners | Hero with reach number, two CTAs (become a partner, develop your app), benefits, tiers. | Large-company version; too heavy for us now. |

## 2. Page outline for us (draft, English)

1. **Headline:** «Bring your module into the Game of Being You.» Sub: «Players looking for what you teach or offer find you inside the game, and come to your world to buy.»
2. **Who we partner with:** founders with a living ecosystem: courses, practices, products, communities.
3. **Live modules, one row each:** our own modules first (Zone of Genius, Quality of Life, Genius Offer), then the first partner row, each as what it gives a player.
4. **What we bring:** players who already know their genius and their next step; the high-precision profile (later, level 4).
5. **How it works:** our AI reads your project and drafts your module cards → you correct them → we place them in the right game space (Academy, Market, Café…) → link with your referral id (level 0) → grow along the roadmap when it pays.
6. **The roadmap 0–4** as a simple strip.
7. **Terms:** your IP stays yours; terms from Module 2 decision (commission % or free listing for first partners).
8. **One CTA:** «Bring your module» → a short form: your project's link; our AI does the rest.
9. **FAQ:** cost, who builds what, who supports a shared client, how to exit.

## 3. Against

A partners page with only our own rows reads as empty; one real partner row is worth more than the whole page. Hence the pick: wait for Phase 3.
