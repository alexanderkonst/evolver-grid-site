## Summary for decision

- **Answer:** Planetir splits cleanly into four Academy courses plus one whole ecosystem. Under the placement rule, the courses go into our Academy and Planetir as a whole earns a building of its own (it already has a live skin at /planetir).
- **Biggest finding:** Planetir launched «Flourish with Planetir», a model almost identical to ours: creators list offerings, curators earn a share when someone books through their recommendation, «no sale, no commission». Tiers 10% / 15% / 20%, of which the curator gets 6% / 10% / 14%. So the connection can run both ways: their pathways sit in our Academy (we earn 10%), and our game lists in Planetir as a curator (we earn 6–14% on what our players book there).
- **Against:** their curator share is higher than our 10%. A partner comparing both may ask why ours is lower or simpler. Our answer: one number, nothing to sign, no membership fee (theirs is 9–99€ a month once you earn).

## What the dry run taught us for the automatic version (L3)

1. Public pages give name, kind, result and link. Price often sits behind sign-up: the card must allow «price on partner's site».
2. Weak descriptions (card 4) are common: the intake must ask the partner to confirm or fix each card before it goes live.
3. Placement by kind works: 4 of 5 cards placed with no judgment needed; the fifth (a whole ecosystem) needs Sasha.
4. Partners may run their own commission model. The connector should treat mutual listing as normal, not an exception.
