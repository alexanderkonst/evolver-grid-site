# Partner offer · Bring your module into the Game of Being You

v1 draft · 2026-11-21 · Hark. Phase L1 of [launch_plan.md](./launch_plan.md). Not published; Sasha approves the text. Sources: [launch_from_corpus.md](./launch_from_corpus.md) §1.

## The offer in one line

Let's connect our systems: my players will come to you. You pay 10% of what each client who comes from the game pays you. No clients, nothing to pay.

## How it works

1. **Paste a link** to your project (site, course, product, community).
2. **Our AI drafts your module cards:** one card per thing you offer, each with one result and one button.
3. **You check and confirm** the cards. Nothing goes live without your yes.
4. **Your module appears in the village** in the place that fits it: a course in the Academy, a product in the Market, a session in the Mastermind. A whole ecosystem may get its own building, by Sasha's decision.
5. **Players click through** to your site with a referral id. They stay as long as they want and buy what they want, on your site, on your terms.

## Terms

- **Commission:** 10% of what each client who arrives through the game pays you. No clients, nothing to pay. (Sasha, 2026-11-02.)
- **Nothing to sign:** listing your module is accepting these terms. Acceptance is recorded with the date.
- **Attribution:** a client counts as game-sourced when they reach you through your module's link with its referral id. Window: open question for Sasha (proposal: 12 months from the first click).
- **Reporting at stage 1:** you report sales from game-sourced clients once a month; we trust by default and check only by exception. Later the payment split happens automatically.
- **Your name stays yours:** your brand, your prices, your clients' data on your side. We never sell or share your data or your players' data.
- **Exit any time:** remove your module and the terms end; commission is owed only on sales already made.
- **No equity, no pooled funds,** ever.

## Quality bar

- One clear result per module, a real working link, honest description.
- Stage 1: our AI checks each card, then one human review before it goes live.
- We may decline or remove a module that misleads players or breaks the game's tone (inform and inspire, no pressure selling).

## Players you send us

Players who come to the game from your link are yours to credit: their referral id points back to you. (Rule to confirm with Sasha: whether partners earn anything on their players' purchases in the game.)
