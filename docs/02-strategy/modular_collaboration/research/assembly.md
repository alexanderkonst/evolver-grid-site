## 3. Implications for Module 2 and the launch

**Ready-made terms to reuse**
1. **Three Terms offer paragraph** as the spine: free = card, listing, method; paid = rev share on attributed sales only; permanent = each side's name stays on its module.
2. **Acceptance by act** (DA "nothing to sign on day one") plus **honor-system reporting** (§5.3). This covers self-serve: a partner submits their link, which is acceptance, and Sasha never has to know. Reuse §3.5 refunds and §10 exit.
3. **Number precedents**, all already in canon:
   - 33.3% when the platform brought the buyer (owner decision 2026-10-30, the exact case for Module 2).
   - 10% (Distributor, Maven, author-brought).
   - 33% of the first two purchases (host referral: a capped rev share, mirror-symmetric to Module 2).
   - "10% capped at $X" (SFC, Destiny).

   One line that fits the rule: *"X% of sales from players we send, first N purchases per player (or capped at $Y per partner per year). No equity, no exclusivity, leave anytime."*
4. **Reciprocity**: we already pay hosts 33% for referrals; "whoever brings the buyer gets the same share" is the simplest mass-message term.
5. **Free / Ask / Never** as the partner FAQ; **consent, opt-in default off** (DA §7, `matchConsentToken`) for anything passed at L4.
6. Framing: Co-Distribute / Door, Spectrum stage 4, PST 86 (link these rather than new "Linux/Red Hat" wording).

**What our drafts contradict or duplicate**
- **Taste-first law vs mass message.** The Three Terms forbid leading with terms to people who haven't tasted. Sasha wants a mass message with simple terms. Fix: invite partners to *play first* (the game is the taste); terms as one line or a link, not the pitch.
- **"Capped" is missing.** connector.md option A is "a % of sales", with no cap or time bound. Sasha's rule and the Three Terms require one ("bounded in time and project").
- **landing.md says "wait for first partner row"**, which conflicts with the simultaneous launch. The page must exist when the message goes out. Our own rack rows plus Planetir/NS skins can be the proof, labelled as skins, not partners.
- **Retired line vs canon.** connector.md retires *"can I incorporate into your system"*, but golden_age_canon §3 and the README still carry it as canon (B2-23). Canon needs Sasha's update.
- **"Individual pricing"** (B2-45) vs uniform mass terms. Read it as "partner sets their own prices" and keep our share uniform. Confirm with Sasha.
- **Duplicates.**
  - The card in connector.md §2 overlaps `05-reference/module_registry.md` (superseded, had Source/Inputs), `src/types/module.ts`, and `templates/module-form.md` (UX form, not partner card). Name one owner.
  - The partner pitch overlaps `templates/ecosystem-offer.md`. That is a high-touch track (proof, call, founder sends personally); state Module 2 is the light one.
- **Do not reuse the equity remnants**: unique_business_cooperative v1 "Venture Equity" (superseded), taxonomy "Business co-founding (equity split)", Playbook l.925 "equity / persistent rev-share" and l.3835 "PARTNERSHIP · equity".
- **Pooled money**: product_brief #6 "общая казна" and the canon Fund fork conflict with "no pooled funds". Module 2 should route no share into any treasury until Sasha settles it (the funnel_map_v1 §3 conflict stands).
