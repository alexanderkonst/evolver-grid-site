## 3. Business model now

- **Commission tiers, chosen by the creator per offering** (source: /for-individuals and /for-communities):
  - **Seed 10%:** curator 6 / connector 2 / platform 1 / fund 1
  - **Grow 15%:** 10 / 3 / 1 / 1
  - **Flourish 20%:** 14 / 4 / 1 / 1
  - Their worked example: a €2,000 retreat at Flourish tier pays the creator €1,600, the curator €280, the connector €80, the platform €20 and the fund €20. Tiers can change monthly.
- **"No sale, no commission":** creators keep 100% of anything sold off-platform. Commission earners invoice Planetir themselves and handle their own income tax.
- **Membership:** €9–99/month for creators, curators and communities, only after they earn revenue through Planetir. Anyone who refers a subscriber earns **20% of that subscription**, with no cap.
  - This sits awkwardly next to the FAQ line "There are no rewards for recruiting people."
- **Communities:** onboarding by invite link or CSV in about 3 minutes, and "governance stays entirely with you." A community acting as curator earns the curator share.
- **Collective Fund:** now 1% of each sale under Flourish, versus 10% in the whitepaper. Early partners get "a voice" in how it is spent.
- **Token:** whitepaper only; no live mechanism seen.
- **Capital:** Convertible Steward Agreement with up to 4× capped return (Notion seed-round page). Planetir (Kulimi e.V.) also acts as trustee with mission-veto rights over Kaphera.
- **Implied arithmetic (my inference):** at 1% platform take, €3M ARR from commissions alone would need about €300M of sales. In practice ARR has to come from subscriptions: roughly 8,300 paying members at about €30/month. Commission revenue is mostly passed through to curators.

## 6. Implications for a mutual connection with "The Game of Being You"

**Fit:**
1. **Model match.**
   - Planetir monetizes through capped commissions and revenue share, never equity. That matches Sasha's rule ("I don't do equity, I do capped rev share… or debt").
   - The curator role is the natural slot for the game. After the talent reveal, the game recommends Planetir offerings that fit the player's genius, and earns 6–14% on resulting sales with no inventory of its own.
2. **Audience match.** Their buyers (coaches, facilitators, retreat-goers, changemakers) are the same people the game serves. Their own language ("unique gifts", "secret superpowers", "Who am I, beyond what a résumé can capture?") is close to the game's promise.
3. **Foothold already exists.** Offer #534 means Sasha is a listed creator. Upgrading the listing to "the game as a curator plus a creator offering" is incremental, not a cold start.
4. **Their gap is our strength.** Merlin does conversational onboarding; the game does precise talent discovery. The game could be the deep onboarding that feeds Merlin's matching.

**Risks:**
1. **"Their courses in our Academy at 10%" assumes they have courses.**
   - The Learning pathways are third-party partner content behind login. Planetir is a marketplace, not a course producer.
   - Through Flourish at the Seed tier, a curator earns **6%, not 10%**. A 10% cut would need either a bilateral deal or creators choosing the Grow/Flourish tiers.
   - Deal with each creator, not with Planetir.
2. **Volume is small.** 10 curated offers and a waitlist; recommendation commissions will be pocket money until they scale.
3. **Listing criteria:** they require "real market traction: prior sales, testimonials." The game's paid conversions are early, so a creator listing may be slow-tracked.
4. **Overlap risk.** Merlin already claims "purpose, dreams… secret superpowers." Pitch the game as precision depth, not as a competing matcher.
5. **Attention and continuity.** Mehemed is focused on Kaphera, and the team is 2–10 people running many events. Expect slow decisions.
6. **Admin.** Commissions are invoiced to a German e.V./partnership. Cross-border invoicing and tax fall on Sasha's side, since he is resident in Mexico.
7. **Doc drift.** Whitepaper (10–20% to Planetir, 10% to the Fund) vs. Flourish (1% + 1%): get the current terms in writing before relying on numbers.

**Bottom line:** the low-cost, low-risk move is the curator role for the game plus selective creator listings. Recruiting Planetir creators into the Academy one by one at 10% is a separate, bilateral track, not a Planetir-level deal.
