## Who it's for

The depth-native. You already see what almost no one sees. You've been a king in
your inner world for years, structure, patterns, depth, the maps. The one thing
you've never done is build the king's **хозяйство** in the material one. This is
where you ground the crown.

## What it is

A small group. An intentional rapid transformation. The «Переход» arc, run as a
cohort, aimed at grounding: your uniqueness (which you already have) turned into an
offer, a business, first real sales. You go together, because you already know
what's possible, and because a cohort of your kind is rare fuel.

## The two things you bring (non-negotiable)

1. A soul-level decision to become a businessperson, not just a seer. To let the
   material world matter.
2. **Apprenticeship: you follow the process with humility and surrender as long as
   you are choosing to continue learning. The reps, at the container's pace.**

## Speed is a gate

This is rapid by design. If you want to go slow, this isn't it, and I'll say no.
(I learned that the hard way: slow means I don't see the result, or the money, in
any reasonable time.)

The first couple of weeks are intense on purpose, so you meet the material that
comes up in one go rather than dragging it out. When deep inner material surfaces,
Karime and Oluwa are available to help (donation or full price depending on your
current reality).

**You have to be willing to learn life-enriching stuff that can be intense,
hot/cold-like, but you are not inner-work shy, so we can totally handle it.**

**There is probably not really much leeway to back out and keep secretly choosing
to play smaller than you know you can.**

## The agreement (trust first, but papered)

- **Skin in the game up front: $555.** For a genuine fit who truly cannot pay it, a
  donation of any amount before we start, and at the end of the first couple of
  weeks we both honestly check whether it's working for both of us. (Not the default,
  a scoped exception, because for you specifically money is part of the medicine.)
- **10% of your first $55,555 of revenue, capped at $5,555.** You keep 100% of your
  company. (The fives: $555 in, $5,555 on the first $55,555.)
- A real contract: clear conditions, clear terms. Because with a clear agreement I
  don't have to chase or litigate you, and I won't.

you can totally be a great one.**

## Contract skeleton (for the lawyer, not the founder)

- **Parties:** Sasha (facilitator) · Founder (sovereign owner, keeps 100% equity).
- **Scope:** the King-Maker Intensive, ~N weeks, group cohort + the artifacts (uniqueness
  articulation, offer, business build to first sales).
- **Consideration:** $555 (or agreed donation) on start; plus 10% of gross revenue
  up to a cap of $5,555, measured against the first $55,555 of revenue.
- **Ownership:** Founder retains 100%. No equity, no control, no board seat taken.
- **Commitments:** the two above (soul decision + apprenticeship); pace clause
  (container speed); the two-week mutual-review checkpoint.
- **Exit:** either party may end at the two-week checkpoint; revenue-share obligation
  survives only on revenue actually earned during/after the engagement, capped.
- **Governing spirit:** trust first; the contract exists so neither has to chase.
