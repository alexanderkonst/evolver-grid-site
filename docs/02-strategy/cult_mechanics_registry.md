## 0. Boundary, centre, how to read this file

**System.** The ecosystems on this planet that hold people for years, ranked by evidence of true human engagement: sustained participation, identity ("I am a Rotarian, a Xoogler, a Burner"), and a community that keeps running without being pushed. Vanity metrics (downloads, impressions, registrations alone) do not count as engagement.

**Centre (draft, for Sasha to correct).** People keep returning for years, often unpaid or paying their own way, because something keeps happening between them and other people that they cannot get alone.

**Grounding rule (Sasha).** The Game digitizes what already happens in real life. This map therefore prefers mechanics proven in real communities over invented ones.

**Modules of the map** = the six holonic levels: artifact → person/craft → team/lab → institution/school → ecosystem/city → movement/planet. **Second axis** = six flavors: game · network · school · lab/company · craft/brand · global movement. **Stages** = a five-state lifecycle (emerging · growing · mature · declining · transformed). The Morphogenetic Holomap uses 27 perspectives × 7 stages; applying the 7-stage octave to 50 outside systems would be guesswork on thin data, so this phase uses the coarser lifecycle. Open question 1 asks Sasha which he wants.

**Source marks.** ✓ = the research pass opened the page. ~ = the figure appeared in search results only; treat as weaker. Wikipedia is secondary and used as a pointer to primary sources; where the primary source is named, it is linked. † = founding year is common knowledge, not source-checked in this pass. "Unknown" means the pass looked and found nothing usable; nothing is estimated silently. "Inferred" marks my reading, not a sourced fact.

**Engagement score E (1–5), the rubric.**

| E | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Decades, or an audited weekly ritual; run by its own participants or self-funding; a named identity; numbers from primary or audited sources |
| 4 | At least two of those, solid numbers, but a gap (retention, trend, or a secondary source) |
| 3 | Scale or influence documented; depth of participation or self-sustainability thin |
| 2 | A credential, a transaction, or paid visits counted; not shown to be a community |
| 1 | Little independent data |

Scores are my judgement from the evidence collected, not a measurement. Where the research pass scored an entry differently from the rubric I changed it (Bell Labs, Duolingo, Couchsurfing: noted in their rows). Rank = E first; inside a tier, depth of participation per person (years, ritual), then scale.

## 4. What this map cannot yet say

- **Retention is the missing datum.** Almost every entry has registered or active counts at one moment; few have years-per-person. AA's average sobriety and MIT's survey are the nearest. A later pass should look for tenure distributions.
- **Many numbers rest on Wikipedia.** It is cited where the primary source was not found; the primary sources (aa.org, WOSM, Rotary, Burning Man AfterBurn, SEC filings, parkrun accounts, MIT, Stanford) are used where the pass found them.
- **Several figures are estimates by the organization itself** (Scouts, AA, Greenpeace supporters, Mondragon). They are marked as such.
- **Founding years marked † are not source-checked.**
- **Nothing here is an Aim or an Act.** The weakest load-bearing module of the *Game* is not yet read against this map.
