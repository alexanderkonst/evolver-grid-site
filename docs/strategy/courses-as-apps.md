## 3. The loop: infographic ↔ course ↔ game
- Every course has an infographic series (ADP done on the kit: `docs/assets/infographics/book/adp/`).
- The infographic has a **teaser** that works as a **lead magnet** and leads to the course.
- The course **ends with the infographic**: a strong accelerator of integration.
- **Every course is an entry into the game.**
- **Invite into the game** (key, emphasized by Sasha).
