# The Entrepreneur's Hero's Journey — Infographic Series (Prompt Spec)

*For Sasha's image agent. The agent already knows the house aesthetic; these prompts specify structure, exact text, and per-slide motif. English first. Day 145.*

**What this is:** the lead-magnet map. 9 slides: cover, 7 chapters, CTA. Posted as a carousel (Instagram first, then everywhere). Funnel: map → "find where you are" → findyourtoptalent.com.

**Core idea:** mythical descriptions leave the hero's journey ethereal. This map lands it in ordinary professional life in 2026: from archetypal to concrete, from high level to eye level. It shows what each chapter looks and feels like for a modern person earning, learning, building, stalling, breaking down, re-forming, and beginning again.

## Slide 2 — Chapter 1: Career-Building

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 1"
Title: "Career-Building"
7-dot line, dot 1 lit.
Body: "You have a reliable job, freelance gigs, or your own business. It just works, money is coming in, you are learning quickly, and build ambitions. You are growing, and all is well."
Phase transition: "you've outgrown your role and your learning dramatically slowed down."
```

## Slide 3 — Chapter 2: Dormant Potential

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 2"
Title: "Dormant Potential"
7-dot line, dot 2 lit.
Body: "Your career still looks good or even great on paper but deep inside you know that your potential is way more ample than your role. The organization is leveraging only a fraction of you but this is what's needed for the business (even if it's your business). Your \"superiors\" don't really care to know you well. Your growth stagnates."
Phase transition: "you start seeing through the corporate game, the linear entrepreneurship, or needing to sell your hours. You look for a way to game the system."
```

## Slide 4 — Chapter 3: The Stuckness

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 3"
Title: "The Stuckness"
7-dot line, dot 3 lit.
Body: "Growth pretty much stopped, and you now dislike most of what you do. However, your overall professional direction still feels like a legit and solid bet. So you push ever harder, try to optimize your productivity, look for growth and career hacks. Alas, results don't come, the system is rigid, and you don't see a better way. You feel stuck, and want out. But if not this, then what? So you keep running on low battery."
Phase transition: "The loathing and soul-draining increases and you are not motivated. Something needs to change asap."
```

## Slide 5 — Chapter 4: The Breakdown

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 4"
Title: "The Breakdown"
7-dot line, dot 4 lit.
Body: "Exhausted, you quit, end the business, experience a burnout, take a sabbatical, get fired, or else. You are at your limit, this time for real. Maybe you are still running on fumes, or pay with your health. But the clock is ticking, or the revenues are decreasing."
Phase transition: "Life makes it unequivocal: the old way of making money is about to be over. Whether you accept it or not. Until you grieve all that could have been and accept the death of the old chapter."
```

## Slide 6 — Chapter 5: The Free Fall

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 5"
Title: "The Free Fall"
7-dot line, dot 5 lit.
Body: "The old way is dead. The new one isn't born yet. You live on savings which naturally melt overtime. Past titles, identities, and accolades are crumbling and expiring as time passes. You are becoming an ordinary person and it freaks you out. It also gives anxiety to your life partner if you have one. Worst, you start doubting yourself as you realize there are no guarantees in life."
Phase transition: "You learn to live in the In-Between-Chapters void and regain the faith in yourself and your professional success."
```

## Slide 7 — Chapter 6: Metamorphosis

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 6"
Title: "Metamorphosis"
7-dot line, dot 6 lit.
Body: "The vision finally comes. There is a new version of you that is beyond your wildest dreams and you cannot unsee it. At first, you are in love with it. Then, you doubt. Finally, you realize this was always meant for you, and eventually it even seems obvious. Details become visible. You start to land it into reality."
Phase transition: "You realize that the new version of you is simply... more you. The new you is the truest you. The you that you have always been."
```

## Slide 8 — Chapter 7: The New You & The New Chapter

```
Eyebrow: "CHAPTER 7"
Title: "The New You & The New Chapter"
7-dot line, dot 7 lit.
Body: "The vision turns into a business. Synchronicities start to happen more and more. Life wants you to do this. Clients, collaborators, mentors start to be pulled in by your magnetism. Challenges on the hero's journey come, and you go into them intentionally. You develop your character, learn to build business structures, continuously clear blocks on money, self-worth, speaking your truth. Your authenticity blossoms."
Phase transition: "The new you emerges as you continue building your career just like you did in the beginning. This time around it is conscious, intentional, and in service to the well-being of others."
```
