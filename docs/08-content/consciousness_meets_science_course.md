### C01E00 — Mystics First
*Source: Sasha's core idea (VIDEO_PRODUCTION_PLAN.md, 2026-10-27). Historical examples [added].*

**Essence:** Every great insight is born within first; science then shapes, proves and applies it.

**Body:**
For most of human history, the people who pushed the edges of what we knew about the world were the mystics. They went within, and came back with something new. Then science arrived. It pushed mysticism out, cast it into ostracism, and forgot the order of things: insight comes from within first. Only afterwards is it shaped, formalized, proven and applied.

The history of science still remembers. [added] Isaac Newton, who gave us classical physics, wrote extensively on alchemy and theology alongside his physics. [added] Srinivasa Ramanujan credited his formulas to the goddess Namagiri. [added] And the chemist August Kekulé, by his own later account, said the ring structure of benzene came to him in a reverie, as an image of a snake seizing its own tail.

When we forgot where insight comes from, humanity got confused and lost its direction. [added] We kept the knowledge and lost the compass.

What is happening now is a return. Mysticism is coming back to its original role: expanding the edges of the known, about the Universe and about the present moment. Not against science. Inner insight and scientific knowledge are two sides of the same coin.

**Practice:** [added] Recall one moment when you simply knew something before you could explain it. Where did that knowing come from?

**Hand-off:** Next: what happens when we place inner experience and science side by side.

### C01E01 — Two Sides of One Coin
*Source: article intro, "Embarking on a Journey", "Inner Experiences Revealing a New Understanding".*

**Essence:** Inner experience and science are not rivals; together they reveal what reality is.

**Body:**
We are on the brink of a transformative understanding of reality, one that blends the exploration of inner space with science. Many people, on many paths, report profound inner experiences. These experiences reveal, with the clarity, undeniability and immediacy of direct experience, something about the true nature of reality.

And when we start from them, and try to explain why so many people experience the same things, a fascinating pattern emerges. They line up closely with some of the most powerful ideas of modern science. So personal, subjective experience and objective scientific knowledge are not mutually exclusive. They are two sides of the same coin.

This course walks that bridge. First, eight experiences people share. Then the doors through which people reach them. Then the science that rhymes with them. [added] Some of that science is established and some is still hypothesis, and we will always say which. Finally, one integral picture that weaves all the threads together.

This is more than a theoretical proposition. It is an invitation: not just to observe, but to participate. To experience these states yourself, to find fellow explorers, and to co-create a new story of reality, one that could take science, consciousness and our very lives to a whole new level.

**Practice:** As you move through this course, keep one question open: does this look, or feel, familiar?

**Hand-off:** But first: can an inner experience count as evidence at all? That is next.

### C01E02 — Inner Experience as Data
*Source: article, "A New Vision of Reality", "Profound Inner Experiences Revealing the Nature of Reality".*

**Essence:** A direct experience is first-person truth: it can be pointed to, never fully handed over.

**Body:**
Imagine standing on a mountaintop, the world spread out beneath you, and suddenly feeling an overwhelming connection to everything around you. The boundaries between you and the universe dissolve, and what remains is unity. Many people report moments like this. Not fleeting moods, but experiences that touch the core of their being, with a clarity and immediacy that leaves no room for doubt in their realness and their significance.

Here is the honest limit. An inner experience can never be relayed with a hundred percent accuracy, by definition. It is direct and first-hand, unique, and accessible as it is only to the one who lives it. Art, words and computer simulations can point toward it. They cannot hand it over.

So why treat it as data? Because it repeats. When vast numbers of people describe the same structures from within, the repetition itself asks to be explained. [added] Phenomenology, the careful study of first-person experience, is already one of the fields of consciousness research.

And the world's wisdom traditions make an even bolder claim: these experiences are not far away. They are happening deep within you, right this moment.

**Practice:** [added] Close your eyes for three breaths. Notice that no one else can see what you are seeing now. That is first-person data.

**Hand-off:** Next: the first of eight experiences that people share.

*Source: article, theories 2, 3, 5. Fixes the article's slip: strings belong to string theory (hypothesis), not to quantum mechanics.*

**Essence:** Reality vibrates and connects beyond distance; strings are a beautiful, unproven hypothesis.

**Body:**
Quantum mechanics and quantum field theory are established. [added] They are among the most precisely tested theories we have. [added] At the tiniest scales they describe particles that behave both as particles and as waves, and every particle as a vibration of an underlying field. This dual, vibrating nature mirrors the experience of being part of a vibratory field, a flow of energy.

Quantum entanglement is established, and confirmed in experiments. Particles can stay linked so that what we measure on one is instantly correlated with the other, regardless of distance. [added] It does not send messages faster than light; it reveals a connection deeper than distance. This reflects the intrinsic link with everything that people feel in profound inner experiences.

Now the hypothesis. String theory, and other theories of quantum gravity, propose that all particles and forces arise from tiny vibrating strings, like different notes in a cosmic symphony. [added] It is elegant, and it is unproven: no experiment has confirmed it yet. Still, it resonates with the experience of being part of a harmonious, vibratory cosmos.

[added] So: vibrating fields, established. Connection beyond distance, established. Strings as the notes of the symphony, a hypothesis.

### C04E03 — The Meditating Brain and the Question of Mind
*Source: article, theories 7–8; map item 3.1.1.*

**Essence:** Meditation leaves measurable traces; what consciousness itself is remains an open hypothesis.

**Body:**
The neuroscience of meditation is established, and growing. Specific brainwave patterns associated with deep meditative states correlate with heightened awareness and perception. Here science meets inner experience in the lab: the states are real enough to measure. [added] Measurement shows that something happens in the brain; on its own, it does not tell us what the experience reveals.

Then comes the deepest question of all: what is consciousness? [added] Here we enter hypothesis. Panpsychism suggests that consciousness is a fundamental feature of all matter, not something that switches on only in brains. [added] Integrated Information Theory proposes that consciousness corresponds to how much a system integrates information as one whole. [added] Orch-OR, from Roger Penrose and Stuart Hameroff, proposes that consciousness is linked to quantum processes inside the brain's microtubules. [added] All three are serious, debated, and unproven.

Yet look at where they point: toward a universe in which awareness is not an accident, but woven into the fabric of things. That aligns with what people report in states of universal consciousness and interconnected awareness.

[added] The lab can measure the meditator. Only the meditator can know the meditation.

**Practice:** [added] Sit for one minute and notice awareness itself, not what it is aware of. Is it located anywhere?

### C05E02 — A Universe of Living Pixels
*Source: article, "An Integral Theory of Everything".*

**Essence:** Reality imagined as tiny points, each holding the whole: a model to explore, not a finding.

**Body:**
Start from the inner experiences, weave in the science, and a vision of reality emerges: everything interconnected, arising from a single source, a cosmic symphony in which we are both participants and expressions. [added] It is offered as a model to explore, a hypothesis, not a proven finding.

At its core, reality is imagined as made of incredibly tiny "pixels," likely at the Planck scale, each one a point that is also a vibrating string.

Each point generates a toroidal, apple-shaped field, a minuscule dynamo where energy flows in and out at once. [added] The torus that people feel from within returns here as a shape of the cosmos.

Interconnected, these points form a vast network: the zero-point energy field, the foundational quantum field of the universe. Each point is a tiny window into the entire cosmos, the way every part of a hologram contains the whole image, or a drop holds the properties of the whole ocean.

The points interact with all the others, a fluid, ever-changing tapestry of energy and information. Fluctuations within this field give rise to particles and energy patterns, and so to matter and the observable universe.

Consciousness, energy and information are unified here. The universe is observing and shaping itself from within. Every part is a whole in itself, and part of a larger whole.

## Part 3 — The shift this course makes (working notes with Sasha, 2026-10-28; not final)
Method: [shift-offer.md](../templates/shift-offer.md) §1a (primitive vs shift; three questions).

**Primitive (mechanism):** split → wholeness. Two sides of one coin (C01E01): inner and outer are two windows on one reality.
**Deeper candidate (universal):** separateness → belonging. From "I am a random speck in a dead mechanical universe, meaning is private" to "I am part of a living, conscious whole, and science does not contradict it".

**The shift, three questions (draft):**
1. **Now I can DO:** use what I know inside together with what the evidence shows when I decide; speak about my inner experiences openly; enter expanded states on purpose and come back grounded.
