## The core

Every workstream cycle is two interlocking triangles: the merkaba, the hexagon, the octahedron seen from above. (It is also the Evolver logo.) One triangle is feminine: nothing needs doing, it unfolds by itself. The other is masculine: it arrives through doing, effort, and the direction of focused energy. They alternate.

The cycle is seen here through the **numerical prism of 6**, whose template is the hexagon.

## The six steps

| # | Mode | What happens |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | DO | A deepening connection with one's Uniqueness illuminates, through the articulation of new layers of expression. |
| 2 | BE | A new clarity appears that lights up the **fit**. |
| 3 | DO | Energy is packed into **focus**. |
| 4 | BE | A **strategy** takes shape. |
| 5 | DO | Energy goes into massive action and execution and meets reality: new offers, clients, features, campaigns. |
| 6 | BE | Through contact with reality and with others, flows of new life bubble up (information, money, what was created, new structure built) and new potential ripens. |

Then the cycle turns again, starting from a deeper connection with Uniqueness.

## Other prisms of the same cycle

- **Prism 4:** the four seasons of the cycle, the four elements.
- **Prism 6:** the hexagon above (two triangles, DO and BE alternating).
- **Prism 8:** 7 + 1, two squares: the 8 lunar phases used in Equilibrium (Planning, Planting, Clearing, Gathering, Seeing, Leading, Harvesting, Celebrating).

## Whose Uniqueness

The Uniqueness at the center can be a person's, or a product's, or a venture's. A product has its own uniqueness and moves through the same cycles. So the technology applies at every holon: the founder, the product, the company, the network.

## Why it is a phase shift technology

Most people push on BE steps (forcing clarity, forcing strategy, forcing results to arrive) and coast on DO steps. Naming the step a stream is in tells you which kind of energy it wants: effort and direction, or space and receptivity. Matching the energy to the step removes the friction that makes cycles slow, which is what makes the shift unusually rapid.

## Practice

1. At the start of a work window, name the step each stream (and each product) is in.
2. DO steps get focused effort, broken into short concrete actions.
3. BE steps are not forced: prepare the ground (record, listen, create contact with reality and others) and let them unfold.
4. When a cycle closes, return to step 1: what new layer of Uniqueness did this cycle reveal?
