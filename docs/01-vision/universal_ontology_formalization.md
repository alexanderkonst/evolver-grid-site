## How to read

Every step carries a **type** and every claim a **register**.

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🧱 Lego | an existing theorem already formalizes the insight |
| 🔗 Stitch | existing pieces joined through the invariant; nobody joined them because nobody saw it |
| 🌱 New | a claim science doesn't have yet: stated as hypothesis, to be tested |
| ⚖ Postulate | an axiom we choose |

| Register | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [E] | established mathematics or physics |
| [M] | mapping to the ontology (a correspondence, not an identity) |
| [P] | postulate |
| [O] | open |

Recognition is never proof: an [M] line says the ontology *reads* a known structure this way, not that physics has confirmed the ontology.

## Part I. From the invariant to pulse and spin (Steps I–XV)

### Step I. The one invariant · ⚖
![step i](assets/formalization/i-the-one-invariant.png)
- [P] Let 𝓘 be a primary invariant that presupposes no external space, time or coordinates: **g𝓘 = 𝓘 for all g ∈ G**, G the group of admissible changes of description.
- [O] G itself is to be defined.

### Step II. The first distinction · ⚖ (kept open)
![step ii](assets/formalization/ii-the-first-distinction.png)
- [P] A distinction operation **D : 𝓘 ↦ (a, b)**; a and b are roles or aspects of one invariant, not two objects.
- [O] Is D a first principle, or derivable from the structure of 𝓘? Both kept open (see XII–XIII).

### Step III. Space and time arise together · 🌱
![step iii](assets/formalization/iii-space-and-time-arise-together.png)
- On a set X of distinguishable states, two relations: R_s ⊆ X×X (structure: how states relate) and R_t ⊆ X×X (sequence: what follows what).
- [O] Hypothesis: **geometry of spacetime = F(R_s, R_t)**; F to be built. Kin to causal-set approaches [E: the programme exists].

### Step IV. Pulse and spin · 🌱
![step iv](assets/formalization/iv-pulse-and-spin.png)
- [E] Once a geometry exists, any velocity field splits as **v = v_rad + v_tan**.
- [O] Hypothesis: primary dynamics **𝒟 = P ⊕ S** (Pulse, Spin), and both arise from one first distinction. To be derived.

### Step V. The first self-relation · ⚖
![step v](assets/formalization/v-the-first-self-relation.png)
- [P] **ρ : 𝓘 → 𝓘**, an operation, not yet motion in time.
- ρ(𝓘) = 𝓘: no difference arises. ρ(𝓘) ∼ 𝓘 but ρ(𝓘) ≠ 𝓘: identity kept, presentation changed, so unity and duality coexist. Needs a space of inner states (VI).

### Step VI. Change without loss · 🧱
![step vi](assets/formalization/vi-change-without-loss.png)
- [E] States 𝒮, a conserved quantity Q : 𝒮 → 𝒦, a transformation T with **Q(T(s)) = Q(s)** while T(s) ≠ s (a conserved quantity, as energy in a pendulum).
- [M] The invariant is what survives every change, not a frozen object.

### Step VII. The proto-pulse · 🔗
![step vii](assets/formalization/vii-the-proto-pulse.png)
- s₀ →T s₁ →T s₂ → …; indices are order of application, not seconds.
- Definition within the model: **proto-process = difference + order + conservation of the invariant.**

### Step VIII. The proto-spin · 🧱
![step viii](assets/formalization/viii-the-proto-spin.png)
- [E] A three-state cycle T(s₀)=s₁, T(s₁)=s₂, T(s₂)=s₀ gives **T³(s₀) = s₀**: return through change, circulation before geometry.
- [O] Not yet physical spin: angular momentum, vortex, torus need further definitions.

### Step IX. One structure, two readings · 🌱
![step ix](assets/formalization/ix-one-structure-two-readings.png)
- **ℛ = (𝒮, T, Q)**: one relational structure. Read as what exists: spatiality. Read as what happens: temporality.
- [O] First central hypothesis: space and time are two descriptions of ℛ.

### Step X. First exit to physics · 🌱 (programme)
![step x](assets/formalization/x-first-exit-to-physics.png)
- Bridges to build: cycles → circulation; asymmetry → orientation; nesting → field geometry. Tools: graph theory, topological dynamics, differential geometry, gauge fields.
- [O] Seam: 𝒮 already assumes several states. Where does distinguishability itself come from?

### Step XI. Pure oneness stays one · 🧱
![step xi](assets/formalization/xi-pure-oneness-stays-one.png)
- [E] If 𝓘 = {∗}, the only map is T(∗) = ∗, so Tⁿ(∗) = ∗ for all n: no deterministic map of a one-point set creates a second state.
- [M] A difference must already be latent in the One. This opens XII–XIII.

### Step XII. Two roads to duality · ⚖ (two models)
![step xii](assets/formalization/xii-two-roads-to-duality.png)
- Model A (axiomatic): **𝓘 = (U, D)**, unity and the principle of distinction as two aspects. Cost: duality stays an axiom.
- Model B (emergent): **𝓘 ⇝ {s₊, s₋}**, the invariant holds the possibility of two realisations. Cost: the mechanism must be explained.

### Step XIII. One law, two states · 🧱
![step xiii](assets/formalization/xiii-one-law-two-states.png)
- [E] **V(φ) = λ(φ² − a²)²**, λ, a > 0: the law is symmetric under φ ↦ −φ, yet stable states are φ₊ = a, φ₋ = −a. Spontaneous symmetry breaking (magnets, Higgs).
- [M] Unity of law admits duality of realisation: joins models A and B. [O] Origin of the law itself remains.

### Step XIV. Pulse and spin in one number · 🧱
![step xiv](assets/formalization/xiv-pulse-and-spin-in-one-number.png)
- [E] For z = re^{iθ}: **ż = e^{iθ}(ṙ + i r θ̇)**, an exact identity. ṙ = change of scale (Pulse), rθ̇ = rotation of phase (Spin).
- [M] One quantity, two aspects: Source ↔ Spin. [O] The dot is an evolution parameter, not yet physical time.

### Step XV. The breathing circle · 🧱
![step xv](assets/formalization/xv-the-breathing-circle.png)
- [E] **ṙ = αr(1 − r²), θ̇ = ω**, α > 0, ω ≠ 0: a stable limit cycle at r = 1. Inside, motion spirals out; outside, in; on the circle it keeps turning.
- [M] Outflow, inflow and circulation in one law. [O] Does not yet put source and sink in one point, nor make a torus.

## Part II. From the sphere to the Holy Grail (Steps XVI–XX)

### Step XVI. A sphere forces two · 🧱
![step xvi](assets/formalization/xvi-sphere-forces-two.png)
- [E] Poincaré–Hopf: on a closed surface the indices of a vector field's zeros sum to the Euler characteristic χ; χ(S²) = 2. Hairy ball: no nowhere-vanishing tangent field on S².
- [E] Minimal realisation: one source (+1) and one sink (+1); the poles define an axis.
- [M] The «two holes» of §11.1 (white = source, black = sink) are forced by the sphere's topology, not added.

### Step XVII. Two holes in one point = the horn torus · 🧱
![step xvii](assets/formalization/xvii-two-holes-in-one-horn-torus.png)
- [E] The horn torus (R = r) is homeomorphic to S² with two points identified.
- [M] §11.1 «two holes in one point» = identifying the source and sink of XVI. The result is the Platonic horn torus with an infinitely small centre.

### Step XVIII. Only a torus flows forever · 🧱🧱
![step xviii](assets/formalization/xviii-only-torus-flows-forever.png)
- [E] χ(T²) = 0: the torus is the only closed orientable surface carrying a nowhere-vanishing flow.
- [E] Arnold structure theorem (1965–66): steady ideal 3D flows with velocity and vorticity not collinear fill the domain with nested invariant tori; likewise force-free magnetic fields.
- [M] «Constantly and endlessly, everywhere» (Grail note) ⇒ torus. Answers why a torus and not another form.

### Step XIX. One birth of form · 🔗
![step xix](assets/formalization/xix-one-birth-of-form.png)
- [M] Sphere (duality forced, χ = 2) → horn torus (both singular points merged in the centre) → ring torus (centre opens, flow everywhere, χ = 0). Passing through the pinch the flow turns inside out: the glove of the Grail note.
- [O] Which dynamics performs the identification and the opening? Candidate: continuous reconnection at the pinch point, helicity conserved.

### Step XX. The centre of the Grail · 🧱 + 🔗
![step xx](assets/formalization/xx-centre-of-the-grail.png)
- The vision: the lines are two asymptotes falling into infinite attraction; at the limit point they are at once two spheres and crossing/touching.
- [E] Inversion centred at the tangency point sends two tangent spheres to two parallel planes; parallel planes meet only at infinity, which inversion folds into that one point. So «touch» and «cross» coincide at the limit point.
- [E] Point-dipole field lines all pass through the centre, mutually tangent there. [M] = §11.1, two holes in one.
- [M] Physical regularisation: with finite size the point becomes a thin current sheet (Dungey 1953, Syrovatskii 1971) with four outflow wedges (Petschek 1964) = the four corner bundles of the drawing. Ideal = the point; physical = the sheet.
- 🌱 Kept only for dynamics: how the pulse moves between the ideal and the physical form.
