## The offer copy (v1.1, as on the site)

My proposal is simple: in one month, you go from "what is my real offer?" to selling your packaged signature offer to real people.

As before, the secret sauce is you :) And how we move swiftly yet with care is this combo: my lived experience + the AI workflows you saw in our session + my guidance.

**Week 1: ONE DIRECTION**

We take the clarity from our session and your new insights to the next level of resolution and choose your ONE next direction. No more hundred paths. By the end of the week you have your business strategy, concrete enough to build on: the essence of your unique business, with the key decisions made. We also set up your working rhythm: entrepreneurial action balanced with real rest and integration, so the flywheel keeps spinning without me.

**Week 2: THE OFFER, PACKAGED**

We turn the direction into the thing itself: your signature offer, packaged and priced, and the value ladder around it. Anchored in your uniqueness, you'll know what's clear signal and what's distraction, and the AI workflows carry the heavy lifting. This is the part that was borderline impossible going solopreneur.

**Week 3: YOUR WORDS, YOUR STAGE**

We give the offer its words: the bio people actually get, a landing page that sells, your channels chosen. By the end of Week 3 you can get on a call or a webinar with no script and fill 60 minutes from your mind, your spirit, your heart, your experiences. Because everything we built is from you.

**Week 4: SERVING REAL PEOPLE**

Your business touches the real world. We prep your first client sessions, run the first manual sales moves together, and set up the feedback loop that turns every conversation into a product improvement. By the end of the month, or within two weeks after, you have your first purchase: someone paying for what you uniquely give. No more spinning wheels: continuous motion, with a direction. Which essentially is a running business.

**Format**

- Two 90-minute workshops every week: one on building the business, one on building the mindset, which in essence activates the bigger version of you that's ready for the business to grow.
- check-ins through chat between sessions
- one clear expected result per week

**Price**

This container is the direct continuation of the “Founder-Market Fit” product. It's a cohort program where we build together as a group, or it can be delivered individually too (seats are limited, naturally).

- **In a group: $1,111.** Button: WhatsApp message to Sasha.
- **Individually, with Sasha: $4,444.** Button: "Check eligibility (3 mins)", linking to `/quiz`.

**What I promise**

To be clear about what I promise: one direction, your unique offer packaged and priced, and at least one real purchase of it. I'm not promising rapid riches and I'm not selling a scheme. This is sustainable business and wealth creation, aligned with who you are.

**The guarantee**

Deliver your offer to at least 5 people (5 individual sessions). If within two weeks after the program ends, or after your 5th session, whichever comes later, there is still no purchase, I refund 90% on request. No questions, no forms. A purchase is any amount of money, an in-kind exchange, a money thank-you or a gift of real value; a thank-you in words alone doesn't count. The 10% covers real costs like AI.

## Copy laws this offer obeys

1. **Vector, not self:** the promise is one direction chosen and sold, not more self-knowledge.
2. **Being-wanted register (Step 0.5):** timing facts stated plainly (cohort cadence), price held, no apology, no luring anchors.
3. **Stage-matched money door:** price integrity kept, never a discount. (v1.0 offered a 2x $600 split as cash-flow shape for survival-stage buyers; the v1.1 page lists the two prices only.)
4. **One result per week:** each week ends in something the client can hold.
5. **No overpromise, named concretely (2026-11-18):** the result is at least one purchase of the client's unique product: the honest floor, not the dream ceiling. The guarantee (90% back on request, conditions above) stands behind that floor.

### Why it works (the laws it obeys)

1. **Answer the exact question shape.** She asked started → built → outcome. Two cases, that shape, no third.
2. **Client words carry the proof.** Verbatim transcript quotes, not paraphrase. The "random hypotheses" and "strategic corridor" lines are Sergey's own.
3. **Build-on-top dissolves the real fear.** Nobody starts from scratch; the existing business is kept, the aligned parts grow, the rest falls away on its own.
4. **Transcripts-as-testimonials is the epistemic asset.** The /ignite link answers social proof structurally; every future prospect gets the same link.
5. **The promise paragraph replaces the guarantee conversation.** Deliverables promised plainly; no rapid riches, no scheme, results come from the client working it. Refunds never mentioned, no doors opened to relitigating "how fully we got there."
6. **Ends open-palmed.** "Lmk if you have any other questions!" — unattached, no chase.
